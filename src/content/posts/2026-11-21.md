  <p class="paper-original-title">VisAlloc: Allocating Visual Supervision for On-Policy Self-Distillation</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Haoran Xu、Tian Chen、Yuhe Wu、Yanbo Zhao、Junchi Liao、Hongyu Wang 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">策略上的自我消化(OPSD)为改进多式大语言模型(MLLMs)的视觉推理提供了一个很有希望的方法. 然而,并非所有样本都同样受益于优雅的视觉指导,统一的象征性监督可以稀释视基信号。 我们提出VisAlloc,一个双层框架,将样本级视觉过滤和符号级反事实再加权相结合. 固定成对的教师过滤器保留了以证据为主的正作物的样本,但错误地保留了以空间为转移的负作物。 在象征层面,EMA的老师都得分相同的学生生成的响应,他们的绝对对数概率差异度量查看敏感性. 额外监督目标</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) offers a promising approach to improving visual reasoning in multimodal large language models (MLLMs). However, not all samples benefit equally from privileged visual guidance, and uniform token supervision can dilute visually grounded signals. We propose VisAlloc, a dual-level framework combining sample-level visual filtering with token-level counterfactual reweighting. A fixed paired-teacher filter retains samples answered correctly from an evidence-centered positive crop but incorrectly from a spatially displaced negative crop. At the token level, both EMA teachers score the same student-generated response, and their absolute log-probability differences measure view sensitivity. Additional supervision targets tokens admitted by sensitivity or teacher-distribution disagreement, with shared frozen-prior, template-bigram, and student--teacher divergence gates. Inverse-frequency redistribution preserves the total additional weight within each micro-batch, while the positive teacher remains the sole distillation target. Inference requires only the full-image student. Across eight benchmarks, our 4B and 9B models achieve macro-averages of 81.44 and 84.92, outperforming Vision-OPD reproductions by 0.97 and 0.52 points, respectively. Code on GitHub: https://github.com/Kaelyn01/VisAlloc</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26239" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">教授远景-语言-行动模态以安全制止</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Teaching Vision-Language-Action Models to Stop Safely</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Zheyuan Liu、Chuhan Wang、Jinduo Guo、Yue Huang、Xiangchi Yuan、Ningshan Ma 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">Vision-language-Action(VLA)模型使机器人能够从视觉观测和语言指令中完成多种任务. 虽然先前的工作已经探索了任务压制和拒绝,但一旦执行时就停止被禁行为,并控制由此产生的实际反应仍然具有挑战性。 我们的评估表明,在通过执行部分接管一项任务时,压制一项任务的策略仍然能够完成。 即使任务仍未完成,机器人也可能掉下一个被持有的物体,使不完成成为不完整的安全措施. 为了应付这些挑战,我们引进了国家 -- -- 警觉的后退模仿(SAFI),用于在举行活动时提供适当的替代对策的任务。 苏丹武装部队由两只雄鹿组成</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Vision-language-action (VLA) models enable robots to perform diverse tasks from visual observations and language instructions. While prior work has explored task suppression and refusal, stopping prohibited behavior once execution is underway and controlling the resulting physical response remain challenging. Our evaluation reveals that policies which suppress a task from its initial state can still complete it when taking over partway through execution. Even when the task remains unfinished, the robot may drop a held object, making non-completion an incomplete measure of safety. To address these challenges, we introduce State-Aware Fallback Imitation (SAFI) for tasks where holding provides an appropriate alternative response. SAFI comprises two stages, (1) fallback target construction, which assigns stationary arm commands and consistent gripper targets throughout prohibited-task demonstrations, and (2) joint policy adaptation, which learns these targets alongside the original actions of retained tasks. This supervision teaches an explicit alternative response at intermediate execution states while maintaining the robot&#x27;s other skills. Extensive experiments with OpenVLA-OFT across LIBERO and BadLIBERO tasks evaluate prohibited completion, physical incidents, and retained-task performance. On the evaluated LIBERO tasks, SAFI reduces aggregate target success after takeover to 1.3\%, with no recorded physical incidents or action saturation, while preserving high retained-task success.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25257" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Keel: 一个边疆模型通过重写其重量来教导一种视觉语言模型</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Keel: A Frontier Model Teaches a Vision-Language Model by Rewriting Its Weights</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Jiajun Fan、Neil He、Bangji Yang、Qinglong Zheng、Ziwen Wang、Chaoran Cheng 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">边疆模型能否通过重写其分量来修复视觉语言模型(VLM)? 像Quen3.5这样的小推理VLM需要这样的修复:在思维模态中,它们在许多图像问题上陷入了循环,重复到符号限制,花费整个预算,失去答案. 通常的补救方法在解码上起作用,使循环模态保持原样,或者必须被告知要改变什么. 然而因由由由入于分量. Keel是VLM在图像任务上的第一个智能体加权编辑方法,在那里找到并移除. 仅告诉症状,一个编辑器,一个边疆多模态LLM作为智能体,定位病因,编辑体重并测试它们,并用失败普查和仪器显示故障图像以及在哪里</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Can a frontier model repair a vision-language model (VLM) by rewriting its weights? Small reasoning VLMs such as Qwen3.5 need such a repair: in thinking mode they fall into loops on many image questions, repeating themselves until the token limit, spending the whole budget and losing the answer. The usual remedies act on the decoding, leaving the looping model as it was, or must be told what to change. Yet the cause is written in the weights. Keel, the first agentic weight-editing method for VLMs on image tasks, finds and removes it there. Told only the symptom, an editor, a frontier multimodal LLM acting as an agent, locates the cause, edits the weights and tests them, with a failure census and instruments showing it the failing images and where the VLM attends; among several editors, the harness keeps only edits that clearly reduce the failure without lowering accuracy on questions no editor saw. The repair needs no training, keeps the model&#x27;s size and per-token cost, and applies to already-edited weights. The editors expose where the loops live: from the original weights, independent editors on both models trace them to the attention outputs of the middle layers, and the smaller VLM stops consulting the image inside a loop. On Qwen3.5-0.8B and Qwen3.5-2B, across six image benchmarks from visual mathematics to GUI grounding, the edit removes most loops on every benchmark and shortens answers, and the edited models are significantly more accurate than the thinking models on average; the larger one gains most on GUI grounding, a task absent from the practice questions. Because the repair lives in the weights, it combines with budget forcing where held-out questions favor it: on the best available weights, Keel has the best average accuracy on both models, significantly above every training-free baseline we compare on the larger one.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23875" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">编码智能体对光神经网络进行西图训练</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">In Situ Training of Optical Neural Networks by Coding Agents</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Ilker Oguz、Colin Christian、Christophe Moser、Demetri Psaltis、Gordon Wetzstein</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">光电神经网络保证快速,节能的计算,但每个设备都偏离了它的设计,必须用硬件进行训练. 现场训练依靠专家设计的每个设备的校准数字模型或无梯度试验和出错。 编码智能体,是写作和运行程序的大语言模型,已经表现出了理解和编辑数字神经网络重心的新兴能力. 在这里,我们引入了光学作为代码, 一种方法,一个编码剂从光学神经网络中 单独从测量中训练出来, 通过一个回回其探测器的相机图像, 并且需要每个请求一个假设。 我们发现这个毒剂 形成对光学的物理理解 用i来修复网络</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Optical neural networks promise fast, energy-efficient computing, but every device departs from its design and must be trained with the hardware. In situ training relies on an expert-built calibrated digital model of each device or on gradient-free trial and error. Coding agents, large language models that write and run programs, have shown an emergent ability to understand and edit the weights of digital neural networks. Here we introduce optics as code, a method in which a coding agent trains an optical neural network from measurements alone, through a harness that returns camera images of its probes and requires a hypothesis per request. We discovered that the agent forms a physical understanding of the optics and reparametrizes the network with interpretable optical elements tuned on the hardware. It outperforms gradient-free methods in classification and image encoding, approaching backpropagation through a calibrated model at a fraction of the cost. Needing neither per-device calibration nor gradients through the hardware, and improving with more capable models, optics as code offers a practical route to large, scalable optical neural networks and a general tool for calibrating and optimizing optical experiments.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated exceptional potential in complex reasoning,pioneering a new paradigm for autonomous agent decision making in dynamic settings.   <p class="paper-summary">我们采用了多模态知识智能体基准(MKA),该基准衡量自主智能体如何很好地开展端到端的以视觉理解为基础的专业知识工作。 每个任务都需要一个智能体从多个视觉稠密文件,如CAD蓝图,电子图谱,射电图,复杂图表和地理空间地图等中提取并合并信息,并以.xlsx,.pptx,.pdf,.docx,.html或.png格式制作独立的可交付文件. 现有基准使得这种能力基本上没有经过测试:删除图像查看工具使得双子座4 Carbon在GDPval上的得分保持不变,单格式评价如GDP.pdf和GDP.xlsx不需要在文档或模态上进行推理.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>We introduce the Multimodal Knowledge Agent (MKA) benchmark, which measures how well autonomous agents perform end-to-end professional knowledge work that depends on visual understanding.   <p class="paper-authors">Son Duong、Truong X. Tran、Hai-Anh Tran</p>

  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>The rise of powerful, fine-tuned large language models (LLMs) necessitates robust natural language inference (NLI) systems. These NLI systems ideally train across various tasks, encompassing diverse concepts like sentiment analysis, news classification, and coreference resolution. However, this continual, multi-tasking approach can lead to catastrophic forgetting, where previously learned tasks are lost, especially in domains lacking specific labels. Rehearsing past examples with labels can further exacerbate this issue by causing overfitting, hindering the system&#x27;s ability to classify new categories. To tackle these challenges, this research proposes ICARE, a novel framework with two key components: an instance selection module and a knowledge distillation method. The former is based on representation learning, which meticulously selects training examples to present to the NLI system. The latter, knowledge distillation with feature and logit-level transfer, facilitates knowledge transfer between tasks. This leverages pruning techniques and optimized objective functions to transfer both essential feature-level and decision-making (logit-level) knowledge, mitigating catastrophic forgetting while adapting to new patterns. Extensive evaluations on benchmark text classification datasets have demonstrated ICARE&#x27;s effectiveness. The framework achieves a significant improvement in target accuracy and reduction in the ``forgetting metric&#x27;&#x27;. These results solidify ICARE&#x27;s ability to preserve and leverage learned knowledge across diverse tasks, ultimately enhancing the adaptability and performance of NLI systems.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Converting complex documents into structured knowledge constitutes the critical infrastructure urgently required for deploying Large Language Models (LLMs) in downstream research.   <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Do standard benchmarks see the architectural differences that matter? We study it with 64 from-scratch pretraining runs of Transformers and three linear-model families at 1.3-8B parameters on 1-2T Nemotron-CC tokens, at a cost of roughly 100k USD per 8B run.   <p class="paper-summary">贝叶斯优化(BO)在事先了解这一问题后,可以大大提高样本效率. 然而,在实践中,这种知识往往仅以自然语言提供,例如专家直觉或文件,而不是作为先前指导的BO方法可能要求的明确概率分布面貌。 有效地利用它既需要从语言到前科的表达式绘图,又需要强健到可能误导的信息。 我们提议HapBO (Harnesed $\ alpha$-$\pi$ Bayesian Optimization),它从人类专家,大语言模型(LLM),或两者中引出过一次信念,并且确定性地将结构化的规格汇编成一个可重复使用的信仰函数(\pi:\ mathcal\\\\\去[0,1]).</p>

  <p class="paper-summary">对时间序列的学习和推论可能构成重大的统计挑战,包括自动相关观测,随时间推移的分布变化,以及维度的诅咒. 这些挑战使因果发现算法的实际应用复杂化了,后者在一般情况下已经遭受了高度的计算和样本复杂性. 为缓解这些问题,本作引入了&quot;时间性父母发现&quot;(ITPD):一个健全而完整的基于约束的程序,用于在多变量时间序列上进行因果结构学习,并具有因果不相容性. ITPD采用局部到全球的学习方法,通过相继地发现每个变量的母集来发现全局图. 信息技术开发处利用时间结构</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Learning and inference on time series can pose significant statistical challenges, including autocorrelated observations, distribution shift over time, and the curse of dimensionality. These challenges complicate the practical application of causal discovery algorithms, which already suffer from high computational and sample complexity in the general case. To mitigate these issues, this work introduces Iterative Temporal Parent Discovery (ITPD): a sound and complete constraint-based procedure for causal structure learning on multivariate time series with causal nonstationarity. ITPD takes a local-to-global learning approach that discovers the global graph by sequentially discovering the parent set for each variable. ITPD exploits temporal structural priors, graph partitioning, and the local Markov condition to swap higher-order conditional independence tests for lower-order alternatives. With an exact oracle, ITPD performed the least number of tests and used the smallest average conditioning set sizes relative to baselines. In benchmarking simulations, ITPD sat on the Pareto frontier with respect to F1 and wall-clock time and was faster than baselines on large graphs (geq 1000 nodes). For graphs with 2000 nodes, ITPD ran 2.3times, 9.1times, and 40.9times faster than iterative IAMB, DF-PC, and PCMCI, respectively.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Closing the Analyze-to-Design Gap: An Authorization-Bounded Agent for Self-Driving Laboratories</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Yimeng Liu、Eric Isbrandt、Felix Katzenburg、Kevin P. Greenman、Han Hao、Yang Cao 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">人工智能(AI)剂被日益融入自驾实验室(SDLs)来计划实验和解释结果. 然而,仅分析推理并不能使实验室自主:只有在可以信任智能体在无监督的物理硬件上行事,并且知道何时服从人类的情况下,SDL中的自主才能实现. 在此,我们提出一个架构,将设计-Make-Test-Analysis(DMTA)周期的分析-设计接口中的一个智能体整合起来,使智能体驱动的测量选择和数据解释成为可能,同时确保改变状态的操作得到授权、验证和可审计。 我们在液相色谱-质谱仪上演示这种结构</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Artificial intelligence (AI) agents are increasingly integrated into self-driving laboratories (SDLs) to plan experiments and interpret results. However, analytical reasoning alone does not make a laboratory autonomous: autonomy in an SDL is achievable only when the agent can be trusted to act on physical hardware unsupervised and knows when to defer to a human.   <p class="paper-summary">科学发现需要根据新的证据重新审视这些假设,并找出超出已经探索的有希望的方向。 然而,研究人员常常提出其观察所不支持的主张,探索狭义的假说空间,尽管承认了相冲突的证据,但仍保留信仰. 这些失败促使人们寻找一种明确的认识状态,既捕捉对现有假设的信念,又捕捉进一步探索的方向。 为此,我们提出Epistemic Compass(EpiCompass),通过连接假说,证据,信仰,和修改的感知树来维持这种状态的插座和游戏脚手架. 随着调查的进展,EpiCompass重新评估了所探讨的假设。</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Scientific discovery requires revisiting the hypotheses in light of new evidence and identifying promising directions beyond those already explored. However, research agents often make claims that are not supported by their observations, explore a narrow hypothesis space, and retain beliefs despite recognizing conflicting evidence. These failures motivate the search for an explicit epistemic state that captures both beliefs about existing hypotheses and directions for further exploration. To this end, we propose Epistemic Compass (EpiCompass), a plug-and-play scaffold that maintains this state through an epistemic tree linking hypotheses, evidence, beliefs, and revisions. As an investigation progresses, EpiCompass reassesses the hypotheses explored against accumulated evidence and uses unresolved conflicts and unexplored directions to guide hypothesis generation. It selects candidates by anticipated epistemic gain, accounting for both refinement of existing hypotheses and expansion of the hypothesis space, and verifies the final conclusion against the accumulated evidence. Experimental results across three scientific discovery benchmarks and three LLM backbones demonstrate that EpiCompass consistently outperforms baselines, with further analyses showing reductions in errors of unsupported claims and failures to revise beliefs.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25325" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Pandora 工具箱或智能体安全为何无法验证</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Pandora&#x27;s Toolbox Or Why Agent Safety Cannot Be Verified</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Mario Franco、Carlos Gershenson</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">目前的人工智能智能体是通过固定语言模型与一般计算设备相接来构建的. 我们显示,这种接合使得安全是无法确定的。 特别是,我们显示,鉴于一些危险的程序,任何算法都无法预测一个特工是否会运行这样一个在模拟上等同的程序. 一般来说,护卫铁路,黑名单,白名单和认证只能导致不完善的安全解决方案. 在有限的限制下(有限度的上下文、价值和运行时间),安全是可以确定的,但仍然难以解决。 具体来说, 问题在于 $\ mathsf{ PSPACE} $ - 完成, 即使对智能体的描述已知 。 最后,安全不是构成财产,即可以证明的物剂。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Present artificial intelligence agents are constructed by means of a coupling between a fixed language model with a general computing device. We show that this coupling makes safety undecidable. In particular, we show that given some dangerous program, no algorithm can predict whether an agent will run such a program that is semantically equivalent. In general, guardrails, blacklists, whitelists and certifications can only lead to imperfect safety solutions. Under finite constraints (bounded context, values and running time) safety is decidable but still intractable. Specifically, the problem is $\mathsf{PSPACE}$-complete even when the description of the agent is known. Finally, safety is not a compositional property, i.e., agents that can be proven safe on their own can still produce unsafe behavior when grouped together. We also discuss relaxed notions of safety that could be used in practice.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25357" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Pro$^2$智能体:自主AI研发的科学问题测试</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Pro$^2$ Agent: Scientific Problem Probing for Autonomous AI R&amp;D</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Yixiong Chen、Liqian Peng、Tian Tan、Ziqian Lin、Blake Jianhang Chen、Chao Zhao 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大语言模型(LLM)智能体现在可以通过写入代码和运行实验,自主地进行AI研究. 它们通常通过迭代优化一个代码库来实现,同时忽略任务背后的问题结构. 这种提法对于更简单的任务是有效的,但是对于困难的问题,它常常被卡在错误的解决方案区域,因为智能体从来没有明确地代表它对这个问题的理解. 我们引入了Pro$^2 Agent, 将自主AI研究的初级状态从代码基态转移到了问题国家:一个明确的结构,说明哪些问题妨碍成功,哪些解决办法可能解决这些问题。 Pro$^2 智能体通过综合分析现有资源,初始化了问题国家</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language model (LLM) agents can now autonomously conduct AI research by writing code and running experiments. They typically do so by iteratively optimizing a codebase, while ignoring the problem structure behind the task. This formulation is effective on simpler tasks, but on difficult problems it often gets stuck in the wrong solution region because the agent never explicitly represents its understanding of the problem. We introduce Pro$^2$ Agent, which shifts the primary state of autonomous AI research from a codebase state to a Problem State: an explicit structure of what problems prevent success and which solutions are likely to address them. Pro$^2$ Agent initializes the Problem State through comprehensive analysis of available resources and constraints. It then performs targeted research to relate the identified problems to candidate solutions. Through controlled hypothesis testing, the agent updates the importance of a suspected problem or the benefit brought by a candidate solution. On the complete MLE-Bench, Pro$^2$ Agent matches the best reported medal rate (70.7%) under the standard 24-hour budget and reaches 76.0% with a 48-hour budget, the highest medal rate reported on the complete benchmark. It also achieves competitive performance on PostTrainBench. In addition, it produces research notes for each task that make the research process transparent and reusable by human researchers.</p></details>
