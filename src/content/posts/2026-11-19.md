  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Humans consistently outperform state-of-the-art AI models in action recognition, particularly in challenging real-world conditions involving low resolution, occlusion, and visual clutter.e those utilised by humans Understanding the sources of this performance gap is essential for developing more robust and human-aligned models. In this paper, we present a large-scale human AI comparative study of egocentric action recognition using human-defined Minimal Identifiable Recognition Crops (MIRCs), defined as the smallest spatial or spatiotemporal regions sufficient for reliable human recognition. We used our previously introduced, Epic-ReduAct, a systematically spatially reduced and temporally scrambled dataset derived from 36 EPIC-KITCHENS videos, spanning multiple spatial reduction levels and temporal conditions. Our analysis combines quantitative metrics, Average Reduction Rate and Recognition Gap, with qualitative analyses of spatial (high-, mid-, and low-level visual features) and spatiotemporal factors, including a categorisation of actions into Low Temporal Actions (LTA) and High Temporal Actions (HTA). Results show that human performance exhibits sharp declines when transitioning from MIRCs to sub-MIRCs, reflecting a strong reliance on sparse, semantically critical cues such as hand object interactions. In contrast, the model degrades more gradually and often relies on contextual and mid- to low-level features, sometimes even exhibiting increased confidence under spatial reduction. Temporally, humans remain robust to scrambling when key spatial cues are preserved, whereas the model often shows insensitivity to temporal disruption, revealing class-dependent temporal sensitivities. Overall, our findings demonstrate that strong benchmark performance can obscure fundamental human AI misalignment and highlight limitations in current spatial and spatiotemporal representations, suggesting concrete directions for more efficient and human-aligned action recognition systems.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly being explored for knowledge-intensive and safety-critical applications in nuclear engineering. However, existing nuclear-domain benchmarks provide limited content coverage or focus primarily on answer accuracy without systematically evaluating the quality of the underlying reasoning. This study introduces NEBench R1, a reasoning-oriented benchmark comprising 7,546 unique multiple-choice questions extracted from U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) reactor operator licensing examinations covering 18 nuclear facilities. Each question is classified according to the NRC Knowledge and Abilities catalogs and accompanied by an official explanation serving as a reference for reasoning evaluation. Six LLMs were evaluated, with overall accuracy ranging from 34.0% to 73.9% across three distinct performance tiers. To assess reasoning quality, an LLM-based judge evaluated correctly answered responses using a multidimensional rubric. Although responses generally demonstrated strong problem comprehension, they scored lower in factual and procedural accuracy and distractor analysis, indicating that correct answers were not necessarily supported by technically sound justifications. Response clarity was also weakly indicative of technical quality. Furthermore, longer reasoning traces were associated with lower accuracy in both frontier reasoning models, suggesting that reasoning length may reflect question difficulty rather than improved problem-solving performance. Reasoning-token consumption may therefore provide an additional signal for identifying potentially unreliable responses. NEBench R1 establishes a benchmark for evaluating both answer accuracy and reasoning quality, providing a foundation for assessing the domain competence and reliability of LLMs in nuclear engineering.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Thinking Harder Doesn&#x27;t Lead to Better Choices: Reasoning Effort Increases the Confidence but Not the Correctness of LLM Shopping Agents</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Adi Krishnamoorthy、X. Y. Han</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大语言模型(LLMS)被越来越多地用作自主购物智能体商,处理多式产品上市(由视觉和文字数据组成)并代表消费者推荐购买. 自然,人们可以假定,具有更大思维力的模型将作出质量更高的采购决定。 为了检验这种信念,我们提出了两个关于这种智能体的问题:问一个模型更难思考是否使它选择更好的产品,它报告的信任是否告诉我们它可能是错误的? 我们以WebShop、Amazon的SQID和Wayfair的WANDS平台所构建的三种产品选择基准为基准, 亚军</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as autonomous shopping agents that process multimodal product listings (consisting of visual and text data) and recommend purchases on behalf of consumers. Naturally, one might assume that models with more thinking power would make higher-quality purchasing decisions. To test this belief, we ask two questions about such agents: does asking a model to think harder make it choose better products, and does the confidence it reports tell us the likelihood it may be wrong? We benchmark ten frontier models on three product-choice benchmarks built from WebShop, Amazon&#x27;s SQID and Wayfair&#x27;s WANDS platforms to see if LLMs with more thinking power actually make better-quality product purchase decisions. Across all three benchmarks, we found that increasing the thinking effort does not increase decision quality in a statistically meaningful way. The same change in thinking effort raises stated confidence at a statistically significant level, so extra thinking buys confidence rather than correctness. We also measure the calibration of these models (how well their reported confidence percentage matches their true correctness percentage) in these tasks and show that most models are not well-calibrated at every effort level. We also find that errors are strongly shared: when one model errs in a task, several others tend to err on that same task. Furthermore, on tasks where several models all err, a large number of them pick the same wrong product.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">语言模型可以重写自己的重量</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Jiajun Fan、Neil He、Changyi Yang、Andy Mao、Xi Zhu、Chaoran Cheng 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">语言模型经过一次训练、被冻结和部署:一次改进一次又需要一次训练,一旦任务结束,即失去一个即时、脚手架或额外计算。 已经显示一个边框模型来编辑一个小得多的边框的权重. 我们问一个模型是否可以重写自己的分量来让自己变得更好,发现它可以通过自己的推理,没有梯度,也没有训练;我们称之为这种自重. 鉴于其重量,分级答案和自身运行能力,一个27B模型诊断其失败并编辑其背后的分量. 它超越自身:它自己的编辑使其在490个它从未见过的问题上更准确6.6分,在预先固定的测试中,另一个会话的编辑也使它变得显著.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Language models are trained once, frozen and deployed: improving one again takes another training run, and a prompt, a scaffold or extra computation at inference is gone once the task ends. A frontier model has been shown to edit the weights of a much smaller one. We ask whether a model can rewrite its own weights to make itself better, and find that it can, by its own reasoning, with no gradients and no training; we call this self-rewriting. Given its weights, graded answers and the ability to run itself, a 27B model diagnoses its failure and edits the weights behind it. It surpasses itself: its own edit makes it 6.6 points more accurate on 490 problems it never saw, in a test fixed in advance, and another session&#x27;s edit also makes it significantly better. It keeps getting better: it submits an edit only when its own tests support it, each edit replacing the last, and over its first eight such sessions its accuracy only rises. Unlike test-time scaling, the gain persists in its weights and adds no cost per answer; unlike training, it needs no pre- or post-training infrastructure, only an open model and hours on one GPU. It repairs a damaged copy of itself: it locates the corrupted matrix and rewrites it with values of its own, never reading the undamaged weights; found on text math problems alone, the repair lifts the copy on six image benchmarks it never saw from 7% to 70% accuracy, 83% of the undamaged model&#x27;s. And the ability emerges with scale: across four sizes of one family, the 2B model makes no edit and the 4B and 9B models break themselves; only the 27B model works on itself as a human would, comparing its hidden states on answers that finish and on answers that run out of tokens, weakening the direction that separates them. Like a human, a model need not separate training from inference; improving a language model may come to take a conversation rather than a training run.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21669" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Token 承诺作为引导蒙面扩散生成的控制表面</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Token Commitment as a Control Surface for Steering Masked Diffusion Generation</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Ambroise Aigueperse、Hugo Buurmeijer、Carmen Amo ALonso、Marco Pavone</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">蒙面扩散模型在采样者决定哪些预测成为永久预测之前,反复地预测了临时信号. 现有的推论时间引导方法主要修改活性化,对数化,或去诺化轨迹,同时将这一承诺策略视为固定的解码细节. 相反,我们研究象征性承诺,将其作为一个针对扩散的控制面,并将其分为两个决定:承诺的象征和每一步骤承诺的象征。 首先,我们引入了反事实目标意识承诺,该承诺通过模态信任和一脚不动目标贡献的混合方式,对未解决的立场进行排序。 第二,我们采用目标进展和信心驱动的调整适应预算。 横跨伊恩</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Masked diffusion models repeatedly predict provisional tokens before a sampler decides which predictions become permanent. Existing inference-time steering methods primarily modify activations, logits, or denoising trajectories, while treating this commitment policy as a fixed decoding detail. We instead study token commitment as a diffusion-specific control surface and separate it into two decisions: which tokens to commit and how many tokens to commit at each step. For the first, we introduce counterfactual target-aware commitment, which ranks unresolved positions by a mixture of model confidence and leave-one-token-out target contribution. For the second, we introduce calibrated adaptive budgets driven by target progress and confidence. Across language and vision-language settings in the LLaDA family, multiple targets, two activation-steering methods, and an unsteered control, both mechanisms yield empirically monotonic target-quality trade-offs in every configuration we evaluate. Adaptive schedules also recover competitive operating points relative to scorer-free stochastic schedules matched in average denoising cost. Because both mechanisms require only a scalar score evaluated on a provisional completion, they apply unchanged when the target is an automatic proxy for task success rather than an output attribute: NLI entailment for dialogue summarization and CLIPScore for captioning, neither of which corresponds to a single reusable steering direction. We reproduce the behavior of both mechanisms on a second masked-diffusion family, Dream-7B, for one control target and one task-success target. These results characterize commitment--which provisional predictions become irreversible, and how rapidly--as an interpretable control surface for steering masked diffusion generation under the conditions we test.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Sheng Lu、Guangpin Tao、Tong Lu、Ying Tai</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">通过合成像素,勾画相隔的思绪链内的迷宫路径,或按框架推出视频解决方案框架,多模态模型越来越有道理。 每个视觉思想都运行着从光现实主义合成中继承下来的全部传播时间表,因此迭代去诺能支配推论成本. 我们研究如何按照两个轴线分配测试时间计算:何时提出视觉思维,而不是文字思考或直接回答,以及每个思维需要多少去名步骤. 控制下的干预,只有不同的思维方式才能揭示出三种制度。 一场8步的草图 几乎匹配50步的精度 承诺执行同样的计划</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Multimodal models increasingly reason by synthesizing pixels, sketching maze paths within interleaved chains of thought or rolling out video solutions frame by frame. Each visual thought runs the full diffusion schedule inherited from photorealistic synthesis, so iterative denoising dominates inference cost. We study how to allocate test-time compute along two axes: when to render visual thoughts rather than thinking in text or answering directly, and how many denoising steps each thought requires. Controlled interventions that vary only how the thought is rendered uncover three regimes. Causal thoughts carry the plan and become legible early: an 8-step sketch nearly matches a 50-step render in accuracy, commits to the same plan as often as two independent full renders agree, and in video models mostly fails by stalling short of the goal, so the rollout abstains. Decorative thoughts can be omitted without loss, whereas harmful thoughts lower accuracy. Because the regime depends on both model and task, always drawing is suboptimal. Across 14 model-benchmark pairs of three interleaved reasoners, answering directly, without any thinking, wins 9 pairs, and choosing among direct, text-only, and visual tiers from ten labeled examples per pair raises mean accuracy from 60.7% (always drawing) to 65.1%. Where drawing pays, we propose sketch-and-vote, which samples 8-step sketches (a step count chosen without labels) from independent noises until one answer reaches three votes. With this one setting, sketch-and-vote beats full rendering with fewer denoising steps on an image reasoner and two video reasoners, by 8.6 points on VSP spatial planning and by 9.6 and 22.1 points on mazes.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">游戏是对视觉特务的丰富考验,要求感知,长相规划,精确控制. 然而,收集人类游戏游戏很昂贵,从游戏游戏视频中学习需要推断缺失的动作. 现有的数据集也涵盖有限的游戏范围,通常没有明确的任务或可核实的结果. 为了弥补这些差距,我们引入了Game2Player,这个框架共同开发游戏环境和玩家,以收集规模上多样和可验证的游戏游戏体验. 在Game2Player循环中,一个Builder智能体修改了已存在的网络游戏,暴露了其内部状态,并用基于状态的进步措施和成功检查来定义任务. 编码播放器和 VLM 播放器然后尝试任务并向您报告问题</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Games are a rich testbed for visual agents, demanding perception, long-horizon planning, and precise control. However, collecting human gameplay is costly, and learning from gameplay videos requires inferring missing actions. Existing datasets also cover a limited range of games, often without explicit tasks or verifiable outcomes. To address these gaps, we introduce Game2Player, a framework that co-develops game environments and players to collect diverse and verifiable gameplay experience at scale. In the Game2Player loop, a Builder agent adapts existing web games, exposes their internal state, and defines tasks with state-based progress measures and success checks. A Coding Player and a VLM Player then attempt the tasks and report problems to the Builder for repair, so that play serves as both environment validation and training data collection. With Game2Player, we build 568 verified web games with 3,436 tasks, collect 15.7K trajectories (307 hours of gameplay), and hold out 50 games as the benchmark PlayerBench. To evaluate the collected experience as training data, we train VLM agents with progress-weighted SFT, assigning greater weight to steps that advance task progress. Game2Player-9B improves progress from 14.3% to 31.2% on PlayerBench and from 30.0% to 53.1% on the external GameWorld benchmark.   <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21799" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">思维控制链可减少思维控制链可监测性</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Chain-of-Thought Controllability Can Reduce Chain-of-Thought Monitorability</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Zheng-Xin Yong、Benjamin Arnav、Miles Wang、Tomek Korbak、Olivia Watkins</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">思维链(CET)监测是检测隐藏目标,任务外行为,以及AI智能体中其他故障的有希望的工具. 一种风险是模型可以刻意地塑造它们的推理,使监视器不透明. 我们通过个人控制训练来测试这种风险: 监督地微调数学推理痕迹 重写在不同的人的声音中。 尽管训练中没有显示器回避的例子,但是这促使控制通信设备的能力更加广泛。 在隐蔽的侧式任务评价中,Gpt-oss-20b模型活体完成隐藏侧式任务的痕迹中包含的可监测性比基模型更差的证据. 按副任务-成功计数加权的评价和综合技术网长度平均数,综合技术网监测器</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Chain-of-thought (CoT) monitoring is a promising tool for detecting hidden goals, off-task behavior, and other failures in AI agents. One risk is that models could deliberately shape their reasoning to make it opaque to monitors. We stress-test this risk through persona-control training: supervised fine-tuning on mathematical reasoning traces rewritten in the voices of diverse personas. This induces a broader ability to control CoT, even though training contains no monitor-evasion examples. In covert side-task evaluations, traces in which a gpt-oss-20b model organism completes the hidden side task contain less monitorable evidence than the base model&#x27;s. Averaged across evaluations and CoT lengths, weighted by side-task-success counts, the CoT monitorability metric g-mean$^2$ falls from 0.94 for the base model to 0.68 for the model organism without a hiding instruction, and to 0.22 when explicitly instructed to hide. In intervention evaluations, explicit hiding often drives monitorability near zero. The effect extends to a Kimi-K2.6 model organism in agentic environments, though hidden objectives requiring extended reasoning or longer trajectories remain more monitorable than simple single-step side tasks. We further discover that targeted transparency prompts can substantially recover monitorability, sometimes surpassing the base model. These model organisms provide a testbed for studying monitorability failures and potential mitigations. Our code can be found on https://github.com/yongzx/cot-control-model-organism.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21925" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DNeo: 您的并行模型草案需要更好的目标特性编码</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">DNeo: Your Parallel Draft Model Needs Better Target Feature Encoding</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Jameson Sandler、Shane Bergsma、Mostafa Elhoushi、Bin Claire Zhang、Gavia Gray、Joel Hestness 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">分光-分光分解解码使用一个小的并列起草器模型在一个前传生成b草稿令牌,然后通过更大的目标模型进行平行验证. 当多个词符同时通过验证时,可以实现更快的推论. 最近并行的草稿架构(DFlash,DSpark)通过一个称为特性编码的机制,通过在目标所计算的丰富地物上进行调制来提高草稿质量. 然而,标准特征编码机制有三个限制:(1) 存在丢失,通过一种被称为剩余混合的已发现效应,应用对基础目标特征-空间具有破坏性的编码预测;(2) 它们是无记忆的,没有以前处理的建议书的记录.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Speculative-diffusion decoding uses a small parallel drafter model to generate b draft tokens in one forward pass, which then undergo parallel verification via a larger target model. Faster inference can be achieved when multiple draft tokens pass verification simultaneously. Recent parallel draft architectures (DFlash, DSpark) improve draft quality by conditioning on rich features computed by the target, through a mechanism known as feature encoding. However, standard feature encoding mechanisms have three limitations: (1) they are lossy, applying encoding projections that are destructive to the underlying target feature-space via a discovered effect known as residual mixing, (2) they are memory-less, with no record of previously processed proposals, and (3) they utilize features sampled uniformly from across the target layers, omitting important hidden states. To address these issues, we propose Mixture-of-Features Attention (MoFA), a lightweight feature-encoding approach equipped with a working memory that encodes previously rejected proposals. Leveraging MoFA, rejection memory, and a set of further enhancements, including 2-tap causal convolutions, prefix-only cross-attention, and non-uniform target layer selection, the final architecture, DNeo, achieves 1.3x faster generation speed than SoTA (DFlash-2) without impacting drafter footprint or generation quality. DNeo marks a new state of the art in high quality, fast LLM inference.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">DiscoveryAgent: A Framework for Scientist-centric Agentic AI</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Owen Queen、Ioana Ciuca、Emma Dann、Jean Somalwar、Carine You、Steven Dillmann 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">AI智能体可以协助科学研究,但是在自主调查与科学家检查和调整正在进行的工作方向的能力之间保持平衡仍然是中心设计挑战. 我们介绍发现特工, 一个域-不可知,以科学家为中心的系统, 将自主调查与科学家引导的假说引导相结合。 科学家在研究回合之间选择、完善或取代假设,而审计人员则对检查记录进行询问,以追踪索赔,复制分析,并通报随后的决定。 在两个预测模型编制任务中,发现剂公司在评估的基线中生产出最佳可变星分级器,在科学家的指导下,一个蛋白质变异效应预测器仅被106个蛋白质基团中的一个所超越。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>AI agents can assist scientific research, but balancing autonomous investigation with scientists&#x27; ability to inspect and redirect ongoing work remains a central design challenge. We present DiscoveryAgent, a domain-agnostic, scientist-centric system that combines autonomous investigation with scientist-directed hypothesis steering. Scientists select, refine, or replace hypotheses between research rounds, while audit agents interrogate checkpointed records to trace claims, reproduce analyses, and inform subsequent decisions. Across two predictive modeling tasks, DiscoveryAgent produced the best variable-star classifier among evaluated baselines and, with scientist steering, a protein variant-effect predictor surpassed by only one of 106 ProteinGym baselines. Together, these results show how autonomous research can expand scientists&#x27; empirical reach while centering their authority over research direction and their ability to understand and challenge the evidence.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">CrisisBench: an evaluation suite for crisis counseling AI</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Akshay Swaminathan、Filip Ramazan、Sharang Phadke、Kevina Wang、Ivan Lopez、Shaked Peleg Azzam 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">背景:危机求助热线对心理健康系统至关重要,但往往人手不足,资金不足。 法学硕士可以支持顾问,但现有的基准不能反映危机对话的升级、风险评估和降级,而隐私限制则限制使用真实记录。 方法:我们提出危机奔驰,这是用于单一和多回合危机咨询评估的隐私保护套房。 其中包括从200个解开身份的谈话中得出的1 085个单回合任务和从热线元数据中得出的1 185个病人有限责任网。 病人模型和一名23项LLM法官被校准为专家顾问。 结果: 我们评价了5个GPT模型,加上当时的草稿和检索的生成。 所有系统</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Background: Crisis helplines are essential to mental health systems but are often understaffed and underfunded.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Sycophancy in large language models is often measured as agreement with the user, yet agreement can be appropriate when the user&#x27;s claim is valid, their preference is genuine, or their experience warrants acknowledgment.   <p class="paper-original-title">Every Camera Grows Up: Camera-Height Regression in Interactive Video World Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Sheng Lu、Guangpin Tao、Tong Lu、Ying Tai</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">互动视频世界模型充当了被体现的智能体的模拟器:从一个初始图像和动作轨迹开始,它们会产生智能体将看到的观点. 起步相机会固定相机的高度,例如平面机器人为0.15米,起跑相机为2.2米,忠实的模拟器必须随着相机的移动而保持这个高度. 我们显示,最近有6个开放量级模型未能做到这一点:当运动用生成的内容来取代起始视图时,它们将相机向人眼水平移动,这是我们称之为相机-高下回归的失败. 我们的基准“EyeHeight”为六对高地(0.15米至2.2米)和四对校准的实地世界钻机高地,无尺度多视图高地估计。 6个模特儿都输了</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Interactive video world models serve as simulators for embodied agents: from a starting image and an action trajectory, they generate the views the agent would see. The starting image fixes how high the camera is, for example 0.15 m for a floor robot or 2.2 m for an overhead camera, and a faithful simulator must keep this height as the camera moves. We show that six recent open-weight models fail to do so: as motion replaces the starting view with generated content, they move the camera toward human eye level, a failure we call camera-height regression. Our benchmark, EyeHeight, pairs six rendered elevations (0.15 m to 2.2 m) and four calibrated real-world rig heights with scale-free multi-view height estimation. All six models lose their initial height during a $180^\circ$ turn-and-walk rollout: initialized at 0.15 m, only 0-20% of clips end within $1.3\times$ of the starting height as inferred viewpoints converge to human heights of 1.1 m to 1.5 m, whereas ground-truth renders remain within a few percent. In Matrix-Game models, homography-tracked floor motion stays consistent with constant elevation during rotation even as newly outpainted room geometry yields a higher reconstructed viewpoint. Regression grows with rotation angle, begins while the start frame remains in context, and largely reverses in SANA-WM when a $360^\circ$ turn restores the initial view. We trace this pull to human-centric video priors in which floor-level views are nearly absent: in SANA-WM, textual height cues and dense floor-disparity maps do not prevent it, whereas classifier-free text guidance during the first half of denoising strengthens it. Combining unguided sampling with height-balanced fine-tuning recovers more retention but costs 5 MUSIQ points, a loss fixed during those same early steps. Viewpoint drift alone shifts the paths predicted by visual navigation policies.</p></details>

  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>A grounded reasoning chain cites a frame span and a box for every step, so a reader can check where the model looked. Whether the answer depends on the cited region is a different question. We test it by intervention: the regions a model cited in its own chain are blacked out, and the share of correct answers that flip is compared with blacking out boxes of the same size elsewhere on the same frames. The paired difference, the cite gap, needs no annotation. We apply it to 2-3B video-language models trained on exact CLEVRER chains and on pseudo-labelled NExT-QA chains. Masking the cited entity on every frame, instead of the cited box, flips 1.9 times as many answers as its control. The NExT-QA chains were supervised to cite two frames of entities that stay visible on most frames, so this outcome is what the supervision predicts; the point is that the usual check on grounded chains cannot see it. A citation can be verifiable and relevant without the answer depending on it, and a test of grounded reasoning should remove what a chain names, not only where it points.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21698" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">FACE: mmWave MIMO系统流动导引通道估计框架</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">FACE: Flow-Guided Angular Channel Estimation Framework for mmWave MIMO Systems</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Samitha Gunarathne、Eslam Eldeeb、Nurul Huda Mahmood、Matti Latva-aho</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">获取准确的信道状态信息对于毫米波(mmWave)多输入多输出系统至关重要。 然而,由于高维度通道、有限的试点观测和测量噪音,它仍然具有挑战性。 为了应对这些挑战,我们提议采用流导角通道估计(FACE),这是一个基于流相匹配的新型通道估计框架,是一个新兴的基因模型模型,它学习了概率分布之间的持续迁移动态. FACE利用mmWave通道内在的角-域相接性,并将所学流相接模型作为先基因相接通道. 数值结果显示,FACE一直实现更高的信道估计精度</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>cs.LG</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Acquiring accurate channel state information (CSI) is essential for millimeter-wave (mmWave) multi-input multi-output (MIMO) systems. However, it remains challenging due to high-dimensional channels, limited pilot observations, and measurement noise. To address these challenges, we propose flow-guided angular channel estimation (FACE), a novel channel estimation framework based on flow matching, an emerging generative modeling paradigm that learns continuous transport dynamics between probability distributions. FACE exploits the inherent angular-domain sparsity of mmWave channels and incorporates the learned flow matching model as a generative channel prior. Numerical results show that FACE consistently achieves higher channel estimation accuracy than both conventional estimators and diffusion-based generative estimator across diverse channel and system configurations. Moreover, FACE maintains high estimation accuracy with fewer pilot observations and achieves an average achievable rate close to the perfect-CSI benchmark. Compared with diffusion-based estimation, FACE requires substantially fewer sampling steps while achieving superior or comparable estimation accuracy, reducing inference time and computational cost. Overall, the proposed framework demonstrates the potential of flow matching for accurate, pilot-efficient, and computationally efficient mmWave MIMO channel estimation.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Speculative decoding accelerates vision-language models(VLMs) while preserving the target model&#x27;s outputs, but its speedup diminishes under high concurrency serving as the system becomes more compute bounded as the batch size increases.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Masked diffusion language models (dLLMs) decode by iteratively denoising a full canvas of tokens, so every forward pass runs a per-token sampling and stopping pipeline over the entire canvas-by-vocabulary logits tensor.   <p class="paper-original-title">Bad Apples in the Kitchen: How LLM Teams Detect, Correct, and Calibrate Against Their Misaligned Members</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Dongseok Heo、Junghwan Kim、Bongwon Suh</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">当一个队友悄悄地出事时,有什么能保护LLM团队的? 多数多智能体LLM工作都以合作伙伴为对象,并在行动前检查各轮之间的聊天情况。 实时被包含的合作不允许这种暂停:聊天与动作并列运行,因此一个在行为差异时其言语保持合作的队友会比队伍能够指名道姓地造成更快的破坏. 我们研究这个案例的方法是将三个基于理论的错配剂注入一个自定义的四玩家过量环境,各自强调一个不同的联合动作层(相声-动作不匹配,角色入侵,和集合-进度中断),并通过3x4x2的设计将强度分解为三个相继的检测,校正和校正的门(n=720相声).</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>What protects an LLM team when one teammate quietly goes wrong? Most multi-agent LLM work assumes cooperative partners and inspects chat between turns before acting. Real-time embodied cooperation does not allow this pause: chat runs alongside action, so a teammate whose words stay cooperative while behavior diverges can cause damage faster than the team can name what is happening. We study this case by injecting three theory-grounded misaligned agents into a custom four-player Overcooked environment, each stressing a different joint-action layer (speech-action mismatch, role invasion, and aggregate-progress disruption), and decompose robustness into three sequential gates of detection, correction, and calibration through a 3x4x2 design (n=720 episodes). Nearly all trolls are eventually named, but recovery follows only when attribution and situational diagnosis appear in the same utterance; direct source-attribution prompting improves the hardest case only by degrading the troll-free baseline by 37.5%. Robustness, then, depends less on detection alone than on whether team chat combines attribution with diagnosis in a single utterance, suggesting that sensitivity to misalignment should be targeted rather than uniformly raised.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training (CLIP) achieves impressive zero-shot image classification performance by comparing image embeddings against class-specific text embeddings.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) aims to align augmented views while preserving representation diversity and preventing collapse.   <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23068" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">解析假说:智能体 Evolution 的关键在于识别改进</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">The Resolution Hypothesis: Agent Evolution&#x27;s Key Is Identifying Improvements</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Yuanhao Zeng、Shaocheng Lan、Ao Lu、Kuang Yan、Heng Lin、Xinang Xu 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">智能体进化遵循一个简单的循环:求婚者会写出候选文物(即时,技能,记忆)并选择接受得分较高的文物. 这个循环显示了希望,然而实践者却面临一个谜题:方法贸易在论文之间获胜,进化过程往往不能解决某些任务。 我们把这个谜题追溯到解析假说: 智能体进化的停滞不是因为方法不能提出更好的工艺品,而是因为噪音的评价无法可靠地识别出它们. 在整个7个演化方法和6个模型(超过10M LLM调用)中,我们发现在使用评价分数的两级都支持假设. 在候选一级(选取文物),存在有助候选文物,但选取并不比ra好.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Agent evolution follows a simple loop: a proposer writes candidate artifacts (prompts, skills, memories) and selection accepts those that score higher. This loop has shown promise, yet practitioners face a puzzle: methods trade wins across papers, and evolution often fails to bootstrap on certain tasks. We trace this puzzle to the Resolution Hypothesis: agent evolution stalls not because methods fail to propose better artifacts, but because noisy evaluations cannot reliably identify them. Across seven evolution methods and six models (over 10M LLM calls), we find support for the hypothesis at both levels where evaluation scores are used. At the candidate level (choosing artifacts), helpful candidate artifacts exist, but selection is no better than random at telling them from harmful ones, so errors accumulate across rounds. At the method level (ranking methods), typical margins of a few percentage points are smaller than the difference between two independent runs of the same method, so published rankings often rest on luck. Together, these findings indicate that agent evolution is often bottlenecked by reliably identifying improvements. This calls for a shift in how we develop and evaluate self-improving agents, from designing ever more candidate improvement systems to making true improvements detectable and accumulable.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Latent diffusion models rely on a decoder to map denoised latents to pixels, adding complexity and inference cost.
