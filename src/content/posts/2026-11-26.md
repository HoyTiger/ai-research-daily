  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language models (LLMs) often handle graph-structural information as textual sequences by reasoning over relations in knowledge. As a result, it remains unclear whether LLMs truly capture graph structure in graph reasoning tasks, or whether their predictions are primarily driven by pre-trained knowledge. This raises a question: Do LLMs truly acquire graph reasoning capabilities? In this paper, we reveal that graph reasoning in LLMs is influenced by actual entity information. Specifically, we find that graph reasoning tasks constructed with real-world knowledge encourage models to rely on lexical dependencies rather than the underlying graph structure. To quantify this behavior, we employ a metric, i.e., Expected Fisher Information Trace, for assessing the relative dominance of knowledge recall versus reasoning. Our analysis reveals that removing access to label semantics forces models to undergo parameter updates that are potentially necessary for structural reasoning. These results indicate that while LLMs can acquire graph-structural reasoning capability, such capability emerges most clearly when reliance on memorized label knowledge is reduced.</p></details>

  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.LG</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Reinforcement learning has achieved remarkable success in sequential decision-making, but learning effective policies often requires substantial interaction experience. Model-based reinforcement learning alleviates this dependence by leveraging world models, which are typically used to support planning or policy optimization. In this paper, we investigate an alternative paradigm in which autonomous LLM-based coding agents directly synthesize control policies by reasoning over readable world models. By examining environment source code, these agents translate environmental knowledge into executable decision rules and iteratively refine their policies using online feedback or offline data, without task-specific RL training. Experiments across diverse decision-making benchmarks show that synthesized policies match or outperform conventional RL methods while requiring substantially less experience. Further analyses reveal interpretable decision rules and how they evolve through feedback-driven refinement. Comparisons with policies generated without source code access and experiments under challenging conditions further demonstrate the benefits of readable world models. These findings suggest that direct policy synthesis can reduce reliance on trial and error, challenging the necessity of task-specific RL training.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Med Team、Ming Hu、Cheng Tang、Wei Li、Rongbin Li、Feilong Tang 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">制定医疗多模态大语言模型的有效比例法仍然是一个长期和根本的挑战。 我们的系统评价表明,现有的医疗MLLMs往往在一系列医疗任务中表现不佳,而缩小模型尺寸主要提高文本能力,而不是多模态感知,而使能推理模态未能弥合这一视觉差距。 为了调查医疗多模态情报的实际规模,我们引入了Sage-Med-Preview,这是35B参数医疗多模态,有3B激活参数,通过将3.227亿份特定阶段的考生记录与涵盖医学预训练的渐进训练课程相结合而建立。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>推理</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Establishing effective scaling laws for medical multimodal large language models (MLLMs) remains a persistent and fundamental challenge. Our systematic evaluation shows that existing medical MLLMs tend to underperform frontier general-purpose MLLMs across a range of medical tasks, while scaling model size primarily improves text capabilities rather than multimodal perception, and enabling reasoning modes fails to close this visual gap. To investigate how medical multimodal intelligence scales in practice, we introduce Sage-Med-Preview, a 35B-parameter medical multimodal model with 3B activated parameters, built by combining stage-specific curation over 322.7 million candidate records with a progressive training curriculum spanning medical pretraining, staged supervised fine-tuning, and adaptive reinforcement learning. Although substantial challenges remain in complex medical reasoning and multi-step clinical workflows, this report provides empirical evidence, actionable data and training recipes, and a rigorous benchmark toward scalable clinical multimodal intelligence.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33563" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">FIG-MAC:一个精细的启发图,赋予多智能体协作能力,促进自动科学假设生成</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">FIG-MAC: A Fine-grained Inspiration Graph Empowered Multi-Agent Collaboration for Automated Scientific Hypothesis Generation</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Siyuan Chen、Yuping Gao、Tai Huang、Yuling Zhou、Dongyu Su、Tongguan Wang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">利用大语言模型(LLMS)的自动科学假设生成(ASHG)旨在利用其在知识集成、逻辑推理和文本生成方面的优势,以高效地产生创新的、可核查的科学假设。 然而,现有方法面临三个关键限制:(一) 缺乏精细的知识代表性,因为没有结构化的背景会损害LLM的理解和推理;(二) 缺乏知识演变的可追溯性,限制了创新跨领域猜想的产生;(三) 缺乏由人启发和作用专门化的多智能体协作,未能利用由于认知多样性和专家分工而产生的高效创新机制。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Automated Scientific Hypothesis Generation (ASHG) leveraging large language models (LLMs) aims to harness their strengths in knowledge integration, logical reasoning, and text generation for efficiently producing innovative, verifiable scientific hypotheses. However, existing approaches face three critical limitations: (i) insufficient fine-grained knowledge representation, as unstructured context impairs LLM comprehension and reasoning; (ii) lack of knowledge evolution traceability, constraining the generation of innovative cross-domain conjectures; (iii) absence of human-inspired and role-specialized multi-agent collaboration, failing to leverage the efficient innovation mechanisms arising from cognitive diversity and division of labor among expert roles to complete the full ASHG-evaluation loop. To address these limitations, we propose the Fine-grained Inspiration Graph-empowered Multi-Agent Collaboration framework (FIG-MAC) including: (i) Fine-grained Inspiration Graph module (FIG), to obtain fine-grained structured knowledge representations, where nodes represent academic semantic units and edges encode heuristic relationships among them; (ii) ontology-guided hybrid reasoning module, which follows the FIG ontology to construct structured reasoning paths and uses vector search to enrich them with semantic evidence, to obtain the traceable scientific conjecture paths; (iii) role-specialized Multi-Agent framework, inspired by real scientific teamwork, enabling a closed-loop process of scientific conjecture generation, screening, and validation. Experimental results demonstrate that FIG-MAC significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods on source diversity, provenance-adjusted novelty, and raw novelty.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Agentic LongLive: Asynchronous Agentic Memory for Long Video Generation</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Shaoteng Liu、Shuai Yang、Wei Huang、Luozhou Wang、Song Han、Yukang Chen</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">实时自递式视频生成器 视频流 分钟并接受新的提示 在苍蝇上, 但努力保持人,物品, 和位置一致 在一个长长的互动故事。 Agent systems为离线多镜头一代提供这样的内存,然而他们提前阅读了整个剧本,在生成前计划每场镜头,再生失败镜头,这是一个直播流不允许的. 我们介绍Agentic LongLive, 一种用于长视频生成的同步智能体记忆. 一个在单独的GPU上的内存智能体只读到迄今为止所揭示的提示,从不读出生成的视频,并且为每个人,对象,场景保持一个文本状态和不断发展的图像. 从这个实体内存中,它构成一个全局图像和一个每秒的内存图像。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Real-time autoregressive video generators stream minutes of video and accept new prompts on the fly, but struggle to keep people, objects, and places consistent over a long interactive story. Agent systems provide such memory for offline multi-shot generation, yet they read the whole script in advance, plan each shot before generating it, and regenerate failed shots, which a live stream does not allow. We present Agentic LongLive, an asynchronous agentic memory for long video generation. A memory agent on separate GPUs reads only the prompts revealed so far, never the generated video, and keeps a text state and evolving images for every person, object, and scene. From this entity memory it composes a global image and a per-shot memory image, which an auxiliary GPU prefills into the generator&#x27;s attention sink before the next shot starts, and writes entity cards for the prompts that name them, so the video never waits for memory and video errors are not written back. A small language model, trained with reinforcement learning and an image reward calibrated using downstream video outcomes, decides what to remember before the next prompt is known. On LongStory-Online, a new benchmark of 25 stories (857 shots) revealed one shot at a time, Agentic LongLive improves persistent-state accuracy of LongLive-2.0, its base generator, by 26.3 points, protagonist identity by 44.9, and revisited-place consistency by 40.4, at about 1% extra generation time on H100. The full system streams faster than real time on one 8xB200 node with the generator in BF16 (34.3 FPS), keeps image quality stable over 14-minute stories, and improves identity on two MiniMax-H3 generators, one of which, LongLive-H3, also runs in real time with it. To our knowledge, it is the first real-time video generator with text and image memory kept by an LLM agent.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Searching Ultra-Long Videos with Decision Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Ce Zhang、Baiqi Li、Seongsu Ha、Gedas Bertasius</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">了解超长视频需要从广泛的视频历史中检索稀少的、与查询相关的证据。 现有的基于相似性的检索方法是高效的,但它们独立地编码了问题和视频内容,因此往往错过了相关证据,而这些证据需要同时进行推理。 generative大语言模型(LLMs)可以进行这样的推理,但是它们通常通过多轮搜索生成文本,这使得每个查询过程缓慢,成本高昂. 在这部作品中,我们引入了DeLOVi(Long Video的决定模型),这个框架利用预先训练的决定模型从超长视频中获取证据. 和LLM一样,一个决定模型会把问题和候选证据一起读出来,这样它就可以说明</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Understanding ultra-long videos requires retrieving sparse, query-relevant evidence from extensive video histories. Existing similarity-based retrieval methods are efficient, but they encode the question and the video content independently and thus often miss evidence whose relevance requires reasoning over both. Generative large language models (LLMs) can perform such reasoning, but they typically generate text over multiple rounds of search, which makes each query slow and costly. In this work, we introduce DeLoVi (Decision Models for Long Video), a framework that leverages pretrained decision models to retrieve evidence from ultra-long videos. Like an LLM, a decision model reads the question together with the candidate evidence, so it can reason over both. Unlike an LLM, it directly outputs a relevance score without generating text, which makes it fast and inexpensive. We first present DeLoVi-S, a simple method that generates captions for short video clips and uses a decision model to rank those captions by their relevance to a question. A generative LLM then answers the question using the top-ranked captions. Despite its simplicity, DeLoVi-S achieves state-of-the-art performance on three ultra-long video understanding benchmarks, EgoLifeQA, EgoMemReason, and MM-Lifelong, and generalizes effectively to long-video understanding benchmarks, VideoMME-L and LVBench. However, DeLoVi-S scores every caption for every question, so its cost grows linearly with video length. To reduce this cost, we propose an automated policy evolution framework that iteratively refines the memory construction and retrieval policies around a fixed pretrained decision model. The resulting method, DeLoVi-E, maintains comparable performance while reducing input tokens by 34x on average compared with DeLoVi-S. It also runs over 3x faster than the best prior method on EgoLifeQA while achieving higher accuracy.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34603" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">VisualMontage:用真实图像序列操纵Visual-Language智能体的信仰</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">VisualMontage: Manipulating the Beliefs of Vision-Language Agents with Authentic Image Sequences</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Jinwei Hu、Lai Wei、Zhuoyun Li、Youcheng Sun、Yi Dong、Xiaowei Huang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">由于基于视觉语言模型(VLMs)的智能体越来越多地诠释社会媒体内容,其分析中的错误可能会为谣言提供可信度并扩大其传播范围. 在本文中,我们研究视觉认知串通的问题,其中协调的帖子结合了真实的图像和当地忠实的字幕,以诱导VLM智能体通过自己的推理推断和接受攻击者选择目标的说法. 为了能够系统地评估这一认知威胁,我们采用了一个新的基准,包括544个目标案例和33.2K八个应用领域的候选图像。 我们进一步提出VisualMontage,这是一个多智能体框架,通过叙事规划、图像选择和订购以及字幕屁股来模拟这种攻击。</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>As agents based on vision-language models (VLMs) increasingly interpret social-media content, errors in their analysis may lend credibility to rumors and amplify their spread. We further propose VisualMontage, a multi-agent framework for simulating such attacks through narrative planning, image selection and ordering, and caption assignment under iterative review. We evaluate this threat through extensive experiments across 12 vision-language models, examining modality effects, production-team components, and downstream defenses. Using identical attack feeds across models without victim-specific optimization, we demonstrate cross-model transferability, achieving mean attack success rates of 61.93% for proprietary models and 68.71% for open-weight models. Moreover, 92.65% of generated feeds pass independent model-based validity auditing. Even after downstream review, residual attack success rates range from 35.3% to 76.7% across the evaluated configurations. Our additional controlled comparisons further reveal that captions can improve model-rated posting naturalness while reducing attack success. Together, these findings reveal a novel cognitive threat in social-media interpretation and motivate safeguards that assess how authentic posts jointly shape agents&#x27; reasoning and conclusions.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31895" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Loupe:加强知识密集型视频生成智能体</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Loupe: Reinforcing Knowledge-Intensive Video Generation Agents</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Kai Zeng、Ruichuan An、Zhen Fang、Xi Fang、Han Lyu、Moran Li 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">知识密集型视频生成仍然具有挑战性,因为提示往往忽略准确生成所需的事实、外表和时间关系。 虽然基于搜索的工作流程可以检索缺失的证据,但人工设计的战略限制了适应性规划,常规训练受到昂贵的视频级反馈和粗糙的轨道级信用分配的影响。 在本文中,我们介绍Loupe, 一个视频生成智能体, 检索和验证文字证据和视觉证据, 构建基于证据的时间视频提示 与参考图像。 我们进一步推出基于Rubric的信用(RBC),它利用迅速具体的事实、视觉和时间要求来评价中间搜索和规划。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Knowledge-intensive video generation remains challenging because prompts often omit the facts, appearances, and temporal relationships required for accurate generation. While search-based workflows can retrieve missing evidence, manually designed strategies limit adaptive planning, and conventional training suffers from costly video-level feedback and coarse trajectory-level credit assignment. In this paper, we present Loupe, a video generation agent that retrieves and verifies textual and visual evidence to construct evidence-grounded temporal video prompts with reference images. We further introduce Rubric-Based Credit (RBC), which leverages prompt-specific factual, visual, and temporal requirements to evaluate intermediate search and planning decisions before video rendering. Building on RBC, RBC-SFT reweights assistant-token learning by step quality, while RBC-GRPO incorporates step-level credits alongside a final planning reward, without rendering videos during RL. To address the lack of dedicated search-oriented evaluation, we construct Search-VBench, which contains 150 held-out prompts across seven categories. Experiments show that Loupe improves all four evaluation dimensions across three video generators, raising the overall score of MiniMax-H3 from 32.80 to 70.67 and outperforming an untrained search workflow. Ablation experiments show that full RBC training outperforms standard SFT followed by trajectory-level GRPO by 5.27 points. Human evaluations and results on Sci-VBench and KIVI-Bench further demonstrate improved generation quality, scientific grounding, factual precision, and helpfulness. Our project page is available at https://zengkaiya.github.io/Loupe/.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33161" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">对 LLM 和 VLM 候选人选择进行约束- Aware 测试- Time 校验</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Constraint-Aware Test-Time Scoring for LLM and VLM Candidate Selection</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Rangarajan Ramesh、Yongchao Huang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">当一个LLM或VLM产生几个候选答案时,选择最好的答案通常依赖于模型本身的信心,一个博学的法官模型,或者一个域名检查. 每一个都有一个已知的弱点:答案可以是流利但不安全的,数学上是可信的,但实际上不可行的,或者在悄悄地违反任务要求时被法官高度评价. 我们提出一个评分框架,把每一份答复都必须经过的一小套硬检查与一套更广泛的软检查结合起来,其得分被混为一数。 这个适应性的评分框架由三个部分组成:一个生成候选人的起草模型,一个选择和加权每项任务的检查的配置模型,以及评分不具有属性的模型。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>When an LLM or VLM produces several candidate answers, picking the best one usually relies on the model&#x27;s own confidence, a learned judge model, or a domain check. Each has a known weakness: an answer can be fluent but insecure, mathematically plausible but physically infeasible, or rated highly by a judge while quietly breaking a task requirement. We propose a scoring framework that combines a small set of hard checks every answer must pass with a wider set of softer checks whose scores are blended into one number per candidate. This adaptive scoring framework consists of three components: a drafting model that produces the candidates, a configuring model that selects and weights the checks for each task, and scoring models that rate properties no grounded tool can compute. We apply our proposed method to four tasks: writing code that is both correct and secure (CWEval), generating robot manipulation plans (LIBERO with added safety variants), passing competitive-programming hidden tests (LiveCodeBench), and answering questions from retrieved documents (HotpotQA). An ablation study isolates the contribution of each component and finds that the largest gains come from combining hard checks with candidate comparison: on tasks where the first sampled answer is often broken or unsafe, this pairing lifts joint correctness-and-safety by up to +0.21 (retrieval-augmented QA) and code-generation safety by up to +0.28 (CWEval), with the size of the gain tracking how weak the first sample is, falling to near zero or slightly negative where it is already strong. The per-task layer that selects and weights the softer checks adds no measurable value over simpler alternatives. This builds a unified scoring framework, together with an ablation that identifies the grounded hard checks, not the soft-factor configuration, as the source of the gain.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Cognitive Load Theory (CLT) shows that human problem-solving is affected by both intrinsic load, arising from task difficulty, and extraneous load, arising from how task information is presented.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large Language Models (LLMs) are known to misinterpret and misrepresent regional and social varieties of English other than Mainstream American English (MAE), such as African American English (AAE), yet most evaluations of these biases have relied on synthetic, stylized or decontextualized data.   <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33232" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RL值多少样本? N类对RL类文字到图像模型的后训练</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">How Many Samples Is RL Worth? Best-of-N versus RL Post-Training for Text-to-Image Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Salil Bhatnagar</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">强化学习(RL)的奖励可以自动检查,已经成为文本到图像模型训练中常见的最后一步. 一个更简单的获得更正确图像的方法是最佳N:从基模型中绘制N图像,并保留一个拾取者喜欢的. 我们问一个RL样本有多少个基模样本值,并将这个数字称为汇率. 以GenEval为基准的398个新提示, 答案取决于及时。 五分之四的提示不要求一个位置(例如,另一个留下的一个对象). 关于这些,</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Reinforcement learning (RL) on a reward that can be checked automatically has become a common last step in the training of text-to-image models. A simpler way to get more correct images is best-of-N: draw N images from the base model and keep the one that a picker prefers. We ask how many base model samples one RL sample is worth, and call this number the exchange rate. We measure it for Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium and its public Flow-GRPO checkpoint on 398 new prompts in the style of GenEval, the benchmark behind the RL reward, with human labels on every prompt and two automatic judges other than the reward. The answer depends on the prompt. Four fifths of the prompts do not ask for a position (for example, one object left of another). On these, the base model catches up: best-of-16 with an off-the-shelf vision-language model (VLM) as the picker is about as good as one RL image. A person who looks through up to 3 base images finds a correct one as often as one RL image is correct. On the prompts that ask for a position, RL is far ahead: a person finds a correct image among 8 base images for only 52% of the prompts, against 92% for one RL image. Over all prompts, our primary test, which we fixed in advance, finds best-of-16 with the VLM picker 9.3 points behind one RL image when both automatic judges must pass an image. People see a smaller gap, 5.5 and 6.5 points in two checks. FLUX.1-dev shows a similar gap in the same test. RL also solves 20 prompts that the base model misses in 128 tries, and 1,024 tries recover half of them. The automatic judges are wrong in opposite directions: a detector-based judge like the reward, as used in our primary test, reports a larger gap than people see, and VLM judges report little or none. Comparisons of RL with best-of-N should therefore check the selected images by hand.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">越来越多地使用大语言模型来提供心理健康支助,引起了重要的安全关切。 现有的护卫模式主要确定有害内容的类别,往往忽略确定适当对策所需的背景。 我们引入了一个临床基础的分类学,它从三个方面描述损害的特点:损害是什么,涉及谁,何时发生。 它进一步将损害定性与干预决定区分开来,从而能够通过可更新和审计的经临床鉴定的明确指示,在模型权重之外具体规定安全策略。 我们用专家临床医生验证分类,并验证一个LLM法官小组,以进行注释。 重新标注公共基准</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>The growing use of large language models (LLMs) for mental health support raises important safety concerns. Existing guard models primarily identify categories of harmful content, often overlooking the context needed to determine an appropriate response. Furthermore, it achieves low false-positive rates at high recall, helping balance the need to detect unsafe situations against the risk of unnecessary interventions that may disrupt support and undermine user trust.   <p class="paper-authors">Jeremy Canale</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">我们提出了对下一代探测和反应管道中动态推理的端到端经验性研究,评价静态分类器,基于图表的信号聚变,以及大语言模型(LLM)分解剂在严格的人-分析注意预算下的表现(在B提醒/天). 跨越56个可复制的实验,跨越一个多层企业和云模拟器和四个公共数据集(SOREL-20M、BODMAS、LANL和DARPA透明计算),我们测试了12个管理现代舰队规模防御的建筑要求。 首先,我们显示机队增长减少了一半,即使计算成本微不足道(低于每百万次事件0.03美元)的固定分析员预算,而且微量加权图的风险传播效果也超过了图SAGE。</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>We present an end-to-end empirical study of dynamic reasoning in next-generation detection and response pipelines, evaluating how static classifiers, graph-based signal fusion, and large language model (LLM) triage agents perform under strict human-analyst attention budgets (Recall at B alerts/day).   <p class="paper-summary">在现实世界中运作的AI特工必须正确和及时地说明理由,但他们目前没有说明时间应如何改变其推理。 现代智能体学会在完全同步的环境中完成任务:即世界等待智能体在改变前采取行动. 相比之下,实时环境会独立于智能体而改变. 现有办法试图通过规定思维与行动的比例来弥补这一差距。 但是,智能体要想在实时上取得成功,就必须学会在拖延代价高昂时迅速采取行动,在利益超过失去的机会时更长时间地说明理由。 为此,我们引入了实时推理训练(RRT)作为实时推理智能体员的训练技巧课程. 我们发展</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>AI agents that operate in the real world must reason correctly and on-time, yet they currently do not account for how time should alter their reasoning. Modern agents learn to do tasks in environments that are entirely synchronous: where the world waits for the agent to act before changing.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Numerical cognition is a simple yet fundamental ability that remains challenging for modern vision-language models.   <p class="paper-authors">Junhong Huang、Xin Tong、Jun Xia</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">长视大语言模型(LLM)智能体需要持续的内存,在正确的时间显示正确的过去交互,跨越数千个转弯. 许多系统通过引用遗传性LLMs将历史重写成摘要、事实或知识图表——一个步骤,即(a) 代价高昂(每块数千次基因调用);(b) 非决定性(破坏再生产性并给出前后不一致的答案);(c) 来源时代(读者看到综合文本,而不是原始转折)。 我们提议MGMem, 一个没有基因索引呼叫的框架, 以决定性的歧视性-parser 管道取代这一步骤: 反复提到的会议被组织成一个事件图表, 问题提法是resol</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Long-horizon Large Language Model (LLM) agents need persistent memory that surfaces the right past interaction at the right time across thousands of turns.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used for biomedical question answering, but plausible incorrect answers remain a safety risk in clinical settings.   <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33316" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RL 后训练教学文本到图像模型是什么? 流动技能分析</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">What Does RL Post-Training Teach Text-to-Image Models? A Skill-Wise Analysis of Flow-GRPO</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Cheng Zhang、Jiesong Liu</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">强化学习(RL)后训练成为改进文本到图像流模式的标准方法: Flow-GRPO将GenEval分数SD3.5-M从0.63升至0.95,这一增益被读作模式已经学会了组成生成. 我们问,通过测量共享噪声种子上的传球@k,在RL之前和之后,在训练中从未使用过的提示上,这些样本中有多少获得了基本模型本身的样本。 答案因技巧而异。 在GenEval的6个技能中的4个技能上,32个碱基样本已经几乎每一次都解答了,收益正在磨损:一个经过训练的样品值4到10个碱基样本,通过@32几乎不动. 在空间关系上解锁: pass@32从0.69升至1.00,128个基准样本不匹配</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Reinforcement learning (RL) post-training has become a standard way to improve text-to-image flow models: Flow-GRPO lifts the GenEval score of SD3.5-M from 0.63 to 0.95, a gain read as the model having learned compositional generation. We ask how much of that gain the base model&#x27;s own samples already contained, by measuring pass@k over shared noise seeds, one skill at a time, before and after RL, on prompts never used in training. The answer differs by skill. On four of GenEval&#x27;s six skills, where 32 base samples already solve almost every prompt, the gain is sharpening: one post-trained sample is worth 4 to 10 base samples, and pass@32 barely moves. On spatial relations it is unlocking: pass@32 rises from 0.69 to 1.00, 128 base samples do not match one post-trained sample, and prompts the base model never solved in 32 samples are solved 93% of the time, as often as those it solved. The benchmark average, 31 base samples per post-trained sample, describes no skill. Relations carry 35% of the gain; of the other 65%, selecting among 8 base samples with the judge behind the reward recovers nine tenths. We propose an account of when each occurs: within a prompt, a group-relative update re-weights only the outcomes its samples contain, and a flow policy cannot widen the noise that produces them; across prompts, shared parameters let successes on some prompts repair a deficit common to many. The same anatomy applies to a text-rendering checkpoint. The account&#x27;s predictions are tested with a checkpoint trained without KL, within-prompt diversity, and controlled training that holds object classes out, where sharpening comes first and transfers, and unlocking follows, on practised objects before unseen ones.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">持续的文字内存可以改善LLM智能体,但重复更新会削弱任务性能. 我们询问,存储统计数据是否在被搁置的准确性下降之前发出警告。 我们在ALFWorld、AppWorld、ScienceWorld、WebShop以及两个ARC-AGI设置中, 这些方法使用选择性检索或全存储提示存储轨迹,规则,工作流程,策略,或链接记录. 没有这种统计数字可以可靠地预测任务精度下降的早期迹象,而且美元中位铅为零。 以高峰精度后检查站作为二级下降点,测量高度相似的对和n</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Persistent textual memory can improve LLM agents, but repeated updates can erode task performance.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Multi-agent systems (MAS) built on large language models (LLMs) outperform single-agent systems, but inter-agent communication incurs substantial token overhead.   <p class="paper-original-title">VibeVideoAgent: Closing the Perceptual Loop for Code-Driven Video Generation</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Xuanyu Zheng、Luoyuan Zhang、Yancheng Long、Haoyang Xu、Muxi Diao、Yuzhe Chen 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">代码驱动的视频生成请求一个编码智能体编写和运行渲染程序. 这使得对打字,数据可视化,同步音频,以及多镜头场景的精确控制,它留下了一个可编辑的项目. 一个干净地退出的程序仍然可以使标签重叠,一个摄影机瞄准它的主题,损坏的物体相互作用,或者说会漂移出同步,这些都没有引起运行时间错误. 饲料使帧和音频回到编码会话中,其长角度上下文模糊不清,而只有完成的全分辨率才使早期设计错误变成昂贵的重写。 我们介绍 VibeVideo Agent 一个免费训练的框架 让编码员检查他们的草稿</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Code-driven video generation asks a coding agent to write and run rendering programs. This gives exact control over typography, data visualizations, synchronized audio, and multi-shot scenes, and it leaves an editable project behind. A program that exits cleanly can still render overlapping labels, a camera aimed away from its subject, broken object interactions, or narration that drifts out of sync, and none of these raise a runtime error. Feeding rendered frames and audio back into the coding session bloats its long-horizon context, while critiquing only the finished full-resolution render turns early design mistakes into costly rewrites. We introduce VibeVideoAgent, a training-free framework that lets coding agents inspect their drafts throughout production. Multimodal perception runs in short-lived contexts kept apart from the coder and returns compact, grounded Findings. Seven primitives operate on a unified multi-clock representation of assets and drafts: they explore media from coarse to fine, verify quoted requirements with evidence from every required modality, compare a draft with a reference or an earlier revision on separate clocks, and measure stream signals locally. A lightweight staged protocol has the agent test its riskiest decision on a minimal preview first. On all 239 VibeVideoBench tasks, with matched agent runtimes and a fixed perception model, VibeVideoAgent improves holistic audiovisual quality (L3) for Claude Opus 5.5 (4.03 -&gt; 4.43, while L2 rubric compliance rises from 98.87% to 99.35%), GPT-6 Astra (4.03 -&gt; 4.27), and Claude Sonnet 5.5 (3.97 -&gt; 4.10), with the largest gains in audio-visual synchronization (up to +0.56). Controlled ablations show that each component contributes, and trajectory audits show that the granularity of code edits governs the trade-off between rubric adherence and perceptual quality.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30460" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CONCOR:为视觉幻觉减缓超出最终产出绩效的基准对比解码</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">CONCOR: Benchmarking Contrastive Decoding for Visual Hallucination Mitigation Beyond Final-Output Performance</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Ting Chen、Geng Li、Guohao Chen、Hanjia Lyu</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">对比解码(CD)通过在不同条件下对比符号分布来调整符号分数,减轻了多模态大语言模型(MLLM)中的对象幻觉. 然而,现有的遗传性和歧视性基准主要衡量最终任务分数,由于候选人过滤,生成轨迹变化,以及反应偏差,这些都可能令人困惑,因此很难孤立潜在的对比性调整的影响. 为了在补充现有评价的同时克服这些局限性,我们采用了CONCOR(协同校正评价),这是直接评价地面真相与不同类型幻觉候选人之间使用对照格鲁恩的竞争的控制基准。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Contrastive decoding (CD) mitigates object hallucination in multimodal large language models (MLLMs) by contrasting token distributions under different conditions to adjust token scores. However, existing generative and discriminative benchmarks primarily measure final task scores, which can be confounded by candidate filtering, changes in generation trajectories, and response biases, making it difficult to isolate the effects of the underlying contrastive adjustments. To address these limitations while complementing existing evaluations, we introduce CONCOR (Contrastive Correction Evaluation), a controlled benchmark that directly evaluates the competition between ground-truth and different types of hallucinated candidates using the Contrastive Grounding Margin (CGM). CGM also enables analysis of directional consistency, composition, and reliability of contrastive corrections. Our evaluation reveals that existing CD methods exhibit distinct correction behaviors: some consistently favor ground-truth candidates, whereas others produce strong corrections but also introduce more errors. Crucially, these differences help explain their contrasting responses to increased contrast strength, which improves some methods while degrading others. These findings highlight the importance of evaluating the contrastive correction itself, rather than relying solely on final-output performance, and CONCOR provides a controlled and interpretable framework for understanding and improving CD methods for hallucination mitigation.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33303" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">认证您所植入的 : 逻辑理性数据中的快捷- 无深度证明</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Certify What You Plant: Shortcut-Free Proof Depth in Logical Reasoning Data</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Baptiste Juran、Dimitri Kachler、Damien Sileo</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">程序产生的逻辑问题既用于评价,也用于训练语言模型的推理. 他们的难度通常通过安插证据来确定,即取样证明所期望的深度,并说明其前提是问题所在。 然而,人工证据只是一种可能的证据。 一个问题也可以有更短的证明,或者几个无关的证明,它所说的深度就过分夸大了它所需要的解决. 我们衡量一个问题实际需要什么, 相对于一套既定的推断规则。 对于每个问题,我们计算每个证明必须采取的步骤,最短的证明,最低限度证明的数量和不可或缺的前提,以及独立核查员核实的出口证明。 对8个数据集的审计</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Procedurally generated logic problems are used to both evaluate and train reasoning in language models. Their difficulty is usually set by planting a proof, that is, by sampling a proof of the desired depth and stating its premises as the problem. However, a planted proof is only one possible proof. A problem can also have shorter proofs, or several unrelated ones, and its stated depth then overstates what solving it requires. We measure what a problem actually requires, relative to a stated set of inference rules. For each problem, we compute the steps every proof must make, the shortest proof, the number of minimal proofs and the indispensable premises, and export certificates that an independent checker verifies. Audits of eight datasets find depth that is forced (ProofWriter, Blocksworld), problems labelled depth 5 that require about two specific rule applications (FLDx2), and answers that need no particular step (ZebraLogic). We then construct problems whose every proof has a requested structure, using a catalog of shortcuts proved once per rule set. Every constructed problem is checked in full and comes with its own certificate. In training, the best data mixes depths and problem types. At equal size, such mixtures of certified problem types, including model-checking problems that forward generation does not produce, transfer better than FLDx2-style derivation data for Qwen3 models from 0.6B to 8B.</p></details>
