  <p class="paper-original-title">Verifiable pXRD: Hill-climbing pXRD structure determination with LLM agents</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Samuel Chen、Jiaru Bai、Austin Henry Cheng、Varinia Bernales、Al\&#x27;an Aspuru-Guzik</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">从粉末X射线疏导(pXRD)中自主的晶体结构测定仍然是自驾材料实验室的关键瓶颈. 我们制定pXRD结构确定的任务,作为一个可核查的智能体搜索问题:在推论期间,可以通过远期模拟配置协议评估候选结构,在评价期间可通过结构匹配到已知的参考. 为了衡量进度,我们引入了pXRD-Bench, 一组200个模拟的单相问题, 跨越分布式结构的确定, 具有挑战性和多形态性的结构, 以及有控制的成分和地形分布的转变。 物剂适应使选定的结构恢复率从27%提高到69%,再生豆的进一步收益</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Autonomous crystal-structure determination from powder X-ray diffraction (pXRD) remains a key bottleneck for self-driving materials laboratories. We formulate the task of pXRD structure determination as a verifiable agentic search problem: candidate structures can be assessed during inference through forward-simulated profile agreement and during evaluation through structural matching to a known reference. To benchmark progress, we introduce pXRD-Bench, a suite of 200 simulated single-phase problems spanning in-distribution structure determination, challenging and polymorphic structures, and controlled compositional and topological distribution shifts. Agent adaptation raises selected structural recovery from 27% to 69%, with further gains from repeated sampling and verifier-guided inference-time scaling. We extend this approach to experimental multiphase pXRD through a reflexive evolution framework that learns from self-generated synthetic problems. A replaceable laboratory specification defines the sample family and measurement conditions. An evolver agent uses the known generating structures and measurement parameters to diagnose solver failures and develop persistent guidance and executable tools, which are then provided to agents on measured patterns. This supplies verifiable training experience without requiring a collection of solved experimental measurements. On 15 measured patterns from solid-state synthesis, the evolved package improves average profile agreement and transfers an analogue-substitution procedure that recovers a phase whose structure is absent from the supplied reference library. These results demonstrate how learning from verifiable synthetic experience can improve agentic characterization of experimental samples whose structures are not known in advance.</p></details>

Controlled replay combines actual inference with recorded environment responses and durations for ablations of individual optimizations.   <p class="paper-authors">Ali Mohammadi Ruzbahani、Rezvan Abouzadeh</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">在训练预算用完之前,一个小的贴有标签的数据集早就被记住了,剩下的一热标签要贴上,然后大多会增强信心。 模式反省将其余预算用于审查和整合,从网络自身知识中重新学习。 训练分为各集,其学习率被再次提高和降温,每个降温状态会加入一个过去自我的记忆,每个新剧集从标签中学习,从这种记忆的温和预测中学习,从学生和记忆分歧最大的对抗性梦中学习. 内存平均预测而不是权重,部署的网络是最后一个学生,因此推论没有额外成本. 横跨CIFAR-100、小图像网络和Imagewoof、fo</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>A small labelled dataset is memorized long before the training budget runs out, and the one-hot labels that remain to be fitted then mostly sharpen confidence. Model annealing spends the rest of the budget on review and consolidation, relearning from the network&#x27;s own knowledge. Training is divided into episodes whose learning rate is raised again and cooled, every cooled state joins a memory of past selves, and each new episode learns from the labels, from the tempered predictions of this memory, and from adversarial dreams sought where student and memory disagree most. For an SGD-trained ResNet-18 it lowers accuracy but still improves robustness to small adversarial perturbations. Separating the reheated schedule from relearning shows that relearning helps every family and that the losses come from reheats, which undo most of this network&#x27;s fit to its training set. How early the accuracy of a cross-entropy run saturates largely separates the AdamW families from ResNet-18 but does not predict the gain within them. With little data, relearning from the latest snapshot without dreams, as snapshot distillation does, compounds overconfidence in the AdamW families from episode to episode. The memory and the dreams each curb most of this drift, and together they remove it.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Recommendation and Ranking (R&amp;R) models, particularly in digital advertising, require massive inference throughput but exhibit extreme sensitivity to precision loss.   <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Before an LLM agent cancels a reservation or deletes a folder for a user, what stops it from acting on a false assumption: what its tools show, or what the user says? In this work, we study this gap with presupposition theory from linguistics: an agent may act only if each claim behind the act (e.g., one reservation fits the name) is seen to hold, answered by the user, or disclosed while the act can be undone. A simple bound shows that when lookups cannot tell one record from two, any agent acts without asking first as often with two records as with one. We then vary whether a search lists, counts, or omits a second match, and whether the user states a claim that a record refutes (e.g., &quot;It is empty&quot;). The newest Claude and GPT models always open a counted second record, then never act. When we add a same-name, same-zip customer to a retail benchmark, a lookup returning that customer first led to wrong-account writes in about a quarter of conversations, and one that checks a street the user knows kept completion near the one-customer level.   <p class="paper-authors">Shubhra Mittal、Gaurav Naresh Mittal</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大语言模型的幻觉检测主要以7B-70B研究模型上评价的方法为主,具有丰富的采样预算和精确匹配或ROUGE正确性标签. 小型模型从业者实际上将Phi-3-mini,Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct,Quen2.5-3B-Instruct,以及Gemma-2-2B-Instruct,一般为4位精度,坐落在这个证据库之外. 我们对四个跨越三个范式的探测器进行可复制的单GPU审计:长度常态化的符号边距校准,一个正方形基线,以及两个基于取样的一致方法(Semantic Entropy和SelfCheck GPT-NLI),两个小型开放式指令调制模型(Llama-3.2B-Instruct和Quen2.5-3B-Instruct),装在单C上的4位NF4 quantization上.</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Hallucination detection for large language models is dominated by methods evaluated on 7B-70B research models with abundant sampling budgets and exact-match or ROUGE correctness labels. The small models practitioners actually ship on-device, Phi-3-mini, Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, and Gemma-2-2B-Instruct, typically at 4-bit precision, sit outside this evidence base.   <p class="paper-original-title">An Operationalization of Opaque Serial Depth</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Nathan Sheffield、Alek Westover、Lukas Finnveden、Alexa Pan、Julian Stastny、Ryan Greenblatt</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">思维链(CoT)目前是监督AI模型的宝贵工具,但一些建筑转变可能会显著降低CoT的可监测性. 我们实施了一个计量标准,作为模型可以执行的无语言序列识别量的代称,作为不透明序列深度概念的具体即时描述:大致来说,通过模型的计算,最长路径的长度不会通过可解释的瓶颈. 我们的主要贡献是确定一个特殊标准,以克服这种瓶颈。 我们把基于自然语言(NL-根基)的节点定义为在生产人类可以理解的输出前从预训练中初始化的节点,其输出空间没有扩大至先前的.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.AI</span><span>cs.LG</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Chain-of-thought (CoT) is currently a valuable tool for overseeing AI models, but some architectural shifts could significantly reduce CoT monitorability. We operationalize a measure that serves as a proxy for the amount of unverbalized serial cognition a model can perform, as a specific instantiation of the notion of opaque serial depth: roughly, the length of the longest path through a model&#x27;s computation that does not pass through an interpretable bottleneck. Our main contribution is a particular standard for what counts as such a bottleneck. We find that, across open-weight models, NLS depth correlates strongly with layer count, with some exceptions, and has grown slowly over recent years. It would likely grow slowly under continued scaling of standard transformers, while latent-reasoning architectures such as full-bandwidth transformers could increase it by a large amount. We work through how NLS depth applies to a range of existing and proposed architectures, discuss alternative operationalizations, and give a worked example bounding the depth of Gemma3-1B.   <p class="paper-summary">行为群集在用户或项目交互作用上被学习到随着行为演化而漂移:一些片段分化成子行为群集,有些趋同,有些消失,出现了新的规律. 两种模式主导着不断演变的集群的维持. 当硬码统计跨过截断点时,阈值触发了预先指定的动作;重置和履带(MONIC-tyle)重新组合每个窗口,并推断新集群如何与前一个窗口对齐的过渡. 既将决定硬化为门槛规则,又重新组合规则。 我们研究一种替代方法,一种大语言模型(LLM)作出生命周期决定。 鉴于当前集群集的无门槛、全人口观点(统计健康、标签、成员文本、resi)</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Behavioral clusters learned over user or item interactions drift as behavior evolves: some fragment into sub-behaviors, some converge, some disappear, and new patterns emerge. Two paradigms dominate the maintenance of evolving clusterings. Threshold triggers fire a pre-specified action when a hardcoded statistic crosses a cutoff; refit-and-track (MONIC-style) re-clusters every window and infers transitions from how the new clusters align with the previous window&#x27;s.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Generative model-based approaches to linear inverse problems typically use the acquired noisy measurement to condition or guide sampling in the image domain. These findings point to concrete design principles for autoresearch systems: since models can judge candidates they cannot produce, enriching the search with externally sourced ideas and questions may be more effective than improving any single model&#x27;s generation ability.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">指挥家对一支管弦乐队的表演至关重要,尽管他们不发出声音. 过去十年的基本研究和临床研究揭示了天体细胞可能是大脑网络即&quot;管弦乐团&quot;的&quot;导体&quot;:天体细胞占所有脑细胞约50美元,但不发出电讯. 尽管如此,它们可能在重要的认知大脑功能,特别是学习,记忆和信息处理中发挥关键作用. 在这里,我们考虑这些发现对脑振荡的机器学习(ML)的影响,通常在神经元及其电相互作用之间作类比。 我们强调两个关键天体细胞特性的具体算法。 第一个角色</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Conductors are critical for the performance of an orchestra even though they do not emit sound. Basic research and clinical studies in the last decade are revealing that astrocytes may be the &quot;conductors&quot; of the &quot;orchestra&quot; that is the brain network: astrocytes make up approximately $50\%$ of all brain cells but do not emit electrical signals. The rhythmic sharing algorithm we developed based on this premise yields rapid anomaly detection adaptation and prevents catastrophic forgetting.
