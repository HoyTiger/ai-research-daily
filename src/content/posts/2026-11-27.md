  <p class="paper-original-title">Accelerating inverse materials design using generative diffusion models with reinforcement learning</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Junwu Chen、Jeff Guo、Edvin Fako、Philippe Schwaller</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">传播模型有望通过直接生成具有理想特性的新结构来加速材料设计,但有条件的生成等现有方法通常需要昂贵且贴上大量标签的数据(&gt;10 000),缺乏适应性。 在这里,我们介绍 MatInvent,一种一般和高效的强化学习工作流程,它优化了目标定向晶体生成的传播模型. MatInvent通过将等同的去职确定为一个多步骤的决策问题,利用奖励加权的Kullback- leibler正规化,包括经验回放和多样性过滤器,以提高优化效率和样本多样性. 对于单一目标设计,MatInvent会迅速汇合到柏油</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>Nature Machine Intelligence</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Diffusion models promise to accelerate materials design by directly generating novel structures with desired properties, but existing approaches such as conditional generation typically require expensive and substantial labelled data (&gt;10,000) and lack adaptability. Here we present MatInvent, a general and efficient reinforcement learning workflow that optimizes diffusion models for goal-directed crystal generation. By formulating equivariant denoising as a multistep decision-making problem, MatInvent leverages policy gradient with reward-weighted Kullback–Leibler regularization, including experience replay and diversity filters to enhance optimization efficiency and sample diversity. For single-objective designs, MatInvent rapidly converges to target values within 60 iterations (~1,000 property evaluations) across electronic, magnetic, mechanical, thermal and physicochemical properties. Furthermore, MatInvent achieves robust optimization in design tasks with multiple conflicting properties, successfully proposing low-supply-chain-risk magnets and high- κ dielectrics. Compared with state-of-the-art methods, MatInvent exhibits comparable or superior generation performance under specified property constraints while markedly reducing the demand for property computation. Compatible with diverse diffusion model architectures and property constraints, MatInvent could offer broad applicability in materials discovery.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Ross Murphy、Gabriel Brostow</p>

  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>The Platonic Representation Hypothesis (PRH) posits that AI models converge toward a shared representation of the world, even when trained on different data modalities. Previous work has found evidence for the PRH in the activations of vision and language models. In this work, we ask whether vision and language models also converge in how they act on these representations: precisely, in how these internal representations affect the models&#x27; output. At a chosen layer of a transformer, the Jacobian lens gives every word in the vocabulary (or class in an image classifier) a vector in the hidden state: the vector that most increases the model&#x27;s tendency to generate that word, at that position or later, or to predict that class. Collectively, these vectors make up what is referred to as the model&#x27;s J-space. We find evidence of the PRH in J-space across vision and language models, and furthermore, we find that J-spaces mostly converge up to a rotation. That is, a single rotation, fitted on a subset of words, approximately maps one model&#x27;s J-space onto another&#x27;s, and so applies to words and classes it was not fitted on. A word&#x27;s vector, rotated from a text-only language model&#x27;s J-space into the visual patch tokens of a vision-language model, changes the object the vision-language model says is in the image (and only in the image region where it is added). Rotated into an image generator&#x27;s text conditioning, a word&#x27;s vector changes the object in the generated image. Rotated into an ImageNet classifier (trained without text), it raises that object&#x27;s class score above almost all others, and vice versa. This suggests that models converge not only in how they represent the world, but also in how they act on it.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01332-7" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">细胞表达学习的测量噪声缩放法</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">A measurement noise scaling law for cellular representation learning</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Gokul Gowri、Igor Sadalski、Dan Raviv、Peng Yin、Jonathan Rosenfeld、Allon Klein</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大型基因组和成像数据集可用于训练学习细胞系统有意义表现的模型. 在所有领域,模型性能通过数据集大小和计算预算可以预测得到改进,为分配数据和计算提供了基础。 然而,科学数据也因分子下取样,测序误差和图像分辨率等因素产生的噪音而受到限制. 在这里,我们采用带有辅助信号的相互信息作为表示模型性能的衡量标准,并用噪音研究其缩放. 通过在三种数据模式(基因表达、序列和图像数据)中安装2 000多个表达式学习模型,我们显示噪声定义了一个独特的轴线,其性能将随之而来。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>生成与创造</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>AI for Science</span><span>Nature Machine Intelligence</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large genomic and imaging datasets can be used to train models that learn meaningful representations of cellular systems. Across domains, model performance improves predictably with dataset size and compute budget, providing a basis for allocating data and computation. Scientific data, however, are also limited by noise arising from factors such as molecular undersampling, sequencing errors and image resolution. Here we introduce mutual information with auxiliary signals as a metric of representation model performance and study its scaling with noise. By fitting more than 2,000 representation learning models across three data modalities (gene expression, sequence and image data), we show that noise defines a distinct axis along which performance improves. Noise scaling follows a logarithmic law. We derive the law from a model of noise propagation, and use it to define noise sensitivity and model capacity as benchmarking metrics. We show that protein-sequence representations tolerate error rates in modern sequencing data, whereas single-cell transcriptomic models remain noise limited at current measurement signal-to-noise ratio levels. Within transcriptomic cell-state representation learning models, we find that transformer-based models are more noise robust but less performant than variational autoencoders. Noise scaling metrics may support future model evaluation and experimental design.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Reading, Not Adapting: Unlocking Frozen Visual Knowledge for AI-Generated Image Detection</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Yuncheng Guo、Ying Dong、Xiaodong Gu</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">向看不见的图像生成器进行泛化,促使人们努力调整经过预先训练的视觉表现以进行检测。 然而,最近的调查结果表明,愿景基础模型已经编码了可转让检测知识,而进一步调整则可能妨碍普遍性。 这促使人们从骨干适应转向知识阅读。 常规线性探测为读取这种知识提供了直接途径,但仅限于最后一级RGB特征. 多曲线探测器拓宽了证据基础,但其设计引入了其他权衡:适应可能损害预先训练的可转移性,多种编码器使特征集成复杂化,而后聚变排除中间跨视相互作用. 为了克服这些限制,我们建议:</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Generalization to unseen image generators has driven efforts to adapt pretrained visual representations for detection. Yet recent findings show that vision foundation models already encode transferable detection knowledge, while further adaptation can impair generalization. This motivates a shift from backbone adaptation to knowledge readout. Conventional linear probing offers a direct way to read this knowledge but is limited to final-layer RGB features. Multi-cue detectors broaden the evidence base, but their designs introduce other tradeoffs: adaptation may compromise pretrained transferability, heterogeneous encoders complicate feature integration, and late fusion precludes intermediate cross-view interaction. To overcome these limitations, we propose ReMoT, a Mixture-of-Transformers-inspired framework that reads complementary knowledge from spatially aligned RGB and wavelet views within a fully frozen visual foundation. ReMoT employs staged joint attention to enable controlled local and global cross-view interaction, while learnable fusion queries progressively read complementary evidence across Transformer depth without writeback. ReMoT achieves 91.2% average accuracy across eight benchmark groups with 3,074 trainable parameters, attaining the highest overall average among the evaluated methods. These results support structured knowledge readout as a promising alternative to backbone adaptation. Code is available at https://github.com/yunncheng/ReMoT.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Confidence scores for gated automation of large language models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Luiz G. A. Alves、Aaron Vermeersch</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">AI智能体基于大语言模型(LLMs),在自动化决策方面表现出强大的能力. 然而,有限责任公司可能以高度的信心提出不正确的答案,限制了它们在影响个人和组织的决定中的适用性。 可靠的信任分数对于作出这种决定是必要的,现有的估计数常常是没有的,或者太粗糙,无法支持有效的定位。 这项工作利用有监督的学习来训练模型,以预测LLM生成的答案的正确性,使得自动自信的响应和引导不确定的答案用于人类审查. 一个线性置信分数头被安装在冷冻的LLM主干线的终层隐藏状态上,而文本唯一的元模则被训练为切换元件</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>AI agents built on large language models (LLMs) demonstrate strong capabilities in automated decision-making. However, LLMs may produce incorrect answers with high confidence, limiting their applicability in decisions that affect individuals and organizations. Reliable confidence scores are necessary to gate such decisions, and existing estimates are frequently unavailable or too coarse to support effective gating. This work uses supervised learning to train models to predict the correctness of LLM-generated answers, enabling automated confident responses and routing uncertain ones for human review. A linear confidence score head is fitted to the final-layer hidden state of the frozen LLM backbone, and text-only metamodels are trained as an alternative for closed models. All results come from a single 2B-parameter backbone, on graded answers that are about 98% SQuAD and the remainder TruthfulQA. Against multiple baselines, the confidence score head outperforms all baselines and metamodels, automating the largest fraction of the workload at high accuracy targets. Prompted confidence scores can appear well calibrated yet lack the resolution needed for thresholding. Although transferability to unseen datasets is modest, the confidence score head remains the most robust approach. The proposed confidence scoring framework enhances the safety of automating LLM-based agents and can be adapted to gate other properties, including safety or compliance.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Solve Vision with Code: Coding Agents Are Stronger Visual Reasoners Than Video Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Dezhi Luo、Fengyuan Yu、Haotian Zhang、Zehong Zhao、Ran Ji、Siyuan An 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">视频生成模型越来越多地被评价为视觉理性:鉴于第一帧和自然语言的提示,该模型能否产生执行指定任务的视频? 范式被认为是有利的,因为推理是视觉介质的本体,直接在框中展开,而不是通过语言或代码. 事实上,这种本土路线是否是更可靠的视觉推理途径,并没有在同样条件下通过象征性的替代办法来测试。 因此,我们向两种媒体提出相同的任务,提供相同的投入、输出规格和评价:一个编码智能体撰写一个程序,制作解答视频,而不是去除像素,这是一个没有修改的规则评价。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Video generation models are increasingly evaluated as visual reasoners: given a first frame and a natural-language prompt, can the model produce the video in which the specified task is carried out? The paradigm is considered advantageous because the reasoning is native to the visual medium, unfolding directly in the frames rather than through language or code. Whether this native route is in fact the more reliable path to visual reasoning has not been tested against a symbolic alternative under the same conditions. We therefore pose the same tasks, with identical inputs, output specification and evaluator, to both media: rather than denoising pixels, a coding agent writes a program that renders the answer video, which an unmodified rule-based evaluator scores. On VBVR-Pro-Bench (Xu et al., 2026), a 500-instance suite spanning five cognitive categories with symbolic ground truth, the agent receives the same first frame and prompt as the video model, with no access to ground truth, metadata or generation models, and one attempt per instance. Three closed-model agents (Codex with gpt-6-astra, Claude Code with Fable 5.1 and Gemini CLI with Gemini 3.1 Pro) score 0.923, 0.894 and 0.893, each more than 0.22 above the strongest video model. Among 23 runnable open-weight models, the best, GLM-5, reaches 0.572, above all video models except the RL-trained one. Together, we show that for tasks whose solutions can be computed from a first frame and a prompt, program synthesis is currently a substantially more reliable route to visual reasoning than video generation. We further distinguish depictive programs, which navigate the image to recover a transferable scheme, from propositional programs, which operate on hard-coded values, revealing how visual reasoning unfolds in agent-written programs and what it implies for its nature of reasoning in general.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Siddharth Arya、Kshitij Jain</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">人工智能模型在能力和价格上差异很大。 模型的路由通过向更便宜的模型发送更容易的任务来利用这一点. 然而,一个单一的智能体任务往往将容易和困难的子任务结合起来,使得整个任务的一个模型选择效率低下. 我们引入了部分路线,它指导一种前沿领先模式,将合适的子任务委托给一种更便宜的工人模式。 从两个模型的过去运行中,我们确定反复出现的子任务,并衡量每个模型的质量和成本. 这些测量得出了授权指导,其中有一个参数($\delta$),控制了授权的多少. 领导决定执行期间何时和如何授权,而不训练任何一种模式。 关于从ViBench和Terminal-Bench 4.0停运的任务,部分路线</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>AI models vary enormously in capability and price. Model routing exploits this by sending easier tasks to cheaper models.   <p class="paper-authors">Mohsen Shirali、Kean Ooi、Anna Knab、Mohammadreza Fani Sani、Estefania Serral</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">Internet of Things(IoT)系统生成不直接适合过程分析的低水平传感器观测,必须首先转化为具有模拟意义的事件. 本文将协作语言模型作为零射的语义抽象层来调查,用于将此类观测转化为更高层次的事件和活动标签. 我们提出一个五个阶段的工作流程,包括语义绘图、语义汇总、时间和背景汇总、专门推理和最后事件抽象。 由此产生的标签和背景资料随后可作为下游进程采矿任务的事件记录。 通过CASAS阿鲁巴Sma的人类活动认可来评价这一方法。</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Internet of Things (IoT) systems generate low-level sensor observations that are not directly suitable for process analysis and must first be transformed into semantically meaningful events. This paper investigates collaborative language models as a zero-shot semantic abstraction layer for converting such observations into higher-level event and activity labels. We propose a five-stage workflow comprising semantic mapping, semantic aggregation, temporal and contextual aggregation, specialized reasoning, and final event abstraction. The resulting labels and contextual information can subsequently be represented as event records for downstream Process Mining (PM) tasks. The approach is evaluated through Human Activity Recognition on the CASAS Aruba smart home dataset using eleven workflow configurations. The findings indicate that the structure of LM collaboration and task decomposition is more important than simply increasing the number of participating models.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Embedding models increasingly inform how people search, recommend, and retrieve information, yet we lack credible ways to test whether these similarity judgments reflect how humans understand meaning. Existing benchmarks typically rely on static, expert-labeled pairs with coarse Likert-style annotations, limiting scalability and making it difficult to obtain consistent, high-confidence annotations. We introduce Aligned Machine, an interactive, public-facing platform that transforms broad participation into a continuously growing Human-Aligned Similarity Benchmark (HASB) for evaluating semantic alignment in embedding-based systems. Participants complete a cognitively-natural, forced-choice task, comparing two pairs of items (text or images) and selecting which pair is more similar. Text-only models consistently achieve higher overall agreement than multimodal models, while multimodal performance varies systematically by task type: alignment is strongest for image-image comparisons, weaker for image-text comparisons, and lowest for text-text comparisons. Aligned Machine is designed to evolve over time as participation grows, supporting longitudinal analysis, domain-specific studies, bias detection, and measurement of concept drift across model versions. By combining accessible public participation with rigorous comparative evaluation, HASB provides a scalable and accountable framework for auditing semantic alignment in embedding models.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Maximus Adeola</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">文本嵌入模型可以将表面-形式相似性与事实身份相混淆. 鉴于一个被腐蚀的事实被修改的近拷贝,以及措辞不同的保留事实的重写,基模更喜欢被修改的近拷贝. 我们测试硬阴性对比性监督是否能够减少这种表面相似性捷径. 我们构建了FOF-Bench,一个83,263的三重基准,跨越五个领域. 每个锚都配有LLM生成的,保留事实的文体重写和匹配的事实变化近似拷贝. 我们微调FOF-80M(Focus On Facts),一种80M参数嵌入模型,具有三重对比目标,将重写排在事实改变之上. 在8,327个三重测试中, 基型选择了事实改变</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Text embedding models can conflate surface-form similarity with factual identity. Given an altered near-copy with one corrupted fact and a differently worded fact-preserving rewrite, the base model prefers the altered near-copy. We test whether hard-negative contrastive supervision can reduce this surface-similarity shortcut. We construct FOF-Bench, an 83,263-triplet benchmark spanning five domains. Each anchor is paired with an LLM-generated, fact-preserving stylistic rewrite and a matched fact-altered near-copy. We fine-tune FOF-80M (Focus On Facts), an 80M-parameter embedding model, with a triplet contrastive objective that ranks the rewrite above the factual alteration. After fine-tuning, mean positive--negative separation is +0.0329, 98.5% of individual margins move in the intended direction, and the preference rate reaches 29.37%. On DiSC, an external style-transfer corpus, mean cross-style similarity increases from 0.7905 to 0.9559. The model also penalizes omission and transfers poorly to directional tasks such as NLI and summarization.   <p class="paper-original-title">When the Knowledge Runs Out: A Part-Level Pilot Evaluation of How Language Models Handle Partially Covered Questions</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Vahid Mohammadi</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">从提供的参考材料中回答的助理往往收到涵盖用户问题某些部分的材料,而不是其他部分的材料。 现有关于背景不足的评价大多对每个问题有一个答案,往往将部分足够的背景视为完全不充分。 我们描述一个小规模的公开评价,它分别对三部分问题的每个部分进行评分,对从材料中正确回答但包含附加无根据索赔的部分作出裁定。 在30个案例中(5个情况,6个覆盖模式,每个样本5个),我们测试claude-haiku-4-5和lama3.1:8b。 如果没有明确指示要留在材料内,两个模型就填补了它们自己所了解的大部分空白(无支持的索赔率为0.71和0)。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Assistants that answer from supplied reference material often receive material that covers some parts of a user&#x27;s question and not others. Existing evaluations of insufficient context mostly score one answer per question, and often treat partially sufficient context as simply insufficient. We describe a small, open evaluation that scores each part of a three-part question separately, with a verdict for parts that are answered correctly from the material but carry an added unsupported claim. On 30 cases (five scenarios, six coverage patterns, five samples each) we test claude-haiku-4-5 and llama3.1:8b. Without an explicit instruction to stay within the material, both models fill most gaps from their own knowledge (unsupported-claim rates of 0.71 and 0.90). One added sentence instructing them to answer only from the material brings these rates to 0.00 and 0.06, consistent with prior work. What remains is less visible: answers that strengthen the material, for example turning &quot;many job sites prohibit bots&quot; into a flat &quot;no&quot;. We also report on the judge. Three rounds of blind hand labelling show that this added-claim verdict is the hardest to apply, for the judge and the author alike; RAGAS faithfulness is unsuitable for this task because it penalises correct withholding; and a difference between the two models that is clear under one judge disappears when the judge model is replaced. The dataset, code, hand labels and per-run figures are public.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">ShowNow: Fine-Grained Speculative Streaming for Responsive World Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Yiren Song、Guian Fang、Mike Zheng Shou</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">互动世界模式必须不断生成视频,同时迅速回应用户行动。 仅实时吞吐量就留下了输入延迟的未解决问题:粗体生成块延迟动作更新,输入后生成会给响应路径添加工作. 我们介绍ShowNow,一个精细的投机流线框架,它使用在回放时可用的计算,在输入到来之前准备行动条件期货. 单度承诺使得能够经常作出决定,而可描述的准备涵盖一至八项行动。 我们构建了匹配的多未来数据集,从共同状态中训练替代的续作;共享历史树的执行和融合承诺使其编制工作高效。 单程H100</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Interactive world models must generate video continuously while responding promptly to user actions. Real-time throughput alone leaves input latency unresolved: coarse generation blocks delay action updates, and generation after input adds work to the response path. We introduce ShowNow, a fine-grained speculative streaming framework that uses computation available during playback to prepare action-conditioned futures before input arrives. Single-latent commitment enables frequent decisions, while configurable preparation covers one to eight actions. We construct a matched multi-future dataset to train alternative continuations from a common state; shared-history tree execution and fused commitment make their preparation efficient. These results establish complementary roles for fine-grained commitment and advance preparation, with measured trade-offs in visual fidelity and long-horizon consistency.   <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34051" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">稳定Gated DeltaNet-2:理解和减缓经常状态不稳定</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Stable Gated DeltaNet-2: Understanding and Mitigating Recurrent State Instability</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Xichong Zhang、Xixuan Hao、Zhichen Liu、Le Zhou、Zeyu Zhang、Boyu Chen 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">线性注意通过线性复杂度和固定大小的经常状态,为长序建模提供了高效的解决方案. Gated DeltaNet-2(GDN-2)引入了对擦除和书写的独立频道控制,增强了状态更新的表达力和内容内检索,然而在长文本训练中却受到训练失败的影响. 我们追踪到这些失败, 在消除过程中, 读取和修改方向之间的不对称, 产生非正常的擦除操作器, 其方向收益累积跨越符号, 为了解决这个问题,我们提议采用对称的消除法,确保非扩展状态的传播和衰变调节的剩余书写,以保存令状</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Linear attention offers an efficient solution for long-sequence modeling via linear complexity and fixed-size recurrent states. Gated DeltaNet-2 (GDN-2) introduces independent channel-wise control over erasure and writing, enhancing state-update expressiveness and in-context retrieval, yet it suffers from training failures during long-context training. We trace these failures to the asymmetry between reading and modification directions during erasure, which yields non-normal erase operators whose directional gains accumulate across tokens, triggering exponential state growth even without new writes. To resolve this, we propose , employing symmetric erasure to ensure non-expansive state propagation and decay-regulated residual writing to preserve writing flexibility. Theoretically, we establish an O(T) upper bound on recurrent state growth while retaining chunkwise parallel computation. Experiments on ~1.6B parameter models show that achieves stable training alongside improved long-context retrieval. Compared to GDN-2, it boosts the 128K average RULER score by 6.80 points in the recurrent setting and average accuracy across nine downstream tasks by 0.62 points in the hybrid setting.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33160" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">背景放大:AI放大的新前沿</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Context Scaling: A New Frontier of AI Scaling</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Xipeng Qiu</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">部署的AI的许多失败不是因为推论薄弱,而是因为任务取决于国家与系统行动时可用的背景不匹配。 相关的用户状态,寄存器修订,工具痕迹,传感器读数,或环境变化可能无法获取,扭曲或选择不当. 我们呼吁努力缩小这种瓶颈。 我们在一个由三个预算组成的系统内将一个模式设定为$P \theta(d\mid c)$:训练$B \theta$,决策时间$B d$,以及背景基础设施$B c$. 训练前规模化投资为$\theta$;决策时规模化搜索、改进或核实所提供背景下的决定;环境规模化提高系统获取、代表、交付、评价和持续的能力</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>训练与系统</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Many failures of deployed AI arise not from weak inference but from a mismatch between the state a task depends on and the context available when the system acts. Relevant user state, repository revisions, tool traces, sensor readings, or environment changes may be unavailable, distorted, or poorly selected. We call the effort to reduce this bottleneck context scaling. We frame a model as $P_\theta(d\mid c)$ within a system governed by three budgets: training $B_\theta$, decision-time $B_d$, and context-infrastructure $B_c$. Pretraining scaling invests in $\theta$; decision-time scaling searches, refines, or verifies decisions under supplied context; context scaling improves the system&#x27;s capacity to acquire, represent, deliver, evaluate, and persist deployment state for model use. The three programs interact, but only $B_c$ targets what the system can make available before a decision. This view makes the model-infrastructure pair the unit of analysis. We define effective-context value as a task- and intervention-dependent attribution, separate deployment state from model-visible context, and identify availability, fidelity, selection, and utilization failures. We then organize current mechanisms through a five-operation context lifecycle and propose controlled budget interventions and utility-cost curves for evaluation. The resulting agenda centers on grounded reporting, interaction-data and full-cost infrastructure, and safety and governance for persistent, adaptive systems.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33165" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Autoform Bench:高级数学精益正规化基准</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">AutoformBench: A Lean Formalization Benchmark for Advanced Mathematics</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Charles Arnal、Ahmad Rammal、Niket Patel、Fabian Gloeckle、Bartosz Piotrowski、Amaury Hayat 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">AI系统越来越能够产生机器检查的利恩先进数学结果的证明. 然而,真正的数学形式化并不是孤立的定理证明:它需要建立定义和中间lemmas,将结果与正式的库连接起来. 现有评价往往在Mathlib中已经存在许多支持理论的环境下提供孤立的定理语句. 我们引入了Autoform Bench,这是从24个高级数学来源提取的341个精益4正规化任务的基准. 精益求精的数学家将每个目标报表正式化并进行审计,将相应的精益证留给被评价的智能体. 每一个问题都包含在更广泛的数学发展中:年龄</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>AI systems are increasingly capable of producing machine-checked Lean proofs of advanced mathematical results. Real mathematical formalization, however, is not isolated theorem proving: it requires building the definitions and intermediate lemmas that connect a result to a formal library. Existing evaluations often provide isolated theorem statements in settings where much of this supporting theory is already available in Mathlib. We introduce AutoformBench, a benchmark of 341 Lean 4 formalization tasks drawn from 24 advanced mathematical sources. Lean-experienced mathematicians formalized and audited each target statement, leaving the corresponding Lean proof to the evaluated agent. Each problem is embedded in a broader mathematical development: agents receive the informal proof but must translate it into Lean while constructing the missing definitions, intermediate lemmas, and supporting infrastructure. We compare the performance of frontier models on these problems. We find a largely shared difficulty frontier: success depends less on mathematical domain than on models&#x27; ability to construct missing formal infrastructure.   <p class="paper-authors">Vimal Kumar Kumar、Meghana Kshirsagar、Conor Ryan</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">检索增强生成(RAG)通常选取语义相关性的证据,而遵守敏感长式生成也需要支持明确结构义务的证据。 因此,在语义上相关的检索可能省略了强制性下游条款所需的信息。 我们提议在选择证据之前,先用本体学产生的结构限制和以策略为基础的知识-图关系来补充具体科室的查询。 仅以人工附加说明的证据进行的检索评价表明,在检索预算方面情况持续改善。 按6美元=6美元计算,CA-RAG将精确度从0.5268美元增至0.5692美元,集合召回额从0.5268美元增至0.5692美元。</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) typically optimises evidence selection for semantic relevance, whereas compliance-sensitive long-form generation also requires evidence supporting explicit structural obligations.   <p class="paper-authors">Zi Li、Andr\&#x27;e V. Duarte、Lei Li</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">我们能否将AI产生的审查与那些仅仅用来改写人类判断的AI区分开来? 此外,当审查由不同AI模型和跨科学领域的工作流程产生时,我们能否可靠地这样做? 为此,我们引入了PeerReveal, 这是一种新的探测器,它将其决定依据于从大赦国际对同一文件的审查中得出的审查要求与参考要求之间的知识关系,并辅之以审查一级的文字信号。 我们评价PeerReveal on PeerShift, 这是一套具有挑战性的新型测试,包括5,430份审查,涉及多个领域,包括机器学习、生命科学和社会科学,将纪律审查公约的变异与不同的AI审查-制作机制结合起来。 经常</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Can we distinguish fully AI-generated reviews from those using AI only to rewrite human judgement?   <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33176" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">多模态大语言模型中持续时间的心理物理学</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Psychophysics of Duration Perception in Multimodal Large Language Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Auss Abbood、Zihao Fu、Nigel Collier</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">对语言模型中时间感知的研究主要侧重于文本设置. 然而,多模态模型越来越多地用于需要了解时间流逝的任务,因此其时间感知的限度十分重要。 我们对照人类的基线来衡量时间感知的限度,以弥补这一差距。 从心理物理学出发,我们把持续时间感知作为旋律识别任务来操作,当旋律在减速时变得无法识别时进行测试. 在21个经过测试的模型中,大多数模型无法识别没有标注内容中的例子的著名旋律。 以文中的例子来看,有七首能可靠地识别出旋律,大多数在中位数的人类识别带内达到顶峰. 一般化是有限的</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>cs.CL</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Studies of time perception in language models focus mainly on text-based settings. Yet multimodal models are increasingly used for tasks that require perceiving the passage of time, making the limits of their temporal perception important to understand. We measure the limits of duration perception from audio against a human baseline to address this gap. Drawing from psychophysics, we operationalise duration perception as a melody recognition task, testing when a melody becomes unrecognisable as it is slowed down. Of 21 tested models, most cannot recognise well-known melodies without labelled in-context examples. With in-context examples, seven recognise melodies reliably, most peaking within the median human recognition band. Generalisation is limited: (i) recognition peaks shift with in-context example speed, (ii) melody titles induce prediction bias, and (iii) providing examples with scientific pitch notation does not prevent accuracy declining at long note durations for most models. In humans, the recognition window is a property of the perceiver; in current models, it is largely a property of the prompt.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33307" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">失译,不失信心:衡量和校准多种语文决策模式</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Lost in Translation, Not in Confidence: Benchmarking and Calibrating Multilingual Decision Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Mingzhe Du、Thong Thanh Nguyen、Nguyen Tran Cong Duy、See-Kiong Ng、Luu Anh Tuan</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">决策模型是语言模型进行微调,以返回输入查询的每个选项的概率,哪个路由器和护栏阈值是直接的. 它们建立在多语种基础上,但几乎完全以英语排列。 我们用14种语言对17项任务的25个排名最高的开放量级决定模型进行评估,在平行项目上将每种语言与英语进行比较。 每个模型都失去了每种语言的精度,从高资源语言的约2分到泰米尔语的8分,而信心则只下降一半:英语95QQ精度设定的阈值在泰米尔语中得出90%的精度. 损失的大小由语言和基模设定,而不是由领导板排名设定. 在81个有控制的微调运行中,任务技能通过英语转至o学习.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Decision models are language models fine-tuned to return a probability for each option of a typed query, which routers and guardrails threshold directly. They are built on multilingual bases but ranked almost entirely in English. We evaluate 25 top-ranked open-weight decision models on 17 tasks in 14 languages, comparing each language with English on parallel items. Every model loses accuracy in every language, from about 2 points in high-resource languages to 8 in Tamil, while confidence falls only half as much: a threshold set for 95\% accuracy in English yields 90% in Tamil. The size of the loss is set by the language and the base model rather than by leaderboard rank. In 81 controlled fine-tuning runs, task skill learned in English transfers to other languages and a few thousand target-language items narrow large losses, but neither more data nor full-parameter tuning closes them. Finally, the same test items are answered more accurately in all 23 benchmark--language pairs we compare when an LLM rather than a person translated them, so LLM-translated test sets understate the gap.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33327" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">谁给谁一个想法? 模拟用户助理对话中的思维相互理论</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Who Attributes a Mind to Whom? Mutual Theory of Mind in Simulated User-Assistant Conversation</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Mingxi Yan、Vasudha Varadarajan、Qiaosi Wang、Qing Xiao、Hong Shen、Jodi Forlizzi 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">当用户假设AI聊天员有精神状态时,他们更有可能发展过度依赖性,情感依赖性,在极端情况下,心理伤害或自我伤害. 然而,这些心灵属性是如何在对话中产生的,以及人类或AI因素如何影响这一点仍然未知. 我们研究用户如何使用最小框架来描述心灵:一个具有不同特性的模拟用户与在社会风格和内存上有所不同的助手聊天. 我们还采用了一个信息层面的衡量标准,衡量谁将心灵归属于谁,在四个方向上,用受过训练的编码器检查,并由一个精细的编码器应用,该编码器复制了一位LM法官. 我们的结果显示,在13个特点中,平均有9个提高了模拟用户的水平。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.CL</span><span>cs.HC</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>When users assume that an AI chatbot has mental states, they are more likely to develop over-reliance, emotional dependence, and in extreme cases, psychological harm or self-harm. However, how these mind attributions arise in conversations and how human or AI factors influence this is still unknown. We study how users attribute minds using a minimal framework: a simulated user with different characteristics chats with assistants that differ in social style and memory. We also introduce a message-level measure of who attributes a mind to whom in four directions, checked with trained coders and applied by a fine-tuned encoder that reproduces an LLM judge. Our results show that, on average, nine out of thirteen characteristics raised simulated users&#x27; attribution to the assistant. We found that relational needs such as attachment anxiety raised it as theory predicts, some general personality traits raised it as well, and AI-specific characteristics the least. By contrast, the assistant&#x27;s social style barely changed simulated users&#x27; mind attribution. Instead, sycophantic instructions raised the assistant&#x27;s attribution to itself. Memory raised users&#x27; attribution only with some user models. The model playing the user changed overall attribution and the effects of characteristics, style, and memory. Our framework measures how persona descriptions affect mind attribution in simulation. It gives concrete hypotheses for human studies and shows the need for more consistent user simulators.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33190" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AI 配置图宁的通用决定模型</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">General-Purpose Decision Models for AI Configuration Tuning</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Jingzhi Gong、Jie M. Zhang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">AI配置调试搜索在有限预算下改善AI系统性能的设置. 现有的调制器采用不同的取样和搜索策略,使决策战略的选择对调制质量和成本都至关重要。 我们调查通用决策模式是否能够指导调试,利用Jev作为部署的代表,将结构化的选择和数字分数反馈给不同任务。 为了评估它的帮助,我们将它作为优化器、性能预测器和区域指导器的使用与常规方法和大语言模型(LLM)与八个AI调试任务进行比较。 常规优化器在八项比较中六项获得第一名. 同样,既定的预测数在7个中超过了Jev。 色鲁</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>AI configuration tuning searches for settings that improve the performance of AI systems under a limited budget. Existing tuners use different sampling and search policies, making the choice of decision strategy central to both tuning quality and cost. We investigate whether general-purpose decision models can guide tuning, using Jev as a deployed representative that returns structured choices and numeric scores across diverse tasks. To assess where it helps, we compare its use as an optimizer, performance predictor, and region guider against conventional methods and a large language model (LLM) on eight AI tuning tasks. Conventional optimizers attain rank 1 in six of eight comparisons. Similarly, established predictors outperform Jev in seven. Through region guidance, however, it finds configurations with lower perplexity than those selected by the native tuner, offering a quality gain when additional decision time and API fees are acceptable. These findings yield actionable recommendations on selecting the decision role, retaining conventional predictors, and balancing tuning quality against cost.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33266" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">FrId\&#x27;eo:通过多源证据聚合和异形- 敏锐的 Lexical 校对法兰西新闻</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">FrId\&#x27;eo: French News Outlet Ideology Scoring via Multi-Source Evidence Fusion and Distancing-Aware Lexical Scoring</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Amr Sobhy</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">外向思想是媒体框架、媒体影响和政治传播研究中的标准解释变量,但持续、全生态系统的报导允许它作为分级曝光而不是粗糙的垃圾桶进入。 对法国来说,现有的参考资料要么是宽泛的,要么是非分析性的,要么是分析性的,但数量不多,而且有源有源,我们不知道有哪一个以统一的持续规模提供整个生态系统的覆盖。 FrId\&#x27;eo:据我们所知,法国媒体第一个全生态系统连续的意识形态得分,30家法国新闻网的透明综合得分,将9个独立的证据家庭与经验主义的巴耶式缩小结构。 对于文字家族,我们引入对称的平原/quali</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Outlet ideology is a standard explanatory variable in media framing, media effects, and political communication research, but continuous, ecosystem-wide coverage lets it enter as graded exposure rather than a coarse bin. For France, existing references are either broad but non-analytic or analytic but sparse and source-specific, and none we are aware of offers ecosystem-wide coverage on a unified continuous scale. We contribute FrId\&#x27;eo: to our knowledge the first ecosystem-wide continuous ideology scores for the French press, a transparent composite score for 30 French news outlets, fusing nine independent families of evidence with empirical-Bayes-style shrinkage toward a structural prior. For the text-based family we introduce symmetric plain/qualified scoring, which separates the plain use of ideologically loaded vocabulary from its qualified (distanced) use on both sides, a distinction bag-of-words methods miss. The resulting scale runs from L&#x27;Humanit\&#x27;e on the far left to Valeurs actuelles on the far right; the far-right pole is near-indistinguishable and CNews ties Le Figaro. Because the text family reads current articles, it also captures editorial change that static sources miss, such as the Journal du Dimanche&#x27;s sharp rightward shift after its 2023 ownership change. The ordering is robust to perturbation and to dropping any single family, and agrees with held-out Media Bias Fact Check labels. We release the scores, confidence intervals, and replication code.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Conversations are central to how people exchange information, share experiences, and develop collective understanding. Yet, across conversational settings, discussions often suffer from uneven participation, unanswered contributions, and a narrow range of perspectives.   <p class="paper-authors">Michael Hemenway、Edward W. Wolfe</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">基于Transformer的自动书写评分模型可以与人类的分数达成强烈一致,同时为与其预测相关的响应特征提供有限的透明度. 这项研究评价了一种基于特征的事后解释方法,在解释特征重要性之前就确定了解释性忠诚。 对6年级和8年级三个书写提示的英语语言艺术反应进行了分析,每个迅速和单独的DeBERTa-v3基础表达和公约研究模型有12 700个答复。 每个答复都有61个可解释的语言和写作质量特征。 四种办法 -- -- 理论驱动的复合材料、等级特征组合、探索性因素分析</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>综合</span><span>cs.CL</span><span>cs.LG</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Transformer-based automated writing scoring models can achieve strong agreement with human scores while providing limited transparency into the response characteristics associated with their predictions. This study evaluated a feature-based post-hoc explanation approach in which explanatory fidelity was established before feature importance was interpreted. English Language Arts responses from three writing prompts in Grades 6 and 8 were analyzed, with about 12,700 responses per prompt and separate DeBERTa-v3-base research models for Expression and Conventions. Each response was represented by 61 interpretable linguistic and writing-quality features. Four approaches - theory-driven composites, hierarchical feature groupings, exploratory factor analysis, and Random Forest surrogates - were compared in their ability to reproduce DeBERTa predictions. Random Forest surrogates produced the largest observed R2 values across all six prompt-by-trait conditions, with held-out R2 values from .81 to .95. SHAP analyses showed substantial overlap in high-importance features across traits, with 75% to 90% of the top 20 features shared. Trait differentiation occurred mainly through relative feature weighting: Expression showed greater importance for lexical and conceptual indicators, whereas Conventions showed greater importance for sentence-level and syntactic-control indicators. Under researcher-defined mappings, rubric correspondence was 90%-95% for Expression and 12%-18% for Conventions. These percentages are descriptive and conditional on the mappings and available linguistic features. The findings support a fidelity-first framework for examining apparent scoring priorities while distinguishing post-hoc evidence about model behavior from direct explanation of transformer internals.</p></details>
