It requires recognizing revisited places despite viewpoint changes, distinguishing them from similar-looking rooms, and updating memories as environments change. Yet existing evaluations often leave physical place identity across separated visits implicit. We introduce ReSpaceBench, a benchmark comprising 3,244 questions across 590 synthetic and real-home scenes, organized into 9 tasks spanning room association, persistent state tracking, and downstream reasoning.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Prompt enhancement (PE) refines concise user prompts into detailed descriptions to improve the compositional aesthetics of generated images while preserving user intent. A common practice constructs supervised fine-tuning (SFT) pairs by deriving short, user-like prompts from dense captions, implicitly assuming that captions that improve image generation also provide effective supervision. In this paper, however, we uncover a counterintuitive finding: PE models trained on caption pairs with substantial generation gains perform comparably to those trained on pairs with only marginal gains. We argue that dense captions can impose redundant supervision by requiring PE models to reproduce image-specific details weakly related to user intent. Motivated by this discrepancy, we introduce Prune to Expand (P2E), a simple text-only method that refines dense-caption supervision by selectively removing redundant sentences under a per-example deletion bound while preserving explicit user requirements and informative visual structure. Extensive experiments under identical training configurations demonstrate that P2E improves alignment with human preferences and visual quality over conventional dense-caption SFT across diverse image categories and visual generators.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">身份匹配询问两个记录是否表示同一身份,然而答案取决于目标颗粒性. 两个记录可能在基数级别上匹配,但在变体级别上并不匹配:它们共享一个基础设计,但颜色或大小等属性不同. 最终匹配准确性并不单独描述所涉能力。 为了研究这一问题,我们把电子商务作为一个挑战性的现实世界领域。 我们提出了PURE, 即一个诊断性基准,用于多重力多模态身份匹配,涵盖四个能力层面:感知、理解、理性和循证判断。 基准包括14项任务的1 457个多重选择问题,来自现实世界的匹配对并经过核实</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Identity matching asks whether two records denote the same identity, yet the answer depends on the target granularity. Two records may match at the base level but not at the variant level: they share an underlying design while differing in attributes such as color or size.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Vision-language-action (VLA) models enable unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to follow natural-language instructions, yet still fail frequently in closed-loop flight.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Full-duplex interaction models exhibit strong conversational agency, enabling natural, low-latency turn-taking, backchannels, and interruptions. However, completing tasks further requires task agency: timely tool invocation and argument revision following user corrections. Frontend-backend agents separate initiation from execution: the frontend decides whether and what to request, while the backend determines how. Execution can be offloaded, but unsent requests cannot be executed, and incomplete ones can misdirect the backend. On Full-Duplex-Bench v3 (FDB v3), most failures of Realtime-Venus-Audio + Harness in our streaming pipeline occur before a domain tool executes. We introduce Duopra, a full-duplex interaction model that learns task execution alongside conversational control. Duopra treats the backend as a tool, accepting a self-contained natural-language request. Direct calls and backend delegation share an action interface and requirements for faithful initiation and correct execution. Its three-stage training combines protocol SFT, full-duplex RL rewarding timely and complete execution, and projector-only repair of talker conditioning after thinker updates. With the same backend, Pass@1 rises from 4.7% to 44.7%; Duopra achieves 47.7% calling tools directly (26.0% after SFT) and 49.3% with a DeepSeek backend. It improves three FDB v1.0 takeover rates over the base model. These results show that task agency can coexist with key conversational agency.</p></details>
