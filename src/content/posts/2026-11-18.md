  <p class="paper-original-title">LogicEye: Agent Driven Multimodal Dataset for Interpretable Ophthalmic Diagnosis</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Jiajie Mu、Lu Chen、Zheyun Qin、Xiaoming Xi、Lishan Qiao、Xiushan Nie 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">临床上采用眼科人工智能仍然受到限制,因为可解释性低,而且缺乏详细说明的多模态数据。 大多数现有的数据集只提供粗糙的疾病标签,而没有差别诊断所需的颗粒病理结果. 我们介绍LogicEye$^{Agent}$,这是一个双库推论和核查智能体商,通过联合核查积极的包容征兆和负面排除标准来模仿临床推理. 我们使用逻辑Eye$ \ Agent}$, 来管理逻辑Eye$\ Data}$, 一个由804 461 Fundus 和 OCT 图像组成的大型多模态数据集, 配有结构化的病理报告, 覆盖38种疾病和29种子类型, 通过多模态大语言模型的联合验证保证了质量。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Clinical adoption of ophthalmic Artificial Intelligence remains limited by low interpretability and a lack of finely annotated multimodal data. Most existing datasets provide only coarse disease labels without the granular pathological findings required for differential diagnosis. We present LogicEye$^{Agent}$, a dual-pool inference and verification agent that emulates clinical reasoning by jointly verifying positive inclusion signs and negative exclusion criteria. Using LogicEye$^{Agent}$, we curate LogicEye$^{Data}$, a large-scale multimodal dataset of 804,461 fundus and OCT images paired with structured pathological reports spanning 38 diseases and 29 subtypes, with quality assured through joint verification by multimodal large language models and clinical experts. Building on this dataset, we further develop LogicEye$^{Bench}$, which incorporates a category-constrained loss for hierarchical disease recognition and report generation. Grounding coarse labels into verified visual findings effectively curtails diagnostic hallucinations and establishes a solid foundation for interpretable vision-language modeling in ophthalmology. The code is available at:https://github.com/Eden35/LogicEye-Agent-Driven-Multimodal-Dataset-for-Interpretable-Ophthalmic-Diagnosis.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">MedJev: A System One Vision-Language Decision Model for Fast and Effective Medical Image Understanding</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Fan Zhang、Hao Wu、Jinyang Wu、Hongyu Qu、Zheng Lian、Dongfu Yin 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">具有视觉语言模型(VLMs)的医学图像理解通常依赖于自动生成推理和回答符号. 这些不必要的符号使推论成本尺度与响应长度相配合,并迫使答案从自由格式文本中解析,从而发生可避免的延迟和决定错误. 为此,我们引入了MedJev,这是一个系统,它是一个视觉语言决策模型,它可以绘制医疗图像、问题和候选者直接回答一个单一的多模态前传中的决定,而没有任何自动生成过程。 对于快速推论,MedJev移除了上语言层和基因输出路径,同时保留了完整的视觉路径和所有视觉符号. 在这个缩短的骨干上,一个装饰</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>推理</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Medical image understanding with vision-language models (VLMs) typically relies on autoregressively generating reasoning and answer tokens. These unnecessary tokens make the inference cost scale with the response length and force the answer to be parsed from free-form text, incurring avoidable latency and decision errors. To this end, we introduce MedJev, a system one vision-language decision model that maps medical images, questions, and candidate answers directly to decisions in a single multimodal forward pass, without any autoregressive generation process. For fast inference, MedJev removes the upper language layers and the generative output pathway, while keeping the full vision pathway and all visual tokens. On this shortened backbone, a decision head scores each candidate by pretrained query--key matching and refines the scores with a lightweight multilayer perceptron (MLP) residual, coupling the pretrained matching structure with task-specific nonlinear discrimination. Built on Qwen3.5-4B, MedJev-4B improves both accuracy and speed over the original model on each of four medical image classification and visual question answering benchmarks, with an absolute accuracy gain of 7.93\% and a 6.04times end-to-end speedup. Further comparison with 20 cutting-edge models shows that MedJev-4B achieves state-of-the-art performance and inference speed among 4B--9B general and medical VLMs, indicating that direct multimodal decision making is an effective route to fast and effective medical image understanding.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">随着人工智能智能体越来越多地部署在现实世界的应用中,并承担社会角色、动态社会智能,不断认识多模态提示、推断潜在认知状态、通过综合行动进行干预并最终实现社会目标的能力变得至关重要。 然而,现有的评价在很大程度上将社会智能分割成基于文字的抽象,静态推理任务,或包含缺乏社会动态的模拟器. 这些方法未能抓住人类真正互动的多模态、持续和人际复杂性。 为了弥补这一差距,我们引入了 &quot; 生命世界 &quot; (ALIVE-World),这是一个体现的模拟环境,支持动态的多模态互动,包括以自我为中心的视觉、语言,</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>cs.MA</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>As AI agents are increasingly deployed in real-world applications and take on social roles, dynamic social intelligence, the ability to continuously perceive multimodal cues, infer latent cognitive states, intervene through embodied actions, and ultimately achieve social goals, has become essential. However, existing evaluations largely fragment social intelligence into text-based abstractions, static reasoning tasks, or embodied simulators that lack social dynamics. These approaches fail to capture the multimodal, continuous, and interpersonal complexity of authentic human interactions. To bridge this gap, we introduce ALIVE-World, an embodied simulation environment supporting dynamic multimodal interactions encompassing egocentric vision, language, and non-verbal action. To provide socially grounded interactive feedback, we populate this environment with counterpart agents driven by a profile-conditioned, dual-system (intuitive-affective and logical-cognitive) architecture. Building upon this platform, we propose ALIVE-Bench to transform the assessment of social intelligence into a trackable quantitative framework. By deeply leveraging underlying environmental states and agents&#x27; internal variables, ALIVE-Bench systematically evaluates Vision-Language Model (VLM) agents across four critical dimensions: perception, understanding, influence, and outcome. Extensive evaluations reveal that dynamic social intelligence is fundamentally multi-dimensional and scenario-dependent. We identify a critical bottleneck between upstream perception and downstream social intervention, exposing VLM vulnerabilities in fine-grained proxemic awareness, adaptive intervention, and multi-party coordination. By uncovering these limitations and the diverse interaction strategies they produce, this benchmark provides actionable insights for the future behavioral alignment of multimodal agents.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21791" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">常规记分中心</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Regularized Score Centering</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Hao Bai、Chenlu Ye、Xuanchang Zhang、Aviral Kumar、Tong Zhang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">同步的强化学习会增加训练的吞吐量,但来自僵化策略的数据以及训练和推论之间的数字不匹配会破坏学习的稳定. 为了稳定策略外的学习,记分中心(SC)消除奖励基线对预期更新的影响. 我们的梯度诊断显示与重要采样(IS)相比,与SC相比,我们的极端梯度较小,但当学习者远离生成数据的行为策略时,SC仍然可能失败. 为了限制这种策略不匹配,我们引入了常规分数中心(RSC),增加了反向的Kullback-Leibler(KL)罚则,以鼓励学习者的邻接分配与行为策略的邻接分配保持接近. RSC 是否工作</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Asynchronous reinforcement learning increases training throughput, but data from stale policies and numerical mismatch between training and inference can destabilize learning. To stabilize off-policy learning, score centering (SC) removes the effect of reward baselines on the expected update. Our gradient diagnostics show less extreme gradients with SC than with importance sampling (IS), but SC can still fail when the learner moves far from the behavior policy that generated the data. To limit this policy mismatch, we introduce Regularized Score Centering (RSC), which adds a reverse Kullback-Leibler (KL) penalty to encourage the learner&#x27;s next-token distribution to stay close to the behavior policy&#x27;s next-token distribution. RSC works with or without truncated importance sampling (TIS), which caps token importance weights. Our theoretical analysis supports this approach, showing that KL regularization improves convergence guarantees. We evaluate dense and mixture-of-experts models on mathematical reasoning and agentic tasks, including online shopping, tool use, games, and fully asynchronous software engineering. RSC with TIS substantially improves sample efficiency across all agentic benchmarks, attaining higher training rewards with the same number of sampled responses. In final evaluations, it achieves top performance across all six agentic tasks, improving by 14.3% on AppWorld and reaching 9.9 times the strongest baseline&#x27;s score on BabyAI.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Guangzhi Sun、Zhonghao Zhai、Yixuan Li、Chao Zhang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">多模态大语言模型(LLMs)使有能力的计算机使用智能体能够感知图形用户界面,比指令更合理,并执行动作. 然而,从视频演示中学习的现有方法通常将视频转化为基于截图的动作轨迹,而不是直接利用其丰富的视听信息. 我们引入VEX(Video as EXperience),这个框架使用视听理解来统一从外部演示中学习,并反思一个特工自己的行为. 该智能体从包含视觉演示和口语解释的教程视频中提取程序指导,执行任务,并通过重播验证其进展。</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Multimodal large language models (LLMs) have enabled capable computer-use agents that perceive graphical user interfaces, reason over instructions, and execute actions.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large Multimodal Reasoning Models (LMRMs) extend large language models to heterogeneous modalities, demonstrating strong potential across a wide range of open-ended tasks.   <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19559" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ResWAM:为世界行动模态学习什么变化,而不是留下什么</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">ResWAM: Learning What Changes, Not What Stays, for World-Action Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Chongyu Fan、Gaowen Liu、Ramana Rao Kompella、Jason Corso、Sijia Liu</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">世界行动模型用未来的预测来增强视觉语言行动策略,以便策略能够预测其行动将如何影响实体世界。 现有的WAM监督未来完整的观测. 然而,观测通常在某个动作块上变化不大,因此未来的损失主要是复制静态背景内容,而不是学习与任务相关的机器人和物体运动,限制策略学习。 此外,现有的WAM依赖于大型的视频生成模型或独立的世界模型,发生阻碍闭路部署的重型训练和推论计算. 为了改进策略业绩,我们提议进行视觉残余学习,只监督从目前框架到Th的特征变化。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>World-action models (WAMs) augment vision-language-action policies (VLAs) with future prediction so that the policy can anticipate how its actions will affect the physical world. Existing WAMs supervise complete future observations. However, observations typically change little over an action chunk, so the future loss is dominated by copying static background content rather than learning task-relevant robot and object motion, limiting policy learning. Moreover, existing WAMs rely on large video generation models or separate world models, incurring heavy training and inference compute that hinders closed-loop deployment. To improve policy performance, we propose visual residual learning, supervising only the feature change from the current frame to the future frame in a frozen DINOv3 space. Compared with complete future features, this residual learning target yields a higher feature rank with richer and more diverse representations of scene changes, and spatially concentrates on the robot arm and manipulated objects rather than the background. To further improve computational efficiency, we present ResWAM, a residual world-action model with 2.3B parameters in total, including a lightweight 0.5B dual-branch DiT that jointly trains an action branch and a residual branch via conditional flow matching. Without robot pretraining (i.e., pretraining on external robot action trajectories beyond the benchmark demonstrations), ResWAM achieves top average success rates on LIBERO-Plus (81.1%), RoboTwin 2.0 (93.8%), and RoboDojo (9.40% success, 13.41 score) at the lowest training (2.7-4.0 TFLOPs) and inference compute (1.0-1.4 TFLOPs), and leads real-robot Open, Put, and Stack (80.0% average, +10.0% over π_{0.5}) at 92.7 ms latency.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18905" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">准确的询问, 明智地假设: 大语言模型中模糊解析的交互式评价</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Ask Precisely, Assume Wisely: Interactive Evaluation of Ambiguity Resolution in Large Language Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Avik Dutta、Aayush Kumar、Zahra Ashktorab、Hosein Hasanbeig、Austin Z. Henley、Gustavo Soares</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">模糊性是人类-AI相互作用的核心挑战,模型必须决定何时作出假设,何时寻求澄清。 现有的模棱两可的评价办法主要侧重于使用静态衡量尺度的多重解释的涵盖范围,对一个模型如何有效地通过互动实现单一的用户意向解释提供了有限的见解。 我们采用了一个基于偏好的模拟用户框架,用于对模糊性分辨率进行有控制的多回合评价,将模拟反应严格建立在用户混淆偏好的基础上,以避免幻觉和过度披露。 与此同时,我们界定了三种可观察到的不满信号 -- -- 错误的假设、重复的澄清和撤销</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Ambiguity is a core challenge in human--AI interaction, where models must decide when to make assumptions and when to seek clarification. Existing approaches to ambiguity evaluation largely focus on coverage over multiple interpretations using static metrics, offering limited insight into how efficiently a model converges toward a single user-intended interpretation through interaction. We introduce a preference-grounded simulated user framework for controlled, multi-turn evaluation of ambiguity resolution, grounding simulator responses strictly in user disambiguation preferences to avoid hallucination and over-disclosure. Alongside this, we define three observable dissatisfaction signals --- incorrect assumptions, repeating clarifications, and irrelevant clarifications --- that characterize interaction quality beyond task accuracy. Our analysis reveals a clear trade-off: models that frequently seek clarification tend to over-clarify and prolong interactions, while assumption-driven models are more prone to misaligning with user intent. We demonstrate that system prompts optimized on these dissatisfaction signals achieve a Pareto-optimal balance across all three metrics, reducing overall error rates by up to 7.65%, underscoring the value of our framework in designing agents that ask precisely and assume wisely.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Alignment-trained large language model (LLM) agents are increasingly deployed in autonomous, multi-round settings where ethical compliance can come into direct tension with task success.   <p class="paper-authors">Jai Jain、Chihyo Ahn、Yong Liu、Charles Chen、Duc N. M Hoang、Ajay Kumar Jaiswal 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">边缘-云层投机解码将自动递归起草移动到边缘设备,而更大的云模型同时验证草稿令牌,因此边缘起草和云层验证在同步的草稿验证时间表下重叠. 然而,除了低纬度本地网络之外,该时间表的下一次核查请求只能在之前的回复到达后才能发出,因此每轮仍然会等待一个完整的往返,而吞吐量随着网络RTT的增长而下降. 为了弥补这一损失,我们提出了SpecRelay,一种边缘-云LLM推论系统,它从两个层面处理这一成本:当需要云量核实时,它分期摊还往返旅行,当局部推理足够时,则避免往返旅行. 对于带云的查询,一个同步验证p</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Edge-cloud speculative decoding moves autoregressive drafting to an edge device while a larger cloud model verifies draft tokens in parallel, so that edge drafting and cloud verification overlap under a synchronous draft-verify schedule. Beyond low-latency local networks, however, the next verification request of that schedule can be sent only after the previous reply arrives, so every round still waits out a full round trip, and throughput falls as the network RTT grows. To recover this loss, we present SpecRelay, an edge-cloud LLM inference system that addresses this cost at two levels: it amortizes round trips when cloud verification is necessary and avoids them when local reasoning suffices.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used for tutoring and professional knowledge retrieval, yet safety evaluations still focus mainly on overt harms such as toxicity while overlooking benevolent failure modes.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>The computational architecture of human word production -- whether semantic-lexical retrieval and phonological encoding constitute dissociable processing stages -- has been debated for decades using hand-designed models with small vocabularies.   <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19710" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">共同学习事实和反事实世界,供因果前期数据配置网络使用</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Joint Learning of Factual and Counterfactual Worlds for Causal Prior-data Fitted Networks</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Furkan Danisman、Luke Zhang、Zhijing Jin、Dehan Kong</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">估计治疗效果是许多学科科学的核心,需要反事实推理来解释替代世界. 然而,在现实世界中,每个单位只有在现实世界下才能显示其结果。 因此,任何接受过真实数据训练的模型都不可能共同了解事实和反事实结果。 因此,这个问题只能在合成环境中进行,两个世界都可以产生。 近期在前期数据配置网络(PFNs)方面取得的进展表明,模型事先经过训练的纯合成数据可以转移到现实世界的预测. 从那时起,这一范式一直延续到因果估计。 尽管有这种完全合成的设置, 现有的因果PFN 将现实世界的束缚带入他们的火车</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>stat.ML</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Estimating treatment effects is central to science across many disciplines and requires counterfactual reasoning about alternative worlds. In the real world, however, each unit reveals its outcome only under the factual world. Thus, the factual and counterfactual outcomes can never be jointly learned by any model trained on real data. The problem is therefore tractable only in synthetic settings, where both worlds can be generated. Recent progress on prior-data fitted networks (PFNs) has shown that models pre-trained purely on synthetic data can transfer to real-world prediction. This paradigm has since carried over to causal effect estimation. Despite this fully synthetic setup, existing causal PFNs carry the real-world constraint into their training objective, confining learning to a single world&#x27;s outcome per unit. The dependence between the two worlds is thereby lost, and the density of the treatment effect relies by default on independence, which is implausible in practice. We therefore propose joint learning, a training setup in which a causal PFN learns the joint distribution of the factual and counterfactual outcomes of each unit. From the joint distribution, we obtain both the response and the individual treatment effect densities, and at inference, we apply optimal transport across units to obtain an approximation of the average treatment effect density. Ours is therefore the first causal PFN approach to provide all three densities together while accounting for the cross-world dependence. We apply joint learning and optimal transport to existing causal PFNs and evaluate it across semi-synthetic, synthetic, and real benchmarks spanning diverse causal scenarios. The jointly trained models significantly improve upon their baselines in point estimation and uncertainty quantification in most settings, indicating that joint learning is a preferable training setup for causal PFNs.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Ke Sun、Kevin Yuchen Ma、Yike Zhang、Yanzhe Chen、Mike Zheng Shou</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">边疆多模态模型现在可以控制来自图像的机器人零镜头,然而许多操纵任务依赖于图像无法显示的接触力. 将武力带入只限API的冷冻模型,带来了两个挑战. 首先,接触进化速度远快于API模型所能决定的速度,因此系统必须关闭模型下方的强制环. 第二,冷冻模型不能解释原始力量流,所以必须用模型能够解释的形式还原力量. 因此,我们介绍ForceHarness, 一种让冷冻模型在不进行演示或微调的情况下使用武力的绳索。 单从腕感应器或联合扭矩校准手力后,ForceHarness用调节力的自我终结力原始来应对第一个挑战.</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Frontier multimodal models can now control robots zero-shot from images, yet many manipulation tasks depend on contact forces that images cannot show.   <p class="paper-original-title">Large Language Models for AI Infrastructure</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Bolin Shen、Shaoyi Huang、Yushun Dong</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大语言模型(LLMS)越来越多地用于设计,优化,运行AI训练和服务背后的基础设施,然而文献在硬件设计,机器学习系统,电子设计自动化,以及高性能计算方面仍然支离破碎. 我们对LLM协助的AI基础设施在整个生命周期进行全面调查,从加速器设计和内核工程到汇编、部署、操作、数据管理和能源优化。 我们用主要资源整理LLM管理的文献:以人工为中心计算文物的方法、以操作为中心部署背景和系统状态的方法,以及以资源为中心的数据和能源方法。 这些分类</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used to design, optimize, and operate the infrastructure behind AI training and serving, yet the literature remains fragmented across hardware design, machine learning systems, electronic design automation, and high-performance computing. We organize the literature by the primary resource the LLM manages: artifact-centric methods for computational artifacts, operational-centric methods for deployment context and system state, and resource-centric methods for data and energy. This comparison reveals where LLM-driven methods yield consistent gains, how reported gains in workload orchestration depend on the evaluation environment, from simulators to real clusters and production, and where evidence is still lacking. Together, the taxonomy, lifecycle perspective, and quantitative evaluation provide an evidence-grounded reference for LLM-driven engineering of AI infrastructure.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">VidEngram: Agentic Long-Term Video Understanding via Structured Hierarchical Memory</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Zinuo Cheng、Yueqian Lin、Zhongxi Wang、Hai &quot;Helen&quot; Li、Yiran Chen</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">长视频问题回答是浪费和损失的,因为多模态模型必须重新编码每个查询的同一视频:上下文限制了分散的取样或截断,以后的问题则没有可重复使用的代表性。 我们介绍VidEngram,一种智能体视频QA系统,它一次在摄入时就构建了持久的层次记忆,并回答后来从记忆中提出的问题. VidEngram与Qwen2.5-Omni共同将视频和音频标题,将片段记忆整合为插曲和实体层,并使用仅文本的智能体规划器,并配有专门的检索工具来生成被基于的答案. 网络接口使得这一过程可以检查:用户可以监视内存形成,检查检索痕迹,验证视频pl中引用的证据</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Long-video question answering is wasteful and lossy when a multimodal model must re-encode the same video for every query: context limits force sparse sampling or truncation, and no reusable representation remains for later questions. Because perception is paid once, subsequent queries use low-cost text-only planning.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18668" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UMM-JEPA:通过蒙面式下级代表预示统一多模态模型的基因预示</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">UMM-JEPA: Generative Pretraining via Masked Next-Scale Representation Prediction for Unified Multimodal Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Yucheng Zhou、Yunjia Xi、Di Yin、Jianbing Shen</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">统一的多模态模型可以支持视觉理解和生成,但基因训练不一定能增进理解。 我们争辩说,这种限制可能是由于代代相传的代表制与用于理解的观念代表制不匹配。 常规生成目标主要优化以合成为导向的空间,如离散视觉编码或基因潜伏. 因此,我们引入了UMM-JEPA,它将生成重新定型为对连续的视觉理解表达的渐进预测. 该模型构建了从粗细到细细的多级代表等级,而像素渲染则与代表预训练脱钩,仅在战后附加.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Unified multimodal models can support both visual understanding and generation, yet generative training does not necessarily improve understanding. We argue that this limitation may arise from a mismatch between the representation targeted by generation and the perceptual representation used for understanding. Conventional generation objectives primarily optimize synthesis-oriented spaces such as discrete visual codes or generative latents. We therefore introduce UMM-JEPA, which reformulates generation as progressive prediction of continuous visual-understanding representations. The model constructs a multi-scale representation hierarchy from coarse to fine, while pixel rendering is decoupled from representation pretraining and attached only afterward. Controlled comparisons show that the representation-prediction objective accounts for most of the understanding improvement, while a data-matched control leaves the group means nearly unchanged. UMM-JEPA reaches a 16-benchmark visual mean of 51.71, against 46.36 for the understanding-only Qwen-VL baseline, and the text mean also rises. Compared with discrete-code and latent-flow generation objectives, perceptual representation prediction yields stronger and more consistent transfer to understanding. Linear probes and visual-evidence interventions further reveal richer object and spatial structure and stronger use of image-specific information. These results suggest that generation can benefit understanding when it directly supervises the perceptual representations that understanding consumes.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18639" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SynthDCB: 可缩放设备控制世界蒸馏基准</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">SynthDCB: World Distillation for Scalable Device Control Benchmarking</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Zichen Zhu、Dingye Liu、Nan Jiang、Danyang Zhang、Hang Zheng、Shijie Zheng 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">扩大设备控制评价要求扩大与世界智能体互动和必须完成的任务。 我们引入了SynthDCB,这是通过世界蒸馏制成的环境意识和装置控制可扩展的基准. 世界蒸馏法将关于设备行为,相互作用约束,以及环境影响的知识转化为模块化可执行组件,这些组件由具有明确状态,行动和时间动态的互动世界组成. 统一的能力表示将工具发现、世界执行和任务综合联系起来,使新的能力能够通过共享的建设管道进入评价。 SynthDCB 即时处理家用、汽车和电话域,114个设备类,1 166个真菌</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.LG</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Scaling device-control evaluation requires scaling both the worlds agents interact with and the tasks they must solve. We introduce SynthDCB, a scalable benchmark for environment awareness and device control built through world distillation. World distillation transforms knowledge of device behaviors, interaction constraints, and environmental effects into modular executable components, which are composed into interactive worlds with explicit states, actions, and temporal dynamics. A unified capability representation connects tool discovery, world execution, and task synthesis, allowing new capabilities to enter evaluation through a shared construction pipeline. SynthDCB instantiates Home, Car, and Phone domains with 114 device classes, 1,166 functions, and 95 events, supporting five task families with 32 subtypes. The stateful design enables task validation and agent evaluation through state correctness and tool-call traces. We evaluate seven models on 2,560 test tasks; the best achieves 68.2% Pass@1. Error analysis reveals challenges in answer delivery, feasibility diagnosis, and temporal coordination. Construction experiments demonstrate scalability across domains and token efficiency: world distillation reduces token consumption by 0.9-66.0% and 46.2-92.5% relative to Codex with low and xhigh reasoning effort, respectively, while achieving broader capability coverage. SynthDCB makes executable world construction the basis of scalable device-control benchmarking, connecting an expanding space of capabilities and interactions to verifiable agent evaluation.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">HIMX:基于定制的HIFLOAT4表示的混合格式矩阵乘法</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">HIMX: mixed-format matrix multiplication based on customized HIFLOAT4 representations</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Zhiyuan Zhang、Yanzhao Li、Hui Dong、Sifan Cheng、Jingwei Zhang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">4位重活化量化(W4A4)是高效大语言模型(LLM)推论的重要方法. 最近的等级格式如HiFloat4(HIF4)通过多层次的缩放,改进了4位量的表示范围. 然而,HIF4中相同的当地mantissa代表可能并不同样适合重量和激活,因为重量和激活显示出明显的分布特征. 为了消除这一局限性,我们提议在HIF4的分级规模结构基础上建立一个混合模式框架。 我们的关键观察是,两种HIF4兼容的曼提萨表示,S1P2和E2M1,表现出互补的量化行为: 密度较大的S1P2格式为集中的Wei提供了更高的分辨率.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.LG</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>4-bit weight-activation quantization (W4A4) is an important approach for efficient large language model (LLM) inference. Recent hierarchical formats such as HiFloat4 (HIF4) improve the representational range of 4-bit quantization through multi-level scaling. However, the same local mantissa representation in HIF4 may not be equally suitable for weights and activations, which exhibit distinct distribution characteristics. To address this limitation, we propose HiMX, a mixed-mantissa framework built upon the hierarchical scaling structure of HIF4. Our key observation is that the two HIF4-compatible mantissa representations, S1P2 and E2M1, exhibit complementary quantization behavior: the denser S1P2 format provides higher resolution for concentrated weight distributions, while the wider-spaced E2M1 format provides complementary quantization characteristics for activation tensors. HiMX enables operand-tailored mantissa representations while converting heterogeneous mantissas into fixed-point integer operands with operand-specific widths, allowing different operand formats to share a unified integer multiply-accumulate (MAC) array. Block-level analysis on Qwen3-8B shows that HIF4-S1P2 is better suited for weights while HIF4 E2M1 is better suited for activations, and their mixed-format combination achieves lower output MSE than homogeneous configurations. Across multiple benchmarks, HiMX reduces the average end-to-end accuracy loss from 2.38 to 1.77 points on Qwen3-8B and from 2.23 to 1.85 points on Qwen3-30B-A3B, demonstrating the potential benefit of heterogeneous mantissa representations for W4A4 LLM inference.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18648" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">重新思考离线在线策略扭曲:从Scalar优势到分配监督</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Rethinking Offline On-Policy Distillation: From Scalar Advantages to Distributional Supervision</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Qi Sun、Siyue Zhang、Tingyu Song、Yilun Zhao、Chen Zhao</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">策略蒸馏(OPD)通过对学生生成的轨迹的象征性反馈来转移教师的能力,但反复评价与教师一起推出的新版本在计算上是昂贵的. Lightning OPD等离线方法通过在固定的参考推开上缓存记录的符号的教师日志概率来降低这一成本. 通过将这个想法推广到一个多回合智能体搜索设置,我们发现,它始终没有很好地表现同一老师训练的SFT起点. 根据经验,我们观察到比数学推理和训练后分布平坦的教师参考不匹配,理论上表明,对固定推出的标尺优势监督认可了输出平坦的解决方案。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.LG</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>On-policy distillation (OPD) transfers teacher capabilities through token-level feedback on student-generated trajectories, but repeatedly evaluating fresh rollouts with the teacher is computationally expensive. Offline methods such as Lightning OPD reduce this cost by caching recorded tokens&#x27; teacher log-probabilities on fixed reference rollouts. By extending the idea to a multi-turn agentic search setup, we find that it consistently underperforms the SFT starting point trained by the same teacher. We empirically observe greater teacher--reference mismatch than in mathematical reasoning and post-training distributional flattening, and theoretically show that scalar advantage supervision on fixed rollouts admits solutions with flattened output distributions under suitable conditions. Motivated by these findings, we propose Distributional On-Policy Distillation (DistOPD), a simple yet effective offline OPD method that retains candidate-token probabilities from both reference and teacher at selected positions and aggregates the remaining parts into a residual token. On agentic search, DistOPD achieves performance comparable to online OPD while improving student--teacher alignment and using training GPU-hours comparable to Lightning OPD.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18693" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Swarm Shepherd:确保多剂生态系统防止持久的潜在妥协</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Swarm Shepherd: Securing Multi-Agent Ecosystems Against Persistent Latent Compromise</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Wuyang Zhang、Shichao Pei</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">智能体生态系统正在形成一个新的平台层,其中智能体物保持持续的内存,从共享的知识库中检索,通过工具界面执行,并通过共享状态协调. 这些能力造成了一个安全的问题,每回合防御都错过了:妥协可以在可见失败之前在会话,智能体和系统层之间积累. 我们把这个威胁定义为 Agentic Sleeper, 一个系统层面的对手, 它首先行为合法, 获得路由信任, 我们根据部分可观察性来进行防御 并提出Swarm Shepherd 这个系统层面的防御框架 以一个用于预警的推断过滤器为中心</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.MA</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Agentic ecosystems are emerging as a new platform layer in which agents maintain persistent memory, retrieve from shared knowledge bases, execute through tool interfaces, and coordinate through shared states. These capabilities create a security problem that per-turn defenses miss: compromise can accumulate across sessions, agents, and system layers before visible failure. We define this threat as Agentic Sleeper, a system-level adversary that first behaves legitimately, gains routing trust, and later activates across memory, agent logic, and infrastructure. We formulate defense as inference under partial observability and propose Swarm Shepherd, a system-level defense framework centered on an inference filter for early warning, paired with a backward trace-back module for entry attribution. Our analysis yields a two-scale deployment rule that balances longer event histories against broader agent coverage. Across LangGraph, CrewAI, and AutoGen, Agentic Sleeper drift begins 2.7--8.9$\times$ faster than natural baselines, propagates across agents on most topologies, and evades per-turn and stateful defenses above 80\%. Swarm Shepherd warns at least three sessions before visible degradation (2.6 sessions when failure is scored by the benchmark&#x27;s own task grader), while trace-back reaches 0.69 top-1 accuracy and 0.72 macro-F1 over 24 variants. The two-scale deployment rule is empirically confirmed, and in live end-to-end runs on LangGraph and AutoGen the posterior-guided defense stack drives cross-agent propagation below the self-sustaining regime, which removing only the visibly compromised agent does not achieve.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">代码生成语言模型产生构建和运行的用户界面,但页面在层次,间隔,排版和构成上往往比较薄弱. 我们研究一个小代码模型是否能够通过在可核查的合成环境中的强化学习来学习设计品味. 这种方法有三个组成部分。 第一,分层核查框架将设计质量分为硬门、制成的度量衡、标点判官、集团内相对偏好和一个盲目的专家小组。 一个信号只有在通过一个与专家测试协议,因果敏感性,对黑客陷阱的抵抗力和稳定性的录用协议后,才会进入奖励. 其次,在SCALER [1] 之后,合成设计环境是带有测量参数的生成器</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.MA</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Code-generating language models produce user interfaces that build and run, but the pages are often weak in hierarchy, spacing, typography and composition. We study whether a small code model can learn design taste through reinforcement learning in verifiable synthetic environments. The method has three components. First, a layered verification framework splits design quality into hard gates, render-grounded metrics, rubric judges, within-group relative preference and a blinded expert panel. A signal enters the reward only if it passes an admission protocol that tests agreement with experts, causal sensitivity, resistance to hack traps and stability. Second, following SCALER [1], synthetic design environments are parameterised generators with measured difficulty ladders: base pages are written once by an agentic coder, and instances are produced by syntax-tree perturbation operators and constraint samplers, scheduled by an adaptive curriculum with a quarantine rule against reward over-optimisation. Third, a 4B code model is trained with agentic reinforcement learning over multi-turn episodes in which it edits and builds a repository and reads a textual report of its own render, while hidden graders compute the reward. The admission protocol admits 7 of 16 render signals and 6 of 10 rubric dimensions. On TD-Bench, agentic training raises the Taste Index from 0.630 to 0.795 in-family and from 0.598 to 0.700 on held-out families, and wins 0.600 of blinded expert comparisons against the base model and 0.569 against a single-turn control.   <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19311" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">黑牛世界模型的宏观控制与能力评价:LLM-智能体市场双规框架</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Macro Control and Capability Evaluation of Black-Box World Models: A Two-Regime Framework for LLM-Agent Markets</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Zhiyang Niu、Chuanjie Li、Huayuan Shen、Mengmeng Zhao、Jiajia Zhang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大语言模型(LLM)智能体可以组织成多智能体市场,充当世界模型,将宏观信息映射到新兴的宏观动态,而评价者只观察宏观投入和产出. 本文将一个模型的能力分为一个单一汇总分数无法分出的两个阶段:信息模拟,从宏观信息到微观行为,以及宏观出现,从微观行为到宏观结果. 我们采用软宏观控制方法,日复一日地宣布一个公开的参考路径,而不触及单个的决定,命令,或匹配,以及一个单从宏观观测来衡量两个阶段的双机制评价框架. 在只引用的制度中,对称真实和翻转的引用</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language model (LLM) agents can be organized into multi-agent markets that act as world models, mapping macro information to emergent macro dynamics while the evaluator observes only macro inputs and outputs. This paper separates a model&#x27;s capability into two stages that a single aggregate score cannot separate: information simulation, from macro information to micro behavior, and macro emergence, from micro behavior to the macro outcome. We introduce a soft macro-control method that announces a public reference path day by day without touching individual decisions, orders, or matching, and a two-regime evaluation framework that measures both stages from macro observations alone. In the reference-only regime the symmetric true and flipped references share a common level R, which measures realization under the announced reference, while their paired gap B measures structural bias; in the information-injection regime external information dilutes the reference, so only the true-flipped gap Delta remains interpretable. On five single-index LLM-agent markets, announcing a target path shifts the realized path toward the announced target on both announced references under both information conditions. Relative to the no-reference path, the tracking score is higher by 0.306 and 0.415 without news and by 0.183 and 0.329 with news; within the stock/cash scan structural bias is lowest at 70/30 (B=0.042); realization peaks at a 3:7 technical share (R=0.894 versus 0.358 for technical-only); and Delta is 0.0341 with fundamental data and 0.0487 with news. Both stages use the same two observable scores, each read in the regime where it is interpretable.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Jackson Wang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大语言模型(LLM)贸易智能体商现在为其用户进行股票交易,基准根据智能体商的回报排名模型. 但单程的回程却对技能说得不多 在我们的研究中,只有随机种子的两种模式不同,平均结束5.2分,因此从运气中传授技能需要许多市场、模型和种子的反向测试。 然而,现场竞技场每天只交易一次,反选者要么使用模特训练停课后的几个月,要么在模特可能记得的年份奔跑,经常有消息按日历日期与价格结合. 在本文中,我们提出rta-bench-1,这是对美国三个市场(集市、熊市和坠机)的反向测试。 它只在发布一分钟后显示每一条标题,播放</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language model (LLM) trading agents now trade stocks for their users, and benchmarks rank models by their agents&#x27; returns. Yet a single run&#x27;s return says little about skill. In our study, two runs of the same model that differed only in the random seed ended 5.2 points apart on average, so telling skill from luck takes many backtests across markets, models and seeds. However, live arenas trade each day only once, and backtests either use the few months after a model&#x27;s training cutoff or run on years the model may remember, often with news joined to prices by calendar date. In this paper, we present rta-bench-1, a backtest over three US markets (a rally, a bear market and a crash). It shows each headline only after its publication minute, plays each period blind and named, and checks every run for look-ahead. It also splits each return into factor exposure and alpha and scores every decision against random choices of the same day and sector. In 91 runs of six models from four vendors, no alpha was significant, market and style exposure accounted for a median 78% of the return, and the decisions did no better than random stocks of the same sector. The agents bought the stocks with the most positive news, yet in ablations they earned as much without their headlines. Still, the agents carried out rules reliably, since a single cash rule in the prompt cut a GPT-based agent&#x27;s share in stocks by 30.3 points in all six pairs. Builders should therefore supply the edge themselves and report exposures and alpha, and benchmarks should replay news by publication time.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Standard neural architectures such as the Transformer were chosen because they train well with gradient descent and run fast on GPUs.   <p class="paper-original-title">Multi-Branch Transformer</p>

  <p class="paper-summary">Transformer语言模型通常通过使块变宽或堆积更深来缩放. 在这个作品中,我们探索了一条互补的道路:分支化,全Transformer块的平行组成. 我们引入了多块变换器(MBT),它通过共享初始层处理输入,同时执行相继块的独立参数化分支,并在共享最终层之前导出它们的输出. 在可比较的训练计算中,我们研究三个MBT的变体:宽-Shallow保存块宽度,窄-深保存顺序深度,以及它们之间的平衡插值. 在四个专家混合规模(0.8B至2.1B总参数)的受控前训练实验中,2个分支的宽锯齿MBT</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Transformer language models are commonly scaled by making their blocks wider or stacking them deeper. In this work, we explore a complementary path: branching, the parallel composition of full Transformer blocks. We introduce the Multi-Branch Transformer (MBT), which processes the input through shared initial layers, executes independently parameterized branches of sequential blocks in parallel, and fuses their outputs before shared final layers. At comparable training compute, we study three MBT variants: Wide-Shallow preserves block width, Narrow-Deep preserves sequential depth, and Balanced interpolates between them. In controlled pretraining experiments at four mixture-of-experts scales (0.8B to 2.1B total parameters), 2-branch Wide-Shallow MBTs consistently outperform sequential Transformers under matched parameter and training-compute budgets. Balanced MBTs improve further and retain comparable quality as the number of branches increases. To improve inference efficiency at long contexts, we introduce Joint-KV, which constructs a shared KV cache from branch representations at each depth. Our final recipe combines Balanced MBTs with Joint-KV, supported by an inference engine that exploits branch grouping and shared-cache reuse. Fitted scaling curves indicate that standard Transformers require up to 1.26x as much training body compute to match our final models&#x27; validation loss. These models reduce full-model KV-cache storage by up to 34% and deliver up to 51.4% higher decoding throughput on a single H200 GPU. Ablation studies show that branching yields gains beyond changes in width and depth, enables an effective KV-sharing strategy (Joint-KV), remains robust across fusion functions, and performs best in the middle layers. These findings show that branching offers a practical path for scaling language models toward both better modeling quality and faster inference.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">How to make a moral agent: sentimentalism and rationalism as lenses on AI</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">David Gottlieb、Jared Moore</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">AI系统能成为道德智能体吗? 也就是说,它们能否采取可被正确评为道德正确或错误的行动? 没有任何既定的道德权威规范标准可以完全适用于人类案例的范式之外。 由于在规范化账户方面存在困难,我们建议制定一个研究方案,探索描述性道德智能体。 基本上,我们问,我们人类拥有什么能力作为我们道德作用的基础? 为此,我们利用哲学和道德心理学方面的历史奖学金来孤立候选能力. 然后,我们建议改进候选能力,以便在人工智能系统范围内进行测试。 最后,我们提出了具体的经验性建议,以测试人工智能系统,特别是:</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Can AI systems be moral agents? That is, can they undertake actions that can be correctly assessed as morally right or wrong? There is no settled normative criterion of moral agency that applies neatly outside paradigm human cases. Because of the difficulties with a normative account, we instead propose a research program to explore descriptive moral agency. Most basically, we ask, what are the capabilities we humans possess that underlie our moral agency? To do so, we draw on historical scholarship in philosophy and moral psychology to isolate candidate capabilities. Then we suggest refining candidate capabilities so that they are testable in the context of AI systems. Finally, we offer specific empirical recommendations to test AI systems, especially large language models (LLMs), for those refined capabilities. Our approach offers several benefits. Firstly, it may demonstrate whether the behaviors of AI systems are aligned, or in a broad sense desirable. Secondly, the debate may inform policy questions (e.g., what models should be deployed in which contexts?). Thirdly, our approach may complement engineering and design efforts: when capabilities can be precisely characterized, they can inform the development of models. Fourthly, applying our moral reasoning concepts in the new context of AI systems should improve our understanding of those concepts.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Yifan Wang、Hao Ouyang、Qiuyu Wang、Yihao Meng、Wen Wang、Zeyue Tian 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">在电影故事叙述中,声音和视觉的转变并不总是在锁脚步中重合:对话经常通过跨剪切的桥梁来揭示反应或通过J-和L-剪切引入新的场景,同时故意沉默会加快场景的速度. 然而,现有的视听基因框架缺乏明确机制,无法在扩展的视野上协调独立的视觉和声学时间。 作为叙事持续时间和拍摄复杂性尺度,这些系统努力保持长距离对话的节奏,在意向的沉默期间经常出现幻觉的言论,或者错误地驱动沉默的听众的嘴动. 为了进行精细的电影控制,我们介绍了DECOUPAGE,一个由剧本驱动的框架,用于长形、多镜头和多特点的j</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>In cinematic storytelling, sound and visual transitions do not always coincide in lockstep: dialogue frequently bridges across cuts to reveal reactions or introduces new scenes via J- and L-cuts, while deliberate silences pace the scene. Existing audio-visual generative frameworks, however, lack explicit mechanisms to coordinate independent visual and acoustic timing over extended horizons. As narrative duration and shot complexity scale, these systems struggle to maintain long-range dialogue pacing, often hallucinating speech during intended silences or erroneously driving mouth movements on silent listeners. To enable fine-grained cinematic control, we present DECOUPAGE, a screenplay-driven framework for long-form, multi-shot, and multi-character joint audio-video generation. Rather than coupling modality boundaries by default, DECOUPAGE uncouples generation into independently bounded visual and acoustic tracks governed by persistent character identities. DECOUPAGE builds on a joint audio-video diffusion model with dual-schedule temporal cross-attention, incorporating a directorial Lip-sync mechanism that selectively decouples speech conditioning from visual facial dynamics during off-screen dialogue. On multi-shot conversational benchmarks, DECOUPAGE achieves precise control over shot transitions, audio timing, and visibility-aware lip synchronization, directing 15- to 60-second screenplay-driven audio-video generation with multimodal coherence.</p></details>
