  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Recent advances in machine learning have accelerated biomedical prediction, but evaluation on structured biomedical data remains fragmented, especially for datasets with high dimensionality, large sample size, missingness, and class imbalance. We introduce TabBioMed, a large-scale benchmark of 94 curated public biomedical tabular datasets spanning electronic health records, drug discovery, genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, single-cell omics, and systems biology. TabBioMed unifies these datasets through a standardized, dataset-aware preprocessing and evaluation framework, enabling reproducible comparison across classification, regression, and multi-target tasks. We benchmark 23 models across classical baselines, gradient-boosted trees, neural tabular architectures, and tabular foundation models. Foundation models deliver the strongest family-level predictive performance. However, their gains are regime-dependent: tree-based models and tuned multilayer perceptrons remain highly competitive, often dominating under inference-time or training-cost constraints. Overall, TabBioMed shows that biomedical tabular learning is strongly context-dependent, with optimal model choice depending on data regime, task type, and computational budget. We release TabBioMed with curated datasets and splits, preprocessing code, baselines, and results, providing a reproducible benchmark for practical model selection and future methods development in biomedical machine learning, available at https://github.com/AI-sandbox/TabBioMed.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Beyond the Sink Token: Sink-Guided Key Editing in Vision-Language Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Hui Zhu、Saleh Zare Zade、Rafi Ibn Sultan、Xiangyu Zhou、Dongxiao Zhu</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">在视觉语言模型(VLMs)中,少数沉淀符号吸引了不成比例的注意力. 先前的研究研究了这些标志为何会引起人们的注意,但我们调查了沉键方向如何影响对图像的注意并质疑标志. 在四个经探测的VLM中,提高正相汇成元件的体积平均会增加注意力,而负相汇成元件则会降低注意力;这些反应会持续到后几层。 这将本地汇键确定为控制超出自身位置的注意力的参考. 基于这一发现,我们提议采用SHRINK,这是一种无训练方法,在生成期间将不同可见键的相接部分地减少。 根据Sam的9个基准</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>In vision-language models (VLMs), a few sink tokens attract a disproportionate share of attention. While previous studies have examined why these tokens attract attention, we investigate how the sink-key direction affects attention to image and question tokens. Across four probed VLMs, boosting the magnitude of positive sink-aligned components increases attention on average, whereas boosting negative components decreases it; these responses persist through later layers. This identifies the native sink key as a reference for controlling attention beyond its own position. Motivated by this finding, we propose SHRINK, a training-free method that partially reduces sink-aligned components across visible keys during generation. On nine benchmarks under sampled reasoning, it improves mean accuracy by 5.1 and 2.7 percentage points on LLaVA-OneVision-7B and Qwen2.5-VL-3B, with smaller gains on the other two main models, Qwen2.5-VL-7B and Gemma-3-4B. Beyond SHRINK, rescaling sink-aligned key components can replace the attention operations of two existing attention-steering methods, GIFT and ACG, with comparable accuracy on hallucination benchmarks.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21373" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">WeVisForensics:Omni AI-Generated视频检测的自演智能体</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">WeVisForensics: Self-Evolving Agent for Omni AI-Generated Video Detection</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Yipeng Du、Yuxi Li、Ying Zhang、Chong Sun、Chen Li、Jing Lyu 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">录像制作方面最近的进展要求以明确的法医证据为基础进行可靠的探测。 然而,目前的方法主要取决于忽略简短或局部文物的单一思维链推理,或预先界定的多智能体工作流程,这些流程在选择相关检查时提供有限的灵活性。 即使进行了适应性调查,单靠优化调查策略并不能直接提高固定专家的法证能力. 我们介绍WeVisForensics,这是一个智能体框架,在这个框架里,一名主要调查员协调一个自行演变的法医检查员子智能体图书馆,这些智能体跨越视觉和音频提示。 调查员知道要检查哪类文物 在哪扇窗边</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Recent advances in video generation call for reliable detection grounded in explicit forensic evidence. However, current methods primarily depend on single-pass chain-of-thought reasoning that overlooks brief or localized artifacts, or on predefined multi-agent workflows that offer limited flexibility in selecting relevant inspections. Even with adaptive investigation, optimizing the investigation policy alone does not directly improve the forensic capabilities of fixed experts. We introduce WeVisForensics, an agentic framework in which a main investigator agent coordinates a self-evolving library of forensic inspector sub-agents spanning visual and audio cues. The investigator learns which artifact type to examine, over which window, and when to conclude, and these interactions provide shared feedback for both levels of agents. Our dual-timescale policy optimization strategy, DTPO, closes this loop through fast reinforcement learning of the investigator and slower refinement of the inspectors, guided by annotated artifact intervals, to report subtle artifacts they once dismissed. Furthermore, we curate ForenCue-30K, combining role-specific forensic annotations and interaction trajectories to supervise individual inspections and their coordination, with an accompanying reinforcement-learning pool. Experiments show that WeVisForensics averages 95.2% detection accuracy across in-domain, out-of-domain and agentic benchmarks, 9.0% above the strongest prior detector, while grounding its verdicts in localized evidence.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23591" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GRAFT: 图像化原型场,用于VLMs的现实化测试-时间适应</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">GRAFT: Graph-Regularized Prototype Fields for Realistic Test-Time Adaptation of VLMs</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Maxx Richard Rahman、Wolfgang Maass</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">未加标签的测试数据可以改进视觉语言模型的零发分级,然而转录和测试时间的适应方法往往会低于零发分级基线,因为一个批次中只有几个词汇类,或者当流相通时. 我们将此失败追溯到一个共同的设计:每个类都拥有从自己的样本中估计到的独立参数,因此没有的或稀有的类别被留在有偏见的文本锚地上或安装在外国样本上. 我们提议用图表规范化的Anchor-displacement Field Transference(GRAFT),将经过调整的原型作为文字锚地,加上通过Gaussian Markov 随机字段在语义类图上部分集合的放出字段,加上一个明确的类前置字段</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Unlabeled test data can improve the zero-shot classifier of a vision-language model, yet transductive and test-time adaptation methods often fall below the zero-shot baseline when only a few vocabulary classes are present in a batch or when streams are correlated. We trace this failure to a shared design: every class owns independent parameters estimated from its own samples, so absent or rare classes are left at biased text anchors or fitted to foreign samples. We propose Graph-Regularized Anchor-displacement Field Transduction (GRAFT), which models the adapted prototypes as text anchors plus a displacement field that is partially pooled over a semantic class graph through a Gaussian Markov random field prior, together with an explicit class-presence model. Inference is closed-form and gradient-free on cached features. Anchor-only shrinkage and a single global shift are the two extreme settings of the prior; absent classes are provably harmonic extensions of the present ones, and the pooling strength can be chosen by empirical Bayes. Under the realistic batch and online protocols on eleven benchmarks and four ImageNet shifts, GRAFT improves over the zero-shot model in every setting and over anchor-based transduction where few classes are present.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">QDA: Closed-Loop LLM Quantization Design with Agents</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Xingyang Li、Tianlang Zhao、Song Han、Ligeng Zhu</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">训练后量化是低成本为大语言模型服务的标准方式. 然而,最好的量化方法在模型和精度上有所不同。 因此,专家们必须重新设计每个新模式,这既费时又往往不够理想。 所幸的是,一个LLM智能体公司能够通过在建设和评价候选人的几轮中完善配方来接手这个设计,这样更多的轮子会导致更好的配方. 然而,评价一个被量化的推理模型需要数十个GPU-hours,结合不同代码库的方法意味着通过手将每个代码移植到新的架构上. 我们提出QDA,一个系统,一个智能体设计每个模型的配方和精度. 为了加快评价速度,QDA给每个候选人打分</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.LG</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Post-training quantization is the standard way to serve large language models at low cost. However, the best quantization recipe differs across models and precisions. As a result, experts must redesign it for every new model, which is time-consuming and often sub-optimal. Fortunately, an LLM agent can take over this design by refining the recipe over rounds of building and evaluating candidates, so that more rounds lead to better recipes. However, evaluating one quantized reasoning model takes tens of GPU-hours, and combining methods from different codebases means porting each to the new architecture by hand. We present QDA, a system in which an agent designs the recipe for each model and precision. To make evaluation fast, QDA scores each candidate in minutes by its output KL divergence from the original model on reasoning traces that the agent never sees, and checks promising ones with long generations to catch failures such as repetition loops. To make building fast, QDA provides a library of public PTQ methods that are compatible with one another and with new model architectures. Across four reasoning models, QDA improves accuracy over the strongest public recipe by 0.9, 1.4 and 2.2 percentage points on average at W4A16, W4A8 and W3A16, using 64 GPU-hours per search. On the 320B-parameter MoE model GLM-5.3-Flash at NVFP4 W4A4, it recovers 98.3% of the FP8 model&#x27;s accuracy, against 96.9% for the best vendor checkpoint. Every QDA checkpoint also runs on existing serving kernels with no inference overhead. We will release the library, the evaluator and the harness.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23483" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SpecLook: 寻找快而可靠的智能体 MLLMs</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">SpecLook: Speculating Where to Look for Fast and Reliable Agentic MLLMs</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Xi Ding、Jiawei Zhang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">智能体式多模态大语言模型(MLLMs),与图像相思与缩放工具调用相接的推理. 然而,这种相继循环缓慢,其答案没有可靠性分数. 之前对符号的猜测,推理步骤,或答案保持了循环,答案猜测也接受自信错误. 我们发现环大部分都增加了一个缩放步骤,一个小模型在大多数分出时领先于智能体. 我们的关键洞察力是推测智能体的下个观察,这与起草的答案不同,可以被验证,校正,用来测试答案. 我们提出SpecLook,一个没有训练的推测和验证框架,有三个层次. 一个小模型起草一个取景并重新回答,如果</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Agentic multimodal large language models (MLLMs) that think with images interleave reasoning with zoom-in tool calls. However, this sequential loop is slow, and its answers carry no reliability score. Prior speculation of tokens, reasoning steps, or answers keeps the loop, and answer speculation also accepts confident errors. We find that the loop mostly adds one zoom-in step, with which a small model is ahead of the agent on most splits. Our key insight is to speculate the agent&#x27;s next observation, which, unlike a drafted answer, can be verified, corrected, and used to test the answer. We propose SpecLook, a training-free speculate-and-verify framework with three levels. A small model drafts a glimpse and re-answers from it, and the question ends if the answer is unchanged and confident. Otherwise, the agent&#x27;s large model verifies and corrects the glimpse without tools, and their answers are fused. Only questions that still lack evidence reach the agent, in one forward pass that SpecLook-Fast skips, so no question runs the loop. The trace scores every answer&#x27;s reliability without labels. Extensive experiments on V*, HR-Bench, and POPE show that both are more accurate than every baseline on every split. SpecLook reaches 87.2% and 86.2% mean accuracy with 7.7x and 4.7x speedups over the DeepEyes-7B and Thyme-RL agents, and SpecLook-Fast reaches 9.9x and 6.3x.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23508" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">BOS摘要:第一个托肯事项</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">BOS Summary: The First Token Matters</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Hui Zhu、Saleh Zare Zade、Rafi Ibn Sultan、Deng Pan、Arun Ross、Dongxiao Zhu</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大型的视觉语言模型(LVLMs)和语言模型(LLMs)往往表现出注意的沉入,因此特定的位置,例如序列开始(BOS)的标志,得到的注意比例不成比例. 然而,在标准因果掩码下,BOS查询只关注自身,因此后期的表示再三访问时,不包含输入信息. 我们引入了BOS总结,一种无训练的推论-时间方法,它重用这种持久路径来进行输入。 在预填时的单个变压器层上,BOS摘要放松了仅BOS查询行的因果限制,让BOS将所有可视和文字输入符号汇总为自取上下文表示,而其他每个 t</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large vision-language models (LVLMs) and language models (LLMs) often exhibit attention sinks, whereby particular positions, such as the beginning-of-sequence (BOS) token, receive a disproportionate share of attention. Under standard causal masking, however, the BOS query attends only to itself, so the representation that later tokens repeatedly access carries no information about the input. We introduce BOS Summary, a training-free inference-time method that repurposes this persistent pathway to carry input context. At a single transformer layer during prefill, BOS Summary relaxes the causal constraint for the BOS query row alone, letting BOS aggregate all visual and textual input tokens into a self-derived context representation while every other token retains standard causal attention. The enriched representation then remains accessible to the model throughout decoding, with no additional parameters, auxiliary modules, or decoding-time overhead. Across multiple VLM and LLM families and eight multimodal and text-only benchmarks, BOS Summary improves over direct and Chain-of-Thought prompting and outperforms recent sink-intervention methods on multi-step reasoning tasks. A mechanistic analysis shows that making BOS input-specific also weakens its sink dominance and redistributes attention toward content-bearing prompt tokens, indicating that changing what a sink carries reshapes how later layers allocate attention.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23750" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Beyond Control:扩散语言模型的列文斯提因编辑</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Beyond Replacement: Levenshtein Editing for Diffusion Language Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Shuaidi Wang、Zhuocheng Gong、Xuejie Liu、Jianguo Li、Yu Zhang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">分流语言模型(DLMs)提供了自相递入模型的灵活替代,使得任意顺序的填入成为可能. 虽然大多数已存在的解码方法将已解码的活符视为固定的活符,但一些近期的模型只允许通过以替代活符来取而代之来修改先前已解码的活符,我们称之为只替换编辑(ROE)的范式. 虽然ROE可以校正单个的符号,但当许多位置出现出错时,由于缺失或额外符号而导致的序列变化和挣扎却效率低下. 为了克服这些限制,我们提议使用LED(传播语言模型的Evenshtein编辑),这是一种训练后框架,通过插入和删除扩展ROE,使Levenshtein风格的生成序列得以完善.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>cs.CL</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Diffusion language models (DLMs) offer a flexible alternative to autoregressive models, enabling any-order infilling. While most existing decoding methods treat decoded tokens as fixed, some recent models allow previously decoded tokens to be revised only by replacing them with alternative tokens, a paradigm we refer to as replacement-only editing (ROE). Although ROE can correct individual tokens, it is inefficient for sequence shifts caused by missing or extra tokens and struggles when errors occur across many positions. To address these limitations, we propose LED (Levenshtein Editing for Diffusion Language Models), a post-training framework that extends ROE with insertion and deletion, enabling Levenshtein-style refinement of generated sequences. It uses two training designs. First, it reconstructs edit supervision on the fly during supervised fine-tuning using longest-common-subsequence alignment, enabling insertion and deletion learning without manually annotated labels. Second, it adopts multi-stage training, where the state from one stage becomes the input to the next, exposing the model to its earlier errors and enabling it to correct them. When applied to the 16B-parameter LLaDA2.1-Mini Base, LED raises the average score across twelve benchmarks from 54.86 to 57.10 under confidence-guided decoding while increasing tokens per forward from 6.14 to 7.05, whereas under confidence-free decoding it raises the average score from 27.52 to 47.19. We further show that LED achieves higher pass@k at large k than thresholded decoding without editing, demonstrating improved generation diversity and revealing the diversity potential of DLMs without confidence guidance. LED also improves robustness to large block sizes, raising the average score from 36.97 to 51.34 at block size 128. Together, these results suggest that LED supports more robust self-correction in DLMs.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23484" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">3D 医疗视觉-语言的参考指导数据校正</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Reference-Guided Data Curation for 3D Medical Vision-Language Pretraining</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Faidra Patsatzi、Ashwin Kumar、Chong Wang、Curtis P. Langlotz、Reinhard Heckel、Akshay S. Chaudhari</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">各机构收集的大型医疗成像数据集的3D视觉语言模型训练受到数据质量的限制。 粗放,未精密的图像报告对子的特点是冗余,阶级不平衡,以及跨机构异质. 我们通过提出一个离线、无训练、参考指导的数据校正方法来解决数据校正改进3D VLM预训练的挑战。 我们的方法利用一个小的,高质量的参考集,独立抽取一个大型,未精准的集合的每一种方式的质量信号,并通过分级分级步骤将它们合并成一个单一的分级来指导样本选择. 我们在由35克立体CT体积报告对f组成的数据集上验证这种方法.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.CV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Training 3D vision-language models (VLMs) on large medical imaging datasets collected across institutions is constrained by data quality. Vast, uncurated pools of image-report pairs are characterized by redundancy, class imbalance, and cross-institutional heterogeneity. We address this challenge of data curation for improving 3D VLM pretraining by proposing an offline, training-free, reference-guided data curation recipe. Our approach leverages a small, high-quality reference set to independently extract quality signals for each modality of a large, uncurated pool and merges them through a rank-aggregation step into a single ranking that guides sample selection. We validate this method on datasets comprising 35k 3D abdominal CT volume-report pairs from two institutions and 58k 2D chest X-ray image-report pairs. On 3D CT, using our reference-guided data curation strategy, our curated model matches the zero-shot disease classification performance of a state-of-the-art baseline (Merlin) while using $3.3\times$ less training data and a reference set of 5k samples. On 2D chest X-rays, our method yields a curated model that matches or exceeds the zero-shot disease classification performance of a model trained on the full pool while using up to $10\times$ less training data and a reference set of only 74 samples. Ablations show that the selection is stable under resampling of the reference, that smaller reference sets still outperform random sampling, and that label rebalancing does not improve performance. To support reproducibility, we release our code.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Vision-language-action models (VLAs) encode images with 2D encoders that capture little 3D structure.   <p class="paper-authors">Pengxin Wang、Nakjung Choi、Jingdi Chen</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">无线电接入网络(RAN)切分是一个共享的资源分配问题:每个切片都试图以最小的无线电资源消耗来满足其服务级别协议(SLA),而所有切片则争夺有限的资源预算. 这造成苏丹解放军保证和资源效率之间的分歧,以及因共享资源而引起的分歧,要求实行专门而协调的控制。 在这项工作中,我们介绍了CoSlice,这是一个协调的多智能体强化学习框架,用于在开放无线电接入网络(O-RAN)中分配Slice物理资源块。 CoSlice 使用有条件的切片剂进行快速资源分配,并使用基于 LLM 的协调员来利用全球网络调整其目标</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Radio Access Network (RAN) slicing is a shared resource allocation problem: each slice seeks to satisfy its Service Level Agreement (SLA) with minimal radio-resource consumption, while all slices compete for a limited resource budget. We build a network slicing evaluation environment using ORANSlice and its radio-frequency simulator (RFsim), supporting realistic protocol-stack execution and application traffic across multiple slices. Experimental results show that CoSlice reduces both average and tail SLA violations compared with other centralized and multi-agent baselines under diverse slice workloads.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>An agent skill that succeeded yesterday may be executable today without being justified for reuse today.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>While multimodal language models can interpret diagrams and generate structured outputs, their capacity to connect passive radio-frequency (RF) design intent, layout geometry, and electromagnetic behavior remains largely untested.   <p class="paper-summary">自动智能体设计搜索可执行架构能改善基于LLM的智能体性能,其演化方法使用语言模型交叉来重接任务分解,模型协调和验证的有希望的机制. 然而,这一搜索通常仅以整体健身为导向,这掩盖了每个分数背后的例级成功和失败. 我们引入了&quot;执行指南&quot;的&quot;物剂设计基因重组&quot;(GRAFT),该&quot;物剂设计&quot;采用精选和生存的集合健身和亲子特定执行证据来指导语义与综合突变的交叉. 其反馈利用了成功和失败的限值样本的再利用,这些样本来自健身评价、拒绝参考答案和r</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Automated agent design searches for executable architectures that improve LLM-based agent performance, with evolutionary methods using language-model crossover to recombine promising mechanisms for task decomposition, model coordination, and verification.   <p class="paper-original-title">MANGO Arena: Same Seeds, Different Chains Evaluating LLM Agents in a Disrupted Supply Chain</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Sara Amar</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">我们介绍MANGO Arena,一个开源测试台,其中四个LLM智能体公司在系列供应链中经营一个公司,改编自MANGO,一个教室工厂管理游戏. 环境实施硬性规则(卡车限制、无信贷现金限制和生产能力),记录每一次被阻断的试图破坏这些规则的企图,并给特工们一个公开信息板,明确禁止附带付款。 这使得环境可以同时衡量三件事:经济结果(牛鞭效应),在压力下遵守规则,以及串通的建议. 试播集有3个小推理模型,2个脚本参考策略,2个条件(有和没有供应商起火)和3个需求种子,所有LLM智能体都放大了需求varia.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.MA</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>We introduce MANGO Arena, an open-source testbed in which four LLM agents each run one company in a serial supply chain adapted from MANGO, a classroom factory-management game. The environment enforces hard rules (a truck limit, a no-credit cash constraint and production capacity), logs every blocked attempt to break them, and gives agents a public message board on which side payments are explicitly forbidden. This lets one environment measure three things at once: an economic outcome (the bullwhip effect), rule adherence under pressure, and proposals to collude. In a pilot with three small reasoning models, two scripted reference policies, two conditions (with and without a supplier fire) and three demand seeds, all LLM agents amplified demand variability as it moved upstream, by 7 to 25 times at the Retailer and 13 to 179 times at the Supplier, often more than a textbook order-up-to policy. They did so largely by ordering in batches (zero orders in up to 60% of decisions). Real rule violations were rare (at most 7 of 288 decisions per model) and no agent proposed a money side deal. The pilot&#x27;s main lesson concerns evaluation method. API failures (rate limits and runaway hidden reasoning) entered the metrics silently as &quot;no order&quot; decisions. And when we reran games with the same seeds and no failed calls, upstream bullwhip changed by up to 17 times between runs. With three seeds, conclusions about how a disruption affects a given model did not survive a rerun. We report these failures in full and propose reporting practices for multi-agent evaluations of this kind. Code, prompts and every logged decision are public.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">CaDO: Formal Verification-Guided Multi-Agent Framework for Causal Dataset Repair and Generation</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Songli Yu、Qi Luo</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">高质量的因果推理数据集对于开发具有可靠因果理解的模型至关重要,特别是在专门领域。 然而,现有数据集经常受到不正确的说明和结构不平衡的影响,这可能导致偏颇的预测和有限的通缩. 为了解决这个问题,我们提出CaDO(Causal-Engine-Guided Multi-Agent Data Optimation),这是一个用于自动诊断和修复因果推理数据集的多智能体框架. 我们的框架包括三个关键阶段:正式的因果推理,多层次的诊断,以及自动化的数据修复和生成. 首先,一个形式化剂将自然语言问题翻译为正式的因果查询,以尽量减少语义偏差并制作</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>High-quality causal reasoning datasets are essential for developing models with reliable causal understanding, particularly in specialized domains. However, existing datasets frequently suffer from incorrect annotations and structural imbalances, which can lead to biased predictions and limited generalization. To address this issue, we propose CaDO (Causal-Engine-Guided Multi-Agent Data Optimization), a multi-agent framework for automated diagnosis and repair of causal reasoning datasets. Our framework consists of three key stages: formal causal reasoning, multi-level diagnosis, and automated data repair and generation. First, a Formalizer Agent translates natural language questions into formal causal queries to minimize semantic deviations and make retrieval and verification more explicit and tractable. Second, a dual-level diagnosis mechanism employs independent Critic Agents to identify sample-level errors while analyzing query-type coverage and label distributions to detect dataset-level structural imbalances. Finally, the framework employs reusable repair operators grounded in formal causal semantics to correct erroneous samples, while Explorer and Expander Agent collaboratively produce diverse and semantically consistent new samples. Experimental results show that CaDO significantly outperforms the strongest of five data cleaning baselines in 16 out of 18 experimental settings, improving downstream model accuracy by up to 9.1%. Moreover, at least 95% of the samples flagged by CaDO are defective, and on CLadder, CaDO incorrectly modifies 92.2% fewer correct labels than an LLM-based judge, demonstrating its effectiveness and reliability. Our code and data are publicly available at https://github.com/thisissyu/cado.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24059" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">重放,不要复出:长视RL专家策略外整合</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Replay, Don&#x27;t Resample: Off-Policy Consolidation of Long-Horizon RL Experts</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Zhenrui Yue、Gongxiaohui Chen、Huimin Zeng、Honglei Zhuang、Jiashuo Sun、Zhen Han 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">边疆后训练遵循一种共同的取向:每个领域训练一名专家进行强化学习(RL),然后通过多教师策略提炼(MOPD)将专家合并为一个模式。 这种整合费用昂贵:每个环境中的学生样本再次被采出,一个专家给每个被采出的信物评分,学生继承最终专家的漂移. 然而,我们争辩说,专家的RL运行已经产生了整合需要的东西:按专家从中吸取的顺序,以抽样概率和回报展开。 我们提议多专家重放策略提炼(MRPD), 在每一步骤上,策略梯度都重算于</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Frontier post-training follows a common recipe: train one expert per domain with reinforcement learning (RL), then consolidate the experts into one model via multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD). Such consolidation is expensive: the student samples in every environment again, an expert scores every sampled token, and the student inherits the drift of the final experts. Yet we argue that the experts&#x27; RL runs have already produced what consolidation needs: rollouts with their sampling probabilities and rewards, in the order the experts learned from them. We propose multi-expert replay policy distillation (MRPD), which replays these training logs through the student in the experts&#x27; order. At each step, the policy gradient is recomputed on these scored trajectories, and the final experts&#x27; distributions are distilled at the logged prefixes. Following the experts&#x27; own trajectory reduces the training-inference mismatch and keeps the student within the clip range, so the replay is off-policy in origin but quasi-on-policy throughout consolidation. MRPD samples nothing and calls no environment or reward model: the trainer is the sole process, and consolidating the experts costs only about a seventh of the compute used to train them. On three long-horizon agentic domains (reasoning, search and SQL), MRPD yields the best student model, substantially outperforming online RL baselines, and surpassing MOPD from the same experts at a fraction of MOPD&#x27;s cost. Our analysis on the same logs shows that the replayed trajectories carry most of the gain, and that both the policy gradient and the distillation term add to it. Most interestingly, the order of the logs matters more than their amount or rewards, suggesting that expert training logs are a curriculum, not a data mixture.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Training Language Models for Introspective Oversight</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Alex Lin Wang、Linus Yifeng Tang、Dami Choi、Neil Chowdhury</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">语言模型越来越多地展现出在笔录中难以发现的行为,如奖励黑客,欺骗和节奏等. 向助手询问有关其行为的后继问题是不可靠的,因为模型一般没有接受过报告自己不当行为的训练. 因此,监督办法通常涉及询问外部模式。 这些面相限制:外部记录片监测只运行在可见的模型输出上,活化解码器缺乏完整的上下文并容易被相容. 我们引入了内向监督,在模型中训练出一个新的角色来回答关于助理的任意问题,奖励那些根据助理的实际行为来核实的答案. 我们执行这个</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Language models increasingly exhibit behaviors that are difficult to detect in transcripts, such as reward hacking, deception, and sycophancy. Asking the assistant follow-up questions about its actions is unreliable, since models are generally not trained to report their own misbehavior. As a result, approaches to oversight typically involve querying external models. These face limitations: external transcript monitoring operates only on visible model outputs, and activation decoders lack full context and are prone to confabulation. We introduce introspective oversight, which trains a new role in the model to answer arbitrary questions about the assistant, rewarding answers that are verified against the assistant&#x27;s actual behavior.   <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Deep vision models operating in resource-constrained environments depend heavily on low-bit quantization, yet compressing weights below 4 bits frequently induces catastrophic accuracy collapse.   <p class="paper-summary">自主AI智能体将一个被冷冻的基底模型配对,并配有可编辑的诱因,技能,工具规则,和内存. 递减自修(RSI)从经验中适应这种没有重量更新的自修,然而没有外部分级器的自修往往反射出:反射和分数将编辑新打破的与未解的混为一谈,导致局部回归,技能被遗忘,以及内存膨胀. 我们引入了GUARD(GUED-free-Universed Environment Design[UED] with Adaptive Rules Distillation),这是一个用于无参考的连续拉带演化的闭路框架. 我们的关键见解是将每项任务分解为执行前自我检查,并通过定向遗憾指导利用更新。 每个回合的成绩轨迹对 T</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Autonomous AI agents pair a frozen base model with an editable harness of prompts, skills, tool rules, and memory. Recursive self-improvement (RSI) adapts this harness from experience without weight updates, yet self-editing without external graders often backfires: reflection and scalar scores conflate what an edit newly broke with what remains unsolved, causing local regressions, skill forgetting, and memory bloat. We introduce GUARD (Grader-free Unsupervised Environment Design [UED] with Adaptive Rule Distillation), a closed-loop framework for reference-free continual harness evolution. A Soft-HURT gate then promotes an edit only when gains outweigh regressions, scoping specialized fixes to prevent over-triggering and guaranteeing non-regression on its gated buffer. At test time, one frozen harness runs in a single pass. Across benchmarks on memory, skill evolution, continual task streams, and domain reasoning, GUARD consistently improves performance without catastrophic forgetting or memory pollution while substantially cutting deployment token usage.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23572" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">澄清多方智能体粮食援助</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Receiver-Conditioned Clarification for Multi-Agent Food Assistance</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Kaiwen Shi、Zheyuan Zhang、Changzhi Liu、Han Bao、Yanfang Ye</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">粮食无保障造成迫切需要指导,帮助家庭找到可用的粮食资源并根据其情况评估粮食选择。 这种指导必须把资格要求、实际获得限制和饮食需要联系起来,而不是退回孤立的清单或一般性营养建议。 多智能体食品助理可以协调这些任务,但不确定何时需要澄清或核实才能提出建议。 然而,智能体的交接可能会失去不确定性,使暂定解释能够作为事实传播,并模糊纠正的机会。 此外,对服务要求得到正确解释的信心不是对建议sa的信心。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>cs.AI</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Food insecurity creates a pressing need for guidance that helps households find usable food resources and assess food choices in light of their circumstances. Such guidance must connect eligibility requirements, practical access constraints, and dietary needs rather than return isolated listings or generic nutritional advice. Multi-agent food assistants can coordinate these tasks, with uncertainty indicating when clarification or verification is needed before a recommendation. However, uncertainty can be lost across agent handoffs, allowing tentative interpretations to propagate as facts and obscuring opportunities for correction. Moreover, confidence that a service requirement has been interpreted correctly is not confidence that a recommendation satisfies the user&#x27;s needs; local confidence cannot simply be reused as downstream reliability. We introduce an inference-time, receiver-conditioned clarification framework that preserves shared premise identities and replays consistent interpretations across dependent agents. Each receiver exposes admissible decisions and remaining evidence obligations, so question relevance reflects both decision changes and missing support. Information questions and user-confirmable fallback adjustments are evaluated together with their anticipated follow-up interactions under shared budgets. This structure connects clarification to jointly supported plans for resource navigation, nutritional reasoning, and supply-side planning.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">大语言模型(LLMs)被广泛用于回答问题,但容易产生幻觉,然而其根本原因,特别是实际流行程度在训练数据中的作用,仍未得到充分探讨。 在这部作品中,我们通过学术作者的归属来研究这个问题, 假设幻觉对于不太流行的论文来说更有可能发生. 以引用计数作为实际流行程度的代名词,我们整理了8个科学学科的192万篇论文的数据集,并按引用级别分级采集样本. 对三种最新LLMs的实验证实了我们的假设,揭示出所有模型和学科的幻觉率和引用数之间的强烈反向关系,幻觉率超过9个</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large Language Models (LLMs) are widely used for question answering but are prone to hallucinations, yet the underlying causes, particularly the role of factual prevalence in training data, remain underexplored.
