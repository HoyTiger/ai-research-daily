  <p class="paper-authors">Neel Mokaria、Rishie Raj、Dheeraj Baiju、Xiaoqian Shen、Shraman Pramanick、Kevin Qinghong Lin 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大语言模型(LLMs)的进步推动了对智能体的研究浪潮:理性、规划和行动的能力。 这一努力产生了一些智能体框架,这些框架围绕强大的LLM主干线协调感知、记忆和决策。 随着大型多模态模态的出现,这些系统可以处理和整合各种模态,包括图像、音频和视频,从而提高其现实世界的适用性。 然而,虽然对基于LLM的智能体进行了调查,但近年来没有系统地审查多模态在塑造智能体方面的作用。 这项调查通过分析多模态对智能体框架核心功能单元的影响来弥补差距:感知、推理、规划、mem</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>Agent</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Advances in large language models (LLMs) have fueled a wave of research into agency: the ability to reason, plan, and act. This effort has produced agentic frameworks that orchestrate perception, memory, and decision-making around powerful LLM backbones. With the advent of large multimodal models (LMMs), these systems can process and integrate diverse modalities, including images, audio, and video, thereby improving their real-world applicability. Yet, while surveys of LLM-based agents exist, the role of multimodality in shaping agency has not been systematically examined in recent years. This survey fills the gap by analyzing the impact of multimodality across the core functional modules of the agentic framework: perception, reasoning, planning, memory, and action. Using this lens, we trace the evolution from text-centric agents to multimodal frameworks, examine how modalities are integrated through delegated, late-fusion, and early-fusion architectures, and assess the emergence of agentic behaviors enabled by grounded perception and multimodal reasoning. We organize existing work through a modality-centric taxonomy that links architectural design choices to agent capabilities. Moreover, we review multimodal agentic systems across various application domains, including Robotics, GUI &amp; Web Navigation, Multimedia Content Generation &amp; Editing, and Long-form Video Understanding &amp; Retrieval. Beyond capabilities, we analyze performance across these settings and discuss efficiency-scalability trade-offs, including training and inference costs, latency, and deployment constraints. By focusing on the impact of multimodality in agentic design, we aim to identify key gaps and chart a roadmap toward robust and general-purpose intelligent systems.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">视觉基础模型(VFMs),如DINO和CLIP,在对图像的语义理解方面表现优异,但表现出有限的空间推理能力,这限制了其适用于所包含系统. 因此,最近的工作将一些3D任务(如深度估计)纳入了VFM训练. 然而,VFM的性能在其他空间任务中仍然不一致,提出了这些模型是否真正拥有空间意识或是否过于适合特定3D目标的问题. 为了解决这一问题,我们引入了空间关系识别任务(SpaRRTa)基准,该基准评价了VFMs识别可见物体之间的静态方向关系的能力. 不同于传统的3D目标,即侧重于精确的计量前</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>IJCV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Visual Foundation Models (VFMs), such as DINO and CLIP, excel in semantic understanding of images but exhibit limited spatial reasoning capabilities, which limits their applicability to embodied systems. As a result, recent work has incorporated some 3D tasks (such as depth estimation) into VFM training. However, VFM performance remains inconsistent across other spatial tasks, raising the question of whether these models truly possess spatial awareness or overfit to specific 3D objectives. To address this question, we introduce the Spatial Relation Recognition Task (SpaRRTa) benchmark, which evaluates the ability of VFMs to identify static directional relations between visible objects. Unlike traditional 3D objectives that focus on precise metric prediction (e.g., surface normal estimation), SpaRRTa probes a fundamental capability underpinning more advanced forms of human-like spatial understanding. SpaRRTa generates an arbitrary number of photorealistic images with diverse scenes and fully controllable object arrangements, along with freely accessible spatial annotations. Evaluating a range of state-of-the-art VFMs, we reveal significant disparities in their ability to encode directional spatial relations. Through our analysis, we provide insights into the mechanisms that support or hinder this form of spatial awareness in modern VFMs. We hope that SpaRRTa will serve as a useful tool for guiding the development of future spatially aware visual models. Our project page can be found at https://sparrta.gmum.net/ .</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">EgoX: Egocentric Cross-Embodiment Manipulation with Embodiment Dreaming</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Yaru Niu、Binghong Chen、Changwei Yao、Shangtao Li、Zhenlong Fang、Revanth Krishna Senthilkumaran 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">一般操纵策略可以从人类,真正的机器人,和模拟物剂的互补经验中获益. 然而,形态学,感知学,控制界面的差异使得难以从它们的综合演示中有效学习. 我们介绍了EgoX,这是一个以自我为中心的交叉表现学习框架,通过相应的观察和行动方式组织各种示威。 EgoX在这种混合物上预留了被模块化的相模异构Transformer并用相模异构(MXT+)来做相模异构(MXT+),并用相模异构的演示来进行微调. MXT+在每个表征中为每个观测模式指定一个专用的标致器,为每个行动模式指定一个专用专家,并配备一个Trans</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>Agent</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Generalist manipulation policies can benefit from the complementary experience of humans, real robots, and simulated agents. However, differences in morphology, sensing, and control interfaces make it difficult to learn effectively from their combined demonstrations. We introduce EgoX, an egocentric cross-embodiment learning framework that organizes heterogeneous demonstrations through semantically corresponding observation and action modalities. EgoX pretrains a Modularized Cross-Embodiment Transformer with Embodiment Dreaming (MXT+) on this mixture and fine-tunes it on target-embodiment demonstrations. Within each embodiment, MXT+ assigns a dedicated tokenizer to each observation modality and a dedicated expert to each action modality, with a Transformer trunk shared across embodiments. A training-only embodiment-dreaming objective predicts latent representations of future visual and proprioceptive states, encouraging the shared trunk to model each embodiment&#x27;s dynamics. Across 13 task-embodiment pairs spanning five tasks and six robot embodiments, including five physical robots and one simulated humanoid, cross-embodiment pretraining raises MXT+&#x27;s average normalized task progress from 0.63 to 0.80 over training from scratch. The gains are particularly pronounced under out-of-distribution conditions, where average task progress increases from 0.53 to 0.77. Ablations demonstrate the benefits of broader pretraining mixtures, modular interfaces, and embodiment dreaming, with dreaming producing more similar latent geometry across embodiments. Project page: egox-dream.github.io.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=RYoVZPIUoZ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">妥协智能体之外:分散式多智能体系统自发代表团攻击</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Beyond the Compromised Agent: Self-Routing Delegation Attacks in Decentralized Multi-Agent Systems</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">作者信息见原文</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">分散的多智能体系统建立在大语言模型上,通过当地交割进行协作的智能体之间分配能力。 我们显示,这种协作可以承担攻击者控制的任务,超出受损的特工本身的能力,而攻击者不知道目标的位置或计划随后的路线。 我们称这个过程为自发代表团攻击(SRDA), 我们把SRDA模拟为一个有限的范围的分解过程,分析智能体决定和协作的地貌如何攻击成功. 我们审查了两个机制:鼓励智能体继续下放权力的交接指南和下游反馈。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>检索与知识</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Decentralized multi-agent systems built on large language models distribute capabilities across agents that collaborate through local handoffs. We show that this collaboration can carry an attacker-controlled task beyond a compromised agent’s own capabilities, without the attacker knowing the target’s location or planning the subsequent route. We term this process a Self-Routing Delegation Attack (SRDA): downstream agents’ ordinary collaboration decisions carry the task toward a target capability. We model SRDA as a finite-horizon stochastic process to analyze how agent decisions and collaboration topology shape attack success. We examine two mechanisms: handoff guidance that encourages agents to continue delegating, and downstream feedback that enables further decisions after collaborators respond. Through a recursion for attack success probability, we derive sufficient conditions under which each mechanism increases attack success and their combination produces gains beyond the sum of their separate effects. Controlled experiments across six model configurations and 14 collaboration topologies show that delegation guidance and natural-language feedback jointly raise attack success by up to 26.24 percentage points over the baseline without either addition. In the tested topologies, deeper chains exhibit larger guidance gains, while greater branching strengthens the interaction between guidance and feedback. These findings show that restricting an individual agent’s capabilities does not necessarily contain the consequences of its compromise. Security evaluation must account for capabilities reachable through collaboration and whether task authorization is maintained across handoffs and recovery. Code and experimental results are available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/SRDA-C58C/.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-026-03118-1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">无灾难的开放世界MLLMs强力理性分解</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Robust Reasoning Segmentation for Open-World MLLMs without Catastrophic Forgetting</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Jiazhen Liu、Long Chen</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">确保开放世界视觉识别中的可信赖性和多功能性是多式大语言模型(MLLMs)的核心目标. 虽然整合分解是可取的,但现行方法需要侵入性微调,这往往导致灾难性地忘记模型的通用能力。 具体任务的业绩和一般可靠性之间的这种取舍从根本上破坏了建立统一、可信赖的系统的目标。 我们引入了LENS(L everage k E ypoi N ts for MLLMs&#x27; s section),这是一种新颖的插座和游戏范式,旨在为MLLMs配备像素级能力而不损害其基础完整性. LENS在完全被冻结的MLLM上加了轻量级头,解释内在</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>推理</span><span>IJCV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Ensuring trustworthiness and versatility in Open-World Visual Recognition is a core goal of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). While integrating segmentation is desirable, prevailing methods require invasive fine-tuning, which often leads to catastrophic forgetting of the model’s general-purpose capabilities. This trade-off between task-specific performance and general reliability fundamentally undermines the goal of building unified, trustworthy systems. We introduce LENS ( L everaging k E ypoi N ts for MLLMs’ S egmentation), a novel plug-and-play paradigm designed to equip MLLMs with pixel-level ability without compromising their foundational integrity. LENS attaches a lightweight head to a completely frozen MLLM, interpreting the intrinsic spatial cues embedded in attention maps. It extracts keypoints and translates them into prompt-compatible features for a mask decoder. Extensive experiments validate that LENS achieves segmentation performance competitive with retraining-based methods. Crucially, it largely preserves the MLLM’s open-world generalization, avoiding the degradation observed in fine-tuned counterparts. By decoupling segmentation from generation, LENS offers a robust and efficient path toward truly multi-talented and reliable vision models.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17334" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">解开多模态视觉编码</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Ellis Brown、Chris Hoang、Shengyi Qian、Shang-Wen Li、Mike Lewis、Marjan Ghazvininejad 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">多模态应如何编码视觉输入仍然有待辩论。 大多数模型都使用被预先训练的视觉编码器,而一些近期的食谱则用语言模型从头开始将编码器从头开始训练或者完全去除. 视觉编码器将三个设计选择组合在一起:从上游预训练开始的视觉,专用视觉能力,以及这种能力的预置(即在多模态主干线之前)。 因为已公布的模型在数据,架构,和计算上也有所不同,因此比较它们不能孤立每个选择的效果. 我们通过一个控制框架来解析这些选择, 将所有模型从零开始在共同数据上进行预演, 在视觉问题回答和语言基准上进行比较。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>How multimodal language models should encode visual input remains up for debate. Most models use a pretrained vision encoder, while some recent recipes train the encoder from scratch with the language model or remove it entirely. Vision encoders bundle together three design choices: a visual prior from upstream pretraining, dedicated vision capacity, and pre-fusion placement of that capacity (i.e., before the multimodal backbone). Because published models also differ in data, architecture, and compute, comparing them cannot isolate the effect of each choice. We disentangle these choices via a controlled framework where we pretrain all models from scratch on common data and compare them on visual question answering and language benchmarks at matched lifetime compute. We find that the visual prior is not necessary; an encoder trained jointly from scratch is on par with a pretrained one, without a separate training stage. What matters instead is dedicated vision capacity: an encoder-free model trails on visual understanding even when its backbone is enlarged by the encoder&#x27;s parameter count. Yet that capacity need not precede multimodal fusion, as vision-only modules added in-fusion to early backbone layers perform comparably to a pre-fusion encoder and can lower time to first token. These results also hold across backbone sizes, when encoder pretraining is treated as free, and when starting from a pretrained language model. Overall, our findings favor dedicated vision capacity trained jointly with the backbone and placed early within it.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">William Overman、Mohsen Bayati</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">马可夫的AI安全游戏 特工们学会何时推迟 人类学会何时监督 随着能力日益增强的物剂的部署,一个核心的安全挑战是如何在不改变基本系统的情况下保留有意义的人类控制。 我们研究一个最小的控制界面,其中智能体选择是自主行动(游戏)还是推迟(任务),而人同时选择是允许(信任)还是参与监督(overse),并将这种互动模式化为双玩家马尔可夫游戏. 当本游戏形成马尔可夫潜在游戏时,我们证明了一个对齐保证:从更自主的动作中增加智能体的效用,不能降低人的价值. 这确定了一种内在对齐的形式,即:</p>

  <h3><a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=ff4b0ELdU0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hista和Numca:对大语言模式强化学习进行有效评估的国家价值</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Hista and Numca: Estimate State Value Effectively for Large Language Model Reinforcement Learning</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Zizhe Chen、Jiqian Dong、Yizhou Tian、Garry YANG、Yongqiang Chen、Zhitang Chen 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">估计LLM RL强化学习(RL)的状态值通过奖励信号直接优化模型行为来完善大语言模型(LLM). 虽然准确的状态价值估计对于古典RL的稳定训练至关重要,但在LLM后的训练中,它仍然是一个未得到充分探讨的挑战。 在这项工作中,我们引入了国家价值估计基准(SVEB),以评估现有保护责任框架内的国家估计,并表明在PPO等标准方法中的批评者崩溃为粗糙的集团平均基线。 为了解决这个问题,我们提出了两种方法:\textit{Numca},利用数字跨度作为可分级的里程碑进行状态值估计,和\textit{Hista},一个框架,使用LLM的隐藏状态作为对w的表示.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>Reinforcement Learning</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Estimate state value for LLM RL Reinforcement learning (RL) refines large language models (LLMs) by directly optimizing model behavior through reward signals. While accurate state value estimation is critical for stable training in classical RL, it remains an underexplored challenge in LLM post‑training. In this work, we introduce the State Value Estimation Benchmark (SVEB) to assess state estimation within existing RL frameworks and show that critics in standard approaches like PPO collapse to a coarse group‑average baseline. To address this, we propose two techniques: \textit{Numca}, which leverages numerical spans as gradable milestones for state value estimation, and \textit{Hista}, a framework that uses LLM&#x27;s hidden states as representation to weighted average disjoint rollouts and their return. Extensive experiments demonstrate that both methods yield more accurate state value estimates and enhance training performance across different RL algorithms and model sizes without incurring significant computational overhead. Code available at \url{https://github.com/VOXXXX1874/Hista}.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-summary">多Hop知识图问答(KGQA)任务要求模型在KG中沿路径收集关系证据来回答自然语言问题. 然而,现有的KGQA系统一般侧重于预测最终答案,而没有明确的建模或验证中间推理步骤,模糊了正确答案是否出自忠实多跳推理. 为了解决这一限制,我们重新设定了多跳KGQA作为一个有问题条件的图导航问题. 我们把这种提法称为Tethus-可追踪的Hop-wise-证据Search在统一语义中。 在此设置中, 智能体接收一个 KG, 一个问题, 一个主题实体, 并且将一系列的关系 向着答案, 使推理路径</p>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Multi-Hop Knowledge Graph Question Answering (KGQA) tasks require models to assemble relational evidence along paths in a KG to answer natural-language questions. However, existing KGQA systems typically focus on predicting the final answer without explicitly modeling or validating the intermediate reasoning steps, obscuring whether the correct answers arise from faithful multi-hop reasoning.To address this limitation, we re-frame multi-hop KGQA as a question-conditioned graph navigation problem.We refer to this formulation as Theseus -- Traceable Hop-wise Evidence SEarch in a Unified Semantics.   <p class="paper-original-title">TRACE: A Two-Channel Robust Attribution Watermark via Complementary Embeddings for LLM-Agent Trajectories</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">作者信息见原文</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">LLM智能体通过转售商联系到用户,转售商可能重新品牌开发商的智能体商或者替换更便宜的模型. 当出处出现争议时,归属取决于轨迹日志(工具调用,观察和已执行动作的记录,而不是模型的推理),而再销售则存储并处理到仪表使用. 因此,水印必须在敌方完全读取/写入其从中检测到的证据后幸存下来;现有水印则不然,因为其归属直接从日志上读出。 我们介绍TRACE, 据我们所知,第一个在行动选择上无扭曲的水印,在删除时自动同步,在改写后无条件无变化。 删除去同步一个位置偏移</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>推理</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>LLM agents reach users through resellers, who may rebrand a developer&#x27;s agent or substitute a cheaper model. When provenance is disputed, attribution rests on the trajectory log (the record of tool calls, observations, and executed actions, not the model&#x27;s reasoning), which the reseller stores and processes to meter usage. A watermark must therefore survive an adversary with full read/write access to the very evidence it is detected from; existing agent watermarks do not, as their attribution is read straight off that log. We present TRACE, to our knowledge the first agent watermark that is distortion-free in its action choices, self-synchronizing under deletion, and unconditionally invariant under rewriting. Deletion desynchronizes a position-derived key and rewriting alters content, so a deletion-robust key must come from content and a rewrite-robust key from position, and no single key serves both. A trajectory, however, has room for two watermarks. TRACE superposes a selection channel that sets which action is chosen, keyed on local content with a distortion-free sampler, so the agent&#x27;s distribution is provably unchanged and detection resynchronizes after deletions, and a tally channel that sets how many records each decision group holds, keyed on the log&#x27;s skeleton alone, which no rewriting can touch. We prove this behavioral watermark&#x27;s signal is bought with decision entropy, each decision paying at least half its entropy and deterministic decisions nothing, and that erasing both channels forces the reseller to corrupt the trajectories it resells. On ToolBench and ALFWorld, TRACE matches the unwatermarked agent&#x27;s success rate while its selection channel reaches detection scores near z = 100 on long-horizon trajectories, stays detectable under 70% step deletion, and keeps a tally channel exactly unchanged under LLM rewriting of any strength.</p></details>

  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Recently, there has been growing interest in incorporating textual information into foundation models for time series forecasting. However, it remains unclear whether and under what conditions such multimodal text integration consistently yields gains. We systematically investigate these questions across a diverse benchmark of 16 forecasting tasks spanning 7 domains, including health, environment, and economics. We evaluate two popular multimodal forecasting paradigms: aligning-based methods, which align time series and text representations; and prompting-based methods, which directly prompt large language models for forecasting.   <p class="paper-original-title">Causal Dependency-Aware Unsupervised Routing for Large Reasoning Models</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Jiacheng Liu、Hao Liu、Xiaofeng Hou、Wei Xue、Yike Guo</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">我们提出一个不受监督的路由器,通过分析其思维过程与最终答案之间的因果关系来选择最可靠的推理模型. 随着大语言模型(LLM)生态系统的成长,向多样化集合中最合适的模型的路由查询已成为建设高效和高性能AI系统的关键策略. 一种常见的做法是训练一个受监督的路由器;然而,这需要大量、昂贵的人类附加说明的首选数据并创建出出名的简洁模型,在面对用户查询中不可避免的分布变化时无法概括. 因此,在不进行再训练的情况下,制定强有力的、不受监督的路线方法,是一个关键的研究前沿。 这个挑战很严重</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>Large Reasoning Model</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>We propose an unsupervised router that selects the most reliable reasoning models by analyzing the causal link between their thinking process and final answer. As Large Language Model (LLM) ecosystems grow, routing queries to the most suitable model in a diverse pool has become a critical strategy for building efficient and high-performing AI systems. A common approach is to train a supervised router; however, this requires vast, expensive human-annotated preference data and creates models that are notoriously brittle, failing to generalize when faced with inevitable distribution shifts in user queries. Consequently, developing robust, unsupervised routing methods that adapt without retraining is a crucial research frontier. This challenge is severely amplified by Large Reasoning Models (LRMs), which introduce a dual problem for any label-free method: their outputs have a causal thinking $\to$ answer structure that must be modeled, and a structural imbalance where long reasoning text can dominate the final answer signal. We introduce ReasoningRouter, a novel framework that resolves these issues with a length-balanced embedding strategy and a probabilistic model capturing the thinking-to-answer dependency. The proposed Causal Triangulation Property enables the label-free estimation of component qualities and their causal link. Beyond competitive routing accuracy, ReasoningRouter offers unprecedented insights into model behavior, enabling separate quality assessment of reasoning and answer components while maintaining computational efficiency.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">EDCO: Dynamic Curriculum Orchestration for Domain-specific Large Language Model Fine-tuning</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Jing-Cheng Pang、Sun Liu、Chang Zhou、Xian Tang、Haichuan Ma、KUN JIANG 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">我们提出一种动态课程学习方法,用于针对域的 LLM 微调,基于防止 en倒塌的想法. 域特异性大语言模型(LLMs),一般通过在专用数据集上微调预训练通用LLM而得到开发,是应用AI上的一大进步. LLM微调的一个共同策略是课程学习,与随机抽样策略相比,课程学习会根据诸如提高学习效率难度等度量标准预先订购训练样本. 然而,大多数LLM微调的现有方法都依赖于训练前设计的静态课程,在微调时缺乏适应模型不断发展的需要. 为了解决这个问题,我们提出EDCO, 一个小框架W</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>推理</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>Large language model</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>We propose a dynamic curriculum learning method for domain-specific LLM fine-tuning, based on the idea of preventing the entropy collapse. Domain-specific large language models (LLMs), typically developed by fine-tuning a pre-trained general-purpose LLM on specialized datasets, represent a significant advancement in applied AI. A common strategy in LLM fine-tuning is curriculum learning, which pre-orders training samples based on metrics like difficulty to improve learning efficiency compared to a random sampling strategy. However, most existing methods for LLM fine-tuning rely on a static curriculum, designed prior to training, which lacks adaptability to the model&#x27;s evolving needs during fine-tuning. To address this, we propose EDCO, a novel framework based on two key concepts: \textit{inference entropy} and \textit{dynamic curriculum orchestration}. Inspired by recent findings that maintaining high answer entropy benefits long-term reasoning gains, EDCO prioritizes samples with high inference entropy in a continuously adapted curriculum. EDCO integrates three core components: an efficient entropy estimator that uses prefix tokens to approximate full-sequence entropy, an entropy-based curriculum generator that selects data points with the highest inference entropy, and an LLM trainer that optimizes the model on the selected curriculum. Comprehensive experiments in communication, medicine and law domains, EDCO outperforms traditional curriculum strategies for fine-tuning Qwen3-4B and Llama3.2-3B models under supervised and reinforcement learning settings. Furthermore, the proposed efficient entropy estimation reduces computational time by 83.5\% while maintaining high accuracy. The code is available at https://github.com/GTS-AIData/EDCO.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">AA-SVD: Anchored and Adaptive SVD for Large Language Model Compression</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Atul Kumar Sinha、François Fleuret</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">我们引入了快速低级的因子化框架,用于压缩大语言模型,从而能够快速压缩亿个参数模型而无需端到端再训练:所有优化都是局部到单个变压器块并被一个小型的校正集所驱动. 与仅优化原始投入,忽视上游压缩的发行转移从而传播出错的现有基于要素化的方法,或仅依赖转移投入并有偏离原始产出之风险的方法不同,我们的方法兼顾两者。 除了单个地层压缩外,我们还将最小化扭曲的颗粒性作为自身设计选择,并将变压器块当作u.</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>We introduce a fast low-rank factorization-based framework for compressing large language models that enables rapid compression of billion-parameter models without end-to-end retraining: all optimization is local to a single transformer block and driven by a small calibration set. Unlike existing factorization-based approaches that optimize only on the original inputs, ignoring distribution shifts from upstream compression and thus propagating errors forward, or those that rely only on shifted inputs and risk drifting away from the original outputs, our approach accounts for both. Beyond individual layer compression, we treat the granularity at which distortion is minimized as a design choice in its own right, and take the transformer block as that unit: we refine each block end-to-end under the same anchored and adaptive objective, allowing compressed layers to jointly compensate for accumulated errors. By anchoring each compressed layer to the original outputs while explicitly modeling input distribution shifts, our method finds a low-rank approximation that maintains functional equivalence with the original model. Experiments on large language models show that our method consistently outperforms existing SVD-based baselines across compression ratios, with the advantage becoming increasingly pronounced at aggressive compression budgets, where competing methods degrade substantially or collapse entirely, offering a practical solution for efficient, large-scale model deployment.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=kTjSSqgqGf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">大语言模型智能体商并不总是忠实的自我变革者</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Large Language Model Agents Are Not Always Faithful Self-Evolvers</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Weixiang Zhao、Yingshuo Wang、Yichen Zhang、Yang Deng、Yanyan Zhao、Wanxiang Che 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">自发地发展出大语言模型(LLM)智能体通过积累和再利用过去的经验不断改善,然而仍然不清楚他们是否忠实地依靠这种经验来指导他们的行为. 我们提出对经验的忠诚性进行第一次系统的调查——即一个智能体的决定对它所获得的经验的因果依赖——自发的LLM智能体。 我们利用对原始和浓缩形式经验的可控因果干预,对13个LLM主干线和9个环境的四个代表性框架进行全面评价。 我们的分析发现了一个惊人的不对称现象:虽然智能体始终依赖于原始经验,但他们常常忽视或误解浓缩的经验,即使这是唯一的经验。</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>Self-Evolving Agent</span><span>Large Language Model</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Self-evolving large language model (LLM) agents continually improve by accumulating and reusing past experience, yet it remains unclear whether they faithfully rely on that experience to guide their behavior. We present the first systematic investigation of experience faithfulness—the causal dependence of an agent&#x27;s decisions on the experience it is given—in self-evolving LLM agents. Using controlled causal interventions on both raw and condensed forms of experience, we comprehensively evaluate four representative frameworks across 13 LLM backbones and 9 environments. Our analysis uncovers a striking asymmetry: while agents consistently depend on raw experience, they often disregard or misinterpret condensed experience, even when it is the only experience provided. This gap persists across single- and multi-agent configurations and across backbone scales. We trace its underlying causes to three factors: the semantic limitations of condensed content, internal processing biases that suppress experience, and task regimes where pretrained priors already suffice. These findings challenge prevailing assumptions about self-evolving methods and underscore the need for more faithful and reliable approaches to experience integration.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-026-03119-0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">高密文本至4D 生成的块- Wise 可变条件扩散</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Block-Wise Variable Conditional Diffusion for High-Fidelity Text-to-4D Generation</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Zhichao Zhang、Hui Chen、Jinsheng Deng、Ming Xu</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">文本到4D场景的生成目前受到三大挑战的阻碍:训练高维传播模型的令人望而却步的内存成本,文本条件中无法将静态物体的特性从动态运动中分解出来而产生的语义错位,以及准确连接主体,运动和环境的高质量数据集的稀缺. 为了克服这些挑战,我们提出了Swin4D,这是高效和高可靠性文本至4D合成的端到端框架. 首先,我们引入了Block-Windows可变条件分解策略,通过在训练中动态地再配置空间依赖性来缓解内存限制并避免过度调整. 第二,我们提议全球-地方对接</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>训练与系统</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>评测与安全</span><span>IJCV</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Text-to-4D scene generation is currently impeded by three primary challenges: the prohibitive memory cost of training high-dimensional diffusion models, the semantic misalignment arising from the failure to disentangle static object identity from dynamic motion in text conditioning, and the scarcity of high-quality datasets that accurately correlate subjects, motions, and environments. To surmount these challenges, we present Swin4D , an end-to-end framework for efficient and high-fidelity text-to-4D synthesis. First, we introduce a Block-wise Variable Conditional Diffusion strategy, which mitigates memory constraints and prevents overfitting by dynamically reconfiguring spatial dependencies during training. Second, we propose Global–Local Alignment , a hierarchical guidance mechanism that explicitly disentangles global scene context from local subject-action semantics via separate T5 and CLIP encoders, ensuring spatiotemporal consistency and fine-grained semantic fidelity regardless of prompt length. Finally, we address data limitations by curating RealSynth4D , a large-scale hybrid dataset combining 108k real-world captures with 72k AI-synthesised scenes, establishing robust correlations between subjects, actions, and complex backgrounds to significantly enhance model generalisation.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01328-3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">多模态细胞基础模型要求协同整合,而不是调整</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Multimodal cell foundation models require synergistic integration beyond alignment</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Till Richter、Eric Zimmermann、James Hall、Fabian J. Theis、Srivatsan Raghavan、Peter S. Winter 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">将跨模态和尺度的测量纳入细胞功能的统一定量框架是计算生物学的中心目标. 虽然存在着强大的单一模态基础模型,但大规模配对数据的缺乏限制了联合多模态训练。 这种制约有利于组成基础模型,这种模型通过学习到的界面使被冻结的单式专家融为一体。 然而,这种组合何时提供超出最强单式代表的任务相关信息,而不只是汇总冗余信号,仍不清楚。 我们在此介绍协同信息得分,这一衡量标准以部分信息分解为基础,它量化了仅从跨模态互动中获得的信息。 前任</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>多模态</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>Nature Machine Intelligence</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Integrating measurements across modalities and scales into unified quantitative frameworks of cellular function is a central goal in computational biology. Although powerful unimodal foundation models exist, the scarcity of large-scale paired data limits joint multimodal training. This constraint favours compositional foundation models, which fuse frozen unimodal experts through a learned interface. However, it remains unclear when such fusion provides task-relevant information beyond the strongest unimodal representation rather than merely aggregating redundant signal. Here we introduce Synergistic Information Score, a metric grounded in partial information decomposition that quantifies information gain arising only from cross-modal interactions. Extending theory from self-supervised learning, we show that standard alignment-based objectives with frozen encoders collapse to detect linear redundancies, which constrains their ability to capture nonlinear synergistic states. This distinction is especially important for tasks that connect tissue morphology with gene expression. Benchmarking ten fusion methods on spatial transcriptomics datasets, we demonstrate that tasks dominated by linear redundancies are well served by unimodal baselines, whereas complex niche definitions benefit from synergy-aware integration. Finally, we perform a scaling analysis and show that fine-tuning a dominant unimodal expert is the most sample-efficient strategy for standard tasks. Together, these results suggest that progress in multimodal modelling of cellular state requires shifting from alignment-based objectives to synergy-driven integration that exploits complementary cross-modal signal.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-original-title">Experience-Handoff Reinforcement Learning for Recursive Self-Improvement</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Doyoung Kim、Byeongguk Jeon、Kailin Zhang、Sherry Yang</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">常用的是从分级奖励中训练大语言模型(LLMs),用于强化学习(RL)的智能体任务. 然而,这却丢掉了特工在审判期间所经历的环境的精细信息(如出错日志). 为了利用这一丰富的信息,最近的作品将毒剂包裹在一种通过反射或收缩来完善自身投入的绳子上,并将过去的经验用于今后的推广,但移交的文物的建造(例如一本游戏本)本身并没有学到. 我们想要两个世界中最好的:从平分奖励中获得梯度更新,同时利用环境信息来传递经验。 作为混凝土测试床,我们固定 智能体背景工程 () 牵引和训练</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>Agent</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>It is common to train Large Language Models (LLMs) for agentic tasks with Reinforcement Learning (RL) from scalar rewards. However, this throws away the fine-grained information about the environment that the agent experienced during the trial (e.g., error logs). To utilize this rich information, recent works wrap the agent in a harness that refines its own inputs through reflection or compaction and hands past experience to future rollouts, but the construction of the handed-off artifacts (e.g., a playbook) is itself not learned. We want the best of both worlds: gradient updates from a scalar reward, while also utilizing environment information to hand off experience. As a concrete testbed, we fix the Agentic Context Engineering () harness and train one model to act, reflect, and curate task-local playbooks. Our method, , evaluates candidate experience-handoff artifacts by their downstream effect on future actor rollouts, and co-optimizes the role-specific behaviors (action generator, insight reflector, and playbook curator) under a fixed scaffold. Across agentic benchmarks, achieves higher held-out performance than baseline methods. The results suggest that agent training should optimize not only the actions in the current rollout, but also the model-written experience that future rollouts inherit.</p></details>

  <p class="paper-authors">Nikhil Sharma、Kelly Marchisio、Kenton Murray、Ziang Xiao</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">大语言模型(LLMs)越来越多地调解人们如何寻找和消费信息,包括不同语言和地区不同的说法,特别是在有争议的议题上. 支持寻求各种信息的模型需要帮助用户浏览各种语言和信息流之间相互竞争的叙述。 我们界定了寻求多种语文信息所需的6个能力:三个基本能力,用于叙述性包容、叙述性排斥和缺少叙述性识别;三个高级能力,用于提出各种叙述,在确认性询问中保留叙述性报道,并保持各语文之间的信息平等。 指导那些寻求</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>大模型</span><span>训练与系统</span><span>检索与知识</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Large language models (LLMs) increasingly mediate how people seek and consume information, including accounts that differ across languages and regions, especially on contested topics. Supporting diverse information seeking requires models to help users navigate competing narratives across languages and information streams. We define six capabilities necessary for diverse multilingual information seeking: three foundational capabilities for narrative inclusion, narrative exclusion, and missing narrative identification; and three higher-level capabilities for presenting diverse narratives, preserving narrative coverage under confirmatory queries, and maintaining information parity across languages. To guide the development of those information seeking capabilities, we introduce NarrativeBench, a benchmark comprising 1,333 news articles in 23 publication languages across 23 contested topics and 460 Reddit-grounded queries. Evaluating 14 LLMs across 24 query languages, we find that current models lack the capabilities needed to reliably navigate diverse narratives, limiting users&#x27; control over the perspectives they receive and leaving substantial gaps in exposure to competing accounts even when diversity is explicitly requested. Information access also differs across query framings and languages, risking echo chambers, linguistic filter bubbles, and information disparities that weaken the common ground needed for democratic discourse. NarrativeBench provides a foundation for developing information systems that give users across languages and regions agency to navigate diverse narratives.</p></details>

  <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17336" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">回像素:将Latent视频传播到像素空间</a></h3>
  <p class="paper-original-title">Back to Pixels: Lifting Latent Video Diffusion into Pixel Space</p>
  <p class="paper-authors">Yanke Song、Shuo Chen、Chris Rockwell、Guande He、Nan Liu、Desai Xie 等</p>
  <p class="paper-summary">边疆视频生成模型在VAE潜在空间工作,在空间和时间上压缩视频. 这种压缩使得训练可以进行,但现在限制了高分辨,可控,交互的生成——将分辨率翻倍的四分位符号计数,条件适用于各种帧而不是单个帧,每个响应必须等其多帧块的其余部分. 我们提出 PixVid,是一种简单而有效的方法,它可以提升一个预先训练出的潜在视频模型,直接生成像素空间中的每个帧. PixVid保留了扩散变压器,并用一个使输出成像素的回旋头来取代VAE解码器. 我们发现这预言 干净的视频而不是速度 加上更大的噪音</p>
  
  <div class="paper-tags"><span>多模态</span><span>生成与创造</span><span>community pick</span></div>
  <details><summary>查看原始摘要</summary><p>Frontier video generation models work in a VAE latent space that compresses video in both space and time. This compression made training tractable, but now limits high-resolution, controllable, and interactive generation -- doubling resolution quadruples token count, conditions apply to groups of frames rather than individual ones, and each response must wait for the rest of its multi-frame block. We present PixVid, a simple yet effective method that lifts a pretrained latent video model to directly generate every frame in pixel space. PixVid keeps the diffusion transformer and replaces the VAE decoder with a convolutional head that renders its outputs into pixels. We find that predicting clean video rather than velocity, together with a larger noise shift, is key to quality. Finetuned from Wan2.1-14B, PixVid is competitive with the latent baseline within sim 1.3M training clips. Working framewise in pixels, it generates at 2times higher resolution with the same per-frame token count, keeps depth-conditioned video editing aligned under fast motion, and responds to each action at the next frame. Project page: https://chenshuo20.github.io/PixVid/</p></details>
